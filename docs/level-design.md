# Level design

## Summary

The game has one map, with driving challenges placed across it. A challenge is a curved corridor with a width, which gives the player room for tolerance: the car counts as in the challenge while any part of it touches the corridor.

## Decisions

- A challenge is defined as a curved corridor with a width, so there is room for tolerance. (2026-10-03)
- The car counts as in the challenge as long as any part of it is touching the corridor. (2026-10-03)
