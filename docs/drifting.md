## Decided so far

- The drift curve (square root, linear or exponential) is deliberately undecided. Only the board can judge the feel, by playing it. Drift is not meant to be realistic.
- Settling the movement mechanics is one of the project's top priorities and the first thing to work on.
- Far-future idea: a trick system for the car (front flips, back flips, in the style of Olli Olli World).
