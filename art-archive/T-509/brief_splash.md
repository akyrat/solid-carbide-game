Brief for T-509: the rain's splash animation (4 frames in one row).

The board's words (2026-11-02, docs/level-design.md, Decisions, "Rain over the map"): it rains on the map during a run; small splashes where drops land on the ground and rooftops; the art is made with PixelLab and the board said not to be stingy with generations. The scene is night, Crash City: dark blue-grey wet streets and pale steel rooftops under amber and neon light, in a 3/4 isometric view (the ground is seen from above at an angle, so a splash is wider than it is tall). The current code-drawn placeholder is a few faint pixels and reads poorly.

What to make:
- ONE sheet, 4 frames of 12 x 8 pixels side by side (48 x 8 total), transparent background: one splash, played once in order. Frame 1: the impact, a small bright dot with a tiny ring. Frame 2: a crown, 2 to 4 droplets thrown up and out around a flat ellipse ring. Frame 3: the ring widest (about 10 pixels wide, 4 high, as a flat isometric ellipse) with 1 or 2 droplets still falling. Frame 4: the ring fading, broken, a few pixels left. The splash sits at the bottom centre of its frame (the impact point is the bottom middle pixel row, x 5 or 6); keep the four corners of each frame transparent.
- Colour: pale blue-white and light blue-grey (brightest pixels near white-blue for droplets, mid blue-grey for rings, a darker desaturated blue for the ring's far edge), a tiny hint of cyan is fine. Hard pixel edges, every pixel fully opaque or fully transparent (fade by using darker colours and fewer pixels, not transparency). At most 12 colours in all. An effect, so no outline. Not Greenbull's green or yellow. No glow blur.
- Night and retro feel (Visual style rules 1, 4, 5).

How to make it (Asset Generation Agent): PixelLab through game/tools/pixellab_client.py. PixelLab's smallest canvas is 32 x 32, so ask for drawings at 32 x 32 (create-image-pixflux, no_background true, isometric true) of a small water splash on the ground (one generation can be a sheet idea you crop from; one per frame is fine too), then crop to bounding boxes and place into the 12 x 8 frames by script (free; never scale an image). You may fix or redraw single pixels by script for the exact frame shapes. Count every call. Free script fixes first in a refine. Save each version as the loop asks (art-archive/T-509/style-loop/vN.png) with its PixelLab records; do not write into game/art/ (the loop copies the kept version).
