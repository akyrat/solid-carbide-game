Brief for T-509: the rain's raindrops (3 variants in one row).

The board's words (2026-11-02, docs/level-design.md, Decisions, "Rain over the map"): it rains on the map during a run; the art is made with PixelLab and the board said not to be stingy with generations. This is for light, steady rain drawn over the whole screen at night on Crash City's streets and rooftops (dark blue-grey streets and pale steel rooftops, amber and neon accents). The current code-drawn placeholder is a 1-pixel pale blue streak and is far too faint: the new drops must read clearly on both the dark streets and the lighter rooftops without hiding the car, enemies or HUD.

What to make:
- ONE sheet, 3 frames of 8 x 16 pixels side by side (24 x 16 total), transparent background. Each frame is a different drop variant (do NOT animate them): variant 1 a thin streak, variant 2 a slightly thicker and longer streak, variant 3 a short bright streak. Each is a slim slanted raindrop streak falling down the screen: the TOP leans to the RIGHT, the bottom toward the lower left, by about 18 degrees from vertical (about 5 pixels of sideways lean over 15 rows). Keep every drop clear of the frame's four corners (the corners must stay transparent) and inside the frame.
- Colour: cool pale blue-white rain (the lightest pixel near white-blue at the head, a mid blue-grey body, a darker, desaturated blue tail that fades), 1 to 2 pixels wide, a tiny hint of cyan is fine. Hard pixel edges, every pixel fully opaque or fully transparent (no semi-transparent fade: fade with darker colours instead). At most 12 colours in all. Rain is an effect, so there is no outline. Not Greenbull's green or yellow. No glow blur.
- Night and retro feel (Visual style rules 1, 4, 5).

How to make it (Asset Generation Agent): PixelLab through game/tools/pixellab_client.py. PixelLab's smallest canvas is 32 x 32, so ask for a drawing at 32 x 32 (create-image-pixflux, no_background true) of a single thin slanted raindrop streak, then crop it to its bounding box and place it into the 8 x 16 frame by script (free; crops and placing do not scale anything). If PixelLab will not draw a usable streak, one generation at 32 x 32 of a few streaks to crop three drops from is fine, or fix individual pixels by script. Never scale an image. The board's go: generations are not tightly capped for this feature, but count every call and write each one in the loop's record. Free script fixes first in a refine. Save each version as the loop asks (art-archive/T-509/style-loop/vN.png) with its PixelLab record; do not write into game/art/ (the loop copies the kept version).
