T-397, the parked car sample: one plain, boring parked city car at the top-right facing, for the level creator (Level design, Decisions, "Sidewalks and parked cars in the level creator"). Parked cars are solid obstacles like the boulders.

What it must be
- A normal, boring everyday city car (a compact sedan or hatchback), clearly not the player's car: no Mustang shape, no racing stripes, not dark green, no Greenbull colours or logo. Dull, ordinary paint in the city's palette (a pale silver-grey, an off-white or a muted grey-blue; Visual style, Decisions, "City colours"): never the boulders' navy, purple or magenta. Unlit lights, no glow. Light dust or grime at most; it is not a wreck (wrecks are a later ticket).
- Facing top right: its nose points up and to the right on screen, along the 2:1 isometric diagonal of the road tiles (a road along world y runs from the screen's lower left to its upper right).
- Isometric 3/4 view at the game's scale: 32 art px per unit (1 unit = the player car's width), drawn at 0.5. About 1 unit wide and 2.5 to 3 units long, so on screen about 110 to 130 px across, in a 128 x 128 frame like the player car's frames (`game/art/car/car_iso.png`, 128 x 128 frames).
- Rules 1 to 10 of Visual style, Content, "Rules": a dark selective outline, detailed shading, at most 30 colours, alpha 0 or 255, transparent background, no readable words, no real badges.
