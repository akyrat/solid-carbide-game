Piece: game/art/gift_pack/package.png (64 x 64), the gift pack package standing on the ground where T-144's helicopter dropped it. One of Greenbull's own things (Visual style, Decisions, "Greenbull's look": the weapons arrive as Greenbull gift packs; the package itself carries Greenbull's brand; rule 6).

How to make it (free script, no PixelLab: task T-304 says build_gift_package.py first; a recolour and restamp does it):
  python game/tools/build_gift_package.py piece package <the version's PNG path>
The script takes T-140's approved PixelLab gift box (art-archive/T-146/gift_box_from_T-140_raw.png, read only, never changed), recolours it with the level-up screen's mapping (build_level_up_art.recolour_box, T-303: red box to dark greens with Greenbull's dark green RGB 12, 76, 44 leading, gold ribbon and bow to light greens, small sparkles to yellow), scales it to about 40 px wide with its bottom on the canvas's bottom row (it stands on the landing spot), and stamps Greenbull's approved emblem (game/art/logo/greenbull_emblem_32.png, the bull's head) at 16 px wide, snapped to the emblem's own colours, on a pale tag on the box's right face, where the Redgull gull was. To refine, edit `box_sprite()`, `small_emblem()`, `stamp_logo()` or the colour constants in game/tools/build_gift_package.py (it may also use build_level_up_art's colours), keeping 64 x 64, the bottom row on the ground, the box about 40 px wide and no red. Never call PixelLab for this piece.

What it must be: a gift box in Greenbull's colours, mostly dark green with a light green ribbon, Greenbull's bull emblem and no gull, no Redgull red. Hard pixel edges, dark selective outline, detailed shading (from the PixelLab box), at most 30 colours, alpha 0 or 255 only, transparent background. In-world scale: drawn at 0.5 in the game, about 20 px on screen, the size of a small crate next to the car (Visual style rule 3); its 3/4 view shows the top and one side.
