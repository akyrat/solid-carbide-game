# Brief: the splash cover behind the main menu (T-433)

Make the game's cover: one full-screen pixel-art image shown behind the main menu (title "SOLID CARBIDE", a "(work in progress)" line and three buttons, Start, Garage, Exit, over a 35% black scrim). Visual style, Decisions, "The splash cover" is the source: very consistent with the game's style, its colours and the ACTUAL car; it does not have to match the game one to one and can be more cinematic. The composition is yours (the board: "I trust you").

Idea to start from (not a rule): the dark-green Mustang drifting in a wide arc round the kaiju on a night street in downtown Tokyo, meteors falling and bursting nearby, drift trails and tyre smoke behind the car. Keep the car's look exactly (rule 10): 1967 Mustang GT fastback, Greenbull dark green (12, 76, 44), two centred white stripes with a gap between them, 3/4 isometric view as in game/art/car/car_iso.png (use it as the reference, and as the init image or inpaint source if that helps the car stay true). The kaiju is Godzilla-style, never named, never an exact copy. Meteors are alien: dark rock, glowing cracks, navy, purple, magenta. The city is steel and glass: cool blue-grey, pale white and pale blue lit windows, small amber accents, small muted vertical signs with Japanese-like marks that are not words; light to medium damage only. Night, retro. Neon accents pink and cyan (drift trails, glow), glow drawn as stepped pixel halos, no blur.

Hard limits: no light green (104, 220, 96) or yellow (247, 208, 52) anywhere (Greenbull's only; the car's dark green is fine); no real brands, logos, badges or readable words; no text or title in the image (the menu draws the title); true pixel art at its final size, hard edges, no anti-aliasing.

Format: opaque, 16:9, 320 x 180 pixels (the menu crops it to fill and scales it up with hard pixel edges; at 1152 x 648 it is 3.6x). Keep the middle band (where the title and buttons sit) calmer and darker than the edges, so the title and buttons stay readable; put the car and the kaiju off-centre, to the left and right thirds.

For the Style Evaluator: this is an opaque full-screen cover, not a sprite. Rule 1's "transparent background" and its 30-colour limit do not apply to it (the script's "transparent background" check will fail by design; judge the colour count by whether the palette still feels limited and consistent). Judge consistency with the game's style, palette and car, and readability of a title and buttons over it.
