Piece: game/art/gift_pack/helicopter_sheet.png (768 x 160: 4 frames of 192 x 160 side by side, the rotor turned a step per frame), the helicopter that flies in and drops each Greenbull gift pack (T-144). One of Greenbull's own things (Visual style, Decisions, "Greenbull's look": the helicopter that drops each gift pack; rule 6).

How to make it (free script, no PixelLab: task T-305 says build_helicopter.py first; a recolour and restamp does it):
  python game/tools/build_helicopter.py piece <the version's PNG path>
The script takes T-145's board-approved PixelLab helicopter (art-archive/T-145/helicopter_sample.png, read only, never changed), mirrors it so it faces the way it flies, recolours the charcoal body to a dark green ramp led by Greenbull's dark green RGB 12, 76, 44 and the thin accent stripes to Greenbull's light green RGB 104, 220, 96, redraws the rotor blades turned a step per frame, and stamps Greenbull's approved emblem (game/art/logo/greenbull_emblem_32.png, the bull's head, snapped to the emblem's own colours) on cream panels on the fuselage side (18 px) and the tail fin (12 px), where the Redgull gull was. To refine, edit `RAMP`, `STRIPE`, `PANEL*`, `recolour()`, `emblem()`, `stamp()` or `LOGO_SPOTS` in game/tools/build_helicopter.py, keeping 4 frames of 192 x 160, at most 30 colours on the whole sheet and no red. Never call PixelLab for this piece. Do not run `build` (it writes the game file); use `piece`.

What it must be: a helicopter in Greenbull's colours, mostly dark green with light green trim, Greenbull's bull emblem on its logo spots, no gull, no Redgull red; yellow only as a small accent (the emblem's own). Hard pixel edges, dark selective outline, detailed shading (from the PixelLab sample), at most 30 colours on the whole sheet, alpha 0 or 255 only, transparent background. 3/4 isometric view from above, nose down-right. In-world scale: drawn at 0.5 in the game, a bit bigger than the car, flying above the street at night.
