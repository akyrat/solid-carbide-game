T-447, the shared button's `pressed` plate: the button held down: a sunk plate with the bevel reversed (dark top and left, light bottom and right) and a darker flat face; the game moves the text 2 px down.

What it is (HUD and menus, Decisions, "One button style across the game"; Visual style, Content, "Rules" and the HUD style sheet): a 9-slice button plate, 72 x 22 art pixels, shown at 2x with the text on top, for every button in the game (main menu, garage, tutorial, level-up, pause, recap). One of five states; the set must read as one button.

Style (the HUD style sheet's rules 1 to 4 and 7): hard pixels, alpha 0 or 255, at most 30 colours, a 1 px near-black outline (8, 8, 15), a stepped corner of radius 2 (pixels (0,0), (1,0), (0,1) cut), a 3 px steel bezel lit from the top left, two or three steps of flat shading, no gradients. Steel blue-greys only; no Greenbull colours; no red or cyan. 9-slice margins are 3 px on every side, so the middle must be one flat colour (it stretches).

How it is made (free, no PixelLab, never call pixellab_client): it is drawn by `game/tools/build_button_plates.py`. Version 1 is the script as it stands: `python game/tools/build_button_plates.py build --state pressed --out art-archive/T-447/style-loop/work/pressed_v1.png`.
