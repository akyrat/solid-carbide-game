# Brief: Rusty Overcoat icon (T-465)

A 32 x 32 pixel-art icon for the driving power-up "Rusty Overcoat" (a rusty armoured coat on the car that hurts enemies on contact). Shown at 64 x 64 on a power-up card.

Read docs/visual-style.md (Rules 1 to 6, 9) first.

Subject: a rusty, patched metal plate coat or armour jacket (like a scrap-metal overcoat), with rivets, rust patches and a few sharp spikes along its edge. One clear central symbol, readable at a glance.

Matched-set style (all four icons share this): 32 x 32, transparent background, hard pixel edges, a dark almost-black 1 px outline broken by lighter highlight pixels, detailed shading, light from the top-left, neon night palette (cool blue-grey, magenta/cyan/orange neon accents), at most 30 colours, glow drawn as stepped pixel halos inside the sprite. The icon fills about 28 x 28 pixels, centred. No Greenbull light green (104,220,96) or yellow (247,208,52), no text, no real brands.

Rust is orange-brown on blue-grey metal; keep the rust orange distinct from Greenbull's colours. A rule 6 red warning may be allowed with --allow-red if the rust reads as red.
