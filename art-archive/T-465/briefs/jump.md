# Brief: Jump icon (T-465)

A 32 x 32 pixel-art icon for the driving power-up "Jump" (the car makes a hop into the air). Shown at 64 x 64 on a power-up card.

Read docs/visual-style.md (Rules 1 to 6, 9) first.

Subject: a small car seen from the 3/4 isometric view, lifted off the ground, with a pad or spring and short upward motion lines under it and a small shadow on the ground below. One clear central symbol, readable at a glance. (Or an up-arrow on a spring if the car is unreadable at this size.)

Matched-set style (all four icons share this): 32 x 32, transparent background, hard pixel edges, a dark almost-black 1 px outline broken by lighter highlight pixels, detailed shading, light from the top-left, neon night palette (cool blue-grey, magenta/cyan/orange neon accents), at most 30 colours, glow drawn as stepped pixel halos inside the sprite. The icon fills about 28 x 28 pixels, centred. No Greenbull light green (104,220,96) or yellow (247,208,52), no text, no real brands.

Use cyan and magenta accents for this one (lift).
