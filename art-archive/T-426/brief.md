# Brief: the HUD size slider's label (T-426)

The pause screen gets a slider that makes the HUD (timer, health bar, viewers panel) bigger or smaller. Write its label: shown at the left of the slider on the pause strip, next to the labels "Camera zoom level", "Music volume" and "SFX volume".

Form: a short plain noun phrase, sentence case, no end punctuation, no story flavour (menu and settings labels are plain; Extended narrative, Text rules, rule 4). It reads as the sibling of those labels. The word "HUD" is the board's own word for it.

Candidate to start from: HUD size
