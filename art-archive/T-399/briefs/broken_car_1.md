T-399, broken car 1 for the level creator, at its four facings. The board: "a few different car assets that are just sitting parked, just normal, boring kind of cars. Generate some broken ones as well. And make it so that they sit at four rotations: top right, bottom right, bottom left, top left." This one: a wrecked, abandoned compact four-door sedan in faded dusty beige-grey: crushed, dented bonnet, shattered windows, a flat tyre, scorch marks and grime. Built from T-397's approved parked car (`art-archive/T-397/parked_car_ne.png`, 8/10, the board's OK 2026-10-30) in style and scale. A broken car is a wreck from the kaiju attack: light to medium damage (Visual style, Decisions, "City brief"): dents, broken glass, scorch marks, rust, a flat or missing tyre; no fire, no smoke, no gore. It must still read as a car.

What it must be
- The version is one row of four 128 x 128 frames (512 x 128), the car at its four facings in the order the creator keys them: north = top right (nose up and to the right on screen), east = bottom right, south = bottom left, west = top left. Each facing's nose must point the way its key says (the long axis of north and south runs along the road tiles' lower-left to upper-right diagonal; east and west along the other diagonal). The same car in all four, one palette.
- Isometric 3/4 view at the game's scale (32 art px per unit, 1 unit = the player car's width, drawn at 0.5), the same size as T-397's approved parked car (`art-archive/T-397/parked_car_ne.png`, about 110 px across), so it fits its 2 x 3 unit footprint. A solid obstacle in the creator, like the boulders.
- Clearly not the player's car: no Mustang shape, no stripes, never dark green or any of Greenbull's colours or logo. Dull paint in the city's colours (Visual style, Decisions, "City colours"); never the boulders' navy, purple or magenta. Unlit lights, no glow. No readable words, no real badges or brands.
- Rules 1 to 4, 6, 7, 9 of Visual style, Content, "Rules": a dark selective outline, detailed shading, at most 30 colours in the strip, alpha 0 or 255, a transparent background.

How it is made (PixelLab within the task's go: 3 generations for version 1; at most 6 more for all its refines together, then stop)
- Version 1, the top picture (1 generation): the request is ready, `art-archive/T-399/requests/broken_car_1_top_v1.json` (`create-image-pixflux`, 128 x 128, isometric, no background); name `broken_car_1_top_v1_raw`. Clean it to `art-archive/T-399/cars/broken_car_1_top_v1_raw_clean.png`.
- Version 1, the other end (2 generations, one call): the same car in 8 directions from the top picture, as T-101 made the player car's directions: `python game/tools/build_street_cars.py rotations-request --from art-archive/T-399/cars/broken_car_1_top_v1_raw_clean.png --description "a wrecked abandoned compact four-door sedan car, faded dusty beige-grey paint, crushed dented bonnet, shattered broken windows, a flat tyre, black scorch marks and grime, dents and scratches, unlit, no fire, no smoke, no stripes, isometric pixel art game sprite, same camera and same size in every direction" --out art-archive/T-399/requests/broken_car_1_rot8_v1.json`, then generate it with `--endpoint generate-8-rotations-v3`, name `broken_car_1_rot8_v1` (8 pictures, `broken_car_1_rot8_v1_0.png` to `broken_car_1_rot8_v1_7.png`). Look at all 8 and take the one whose nose points down the screen along a diagonal (bottom left or bottom right: headlights and front bumper towards the viewer, tail lights away) and that matches the top picture best in size and look; clean it: it is the bottom picture. (A /rotate by direction names was tried first for parked car 1 and turned the car the wrong way, so it is not used.)
- Make the strip (free): `python game/tools/build_street_cars.py strip --out <the version's PNG> --top-left <top picture, cleaned> --bottom-right <bottom picture, cleaned>`. Use `--top-right` instead of `--top-left` if the cleaned top picture faces top right, and `--bottom-left` instead of `--bottom-right` if the bottom one faces bottom left: look at each picture and name the way its nose really points (PixelLab's direction names are not reliable). The script mirrors the other two facings and gives all four one palette.
- After each version, write the preview: `python game/tools/build_street_cars.py preview --strip <the version's PNG> --out-dir art-archive/T-399/style-loop/broken_car_1_v<N>_preview` (the four facings parked on the road and on gravel next to the curbed sidewalk, a building and a boulder, at the game's anchors; dark and light, 3x: `preview_side_by_side.png`; `strip_4x.png` the strip enlarged; `footprints.png` the 2 x 3 footprints under the cars). Evaluator: the newest `art-archive/T-399/style-loop/broken_car_1_v*_preview/` folder shows the version in place; open it, and check each facing's nose against its key.
- Refines: free script fixes first (`clean` with or without `--mirror`, another of the 8 rotations as the bottom (or top) picture, which picture is named top/bottom left/right in `strip`, a palette or speck fix). Only when the reason needs a new picture: a new top picture is a new pixflux request `art-archive/T-399/requests/broken_car_1_top_v<N>.json` (1 generation, name `broken_car_1_top_v<N>_raw`); a new bottom picture is a new 8-rotations call from the kept top picture (2 generations, request `broken_car_1_rot8_v<N>.json`, name `broken_car_1_rot8_v<N>`). Keep the earlier request files. Keep within this car's generation budget above, never more; say every generation you made in `changed`.
- Every generation: `python game/tools/pixellab_client.py generate --endpoint <create-image-pixflux|generate-8-rotations-v3> --params-file <request> --out-dir art-archive/T-399/raw --name <name> --consulted docs/visual-style.md --consulted docs/level-design.md --consulted docs/extended-narrative.md --consulted art-archive/T-397/parked_car_ne.png --consulted game/art/ground/ground_atlas.png --consulted game/art/boulders/boulder_a.png --task T-399 --notes "<what it is>"`. Clean each picture you use (free): `python game/tools/build_street_cars.py clean --raw art-archive/T-399/raw/<name>.png --out art-archive/T-399/cars/<name>_clean.png`.
- Do not run `install` (the task's agent does it with the kept version). Write nothing outside `art-archive/T-399/` except the version PNG the loop asks for.
