T-399, broken car 2 for the level creator, at its four facings. The board: "a few different car assets that are just sitting parked, just normal, boring kind of cars. Generate some broken ones as well. And make it so that they sit at four rotations: top right, bottom right, bottom left, top left." This one: a wrecked, abandoned small hatchback in dull dark grey: roof caved in by falling rubble, broken glass, a missing wheel so it sits tilted, rust and scorch marks. Built the way parked car 1 was (`art-archive/T-399/parked_car_1_facings.png`), in T-397's style and scale (`art-archive/T-397/parked_car_ne.png`). A broken car is a wreck from the kaiju attack: light to medium damage (Visual style, Decisions, "City brief"): dents, broken glass, scorch marks, rust, a flat or missing tyre; no fire, no smoke, no gore. It must still read as a car.

The board's decision (2026-10-30, after seeing parked car 1's three sets of PixelLab 8 rotations, `art-archive/T-399/screenshots/car1_pixellab_8_rotations_x3.png`): "I really like the car rotations that I see. ... the only issue with the car is that both its headlights and its rear lights are red. Only its rear lights are supposed to be red, and its front lights ... gray because it turned off." So the facings come from PixelLab's 8 rotations, as the board accepted them (their angle, shading and proportions, and how the front end is drawn), and a free script repaints the front lamps grey. Evaluator: score these against the rules and the brief, and take the board's acceptance of PixelLab's rotations as given; red lamps at a car's front end, or a facing that does not match its key, are faults.

What it must be
- The version is one row of four 128 x 128 frames (512 x 128), the car at its four facings in the order the creator keys them: north = top right (nose up and to the right on screen), east = bottom right, south = bottom left, west = top left. Each facing's nose must point the way its key says (the long axis of north and south runs along the road tiles' lower-left to upper-right diagonal; east and west along the other diagonal). The same car in all four, one palette.
- Isometric 3/4 view at the game's scale (32 art px per unit, 1 unit = the player car's width, drawn at 0.5), the same size as T-397's approved parked car (`art-archive/T-397/parked_car_ne.png`, about 110 px across), so it fits its 2 x 3 unit footprint. A solid obstacle in the creator, like the boulders.
- Clearly not the player's car: no Mustang shape, no stripes, never dark green or any of Greenbull's colours or logo. Dull paint in the city's colours (Visual style, Decisions, "City colours"); never the boulders' navy, purple or magenta. Unlit lights, no glow. No readable words, no real badges or brands.
- Rules 1 to 4, 6, 7, 9 of Visual style, Content, "Rules": a dark selective outline, detailed shading, at most 30 colours in the strip, alpha 0 or 255, a transparent background.

How it is made (PixelLab within the task's go: 3 generations for version 1; at most 3 more for all its refines together, then stop)
- Version 1, the first picture (1 generation): the request is ready, `art-archive/T-399/requests/broken_car_2_top_v1.json` (`create-image-pixflux`, 128 x 128, isometric, no background); name `broken_car_2_top_v1_raw`. Clean it to `art-archive/T-399/cars/broken_car_2_top_v1_raw_clean.png` (`clean --raw art-archive/T-399/raw/broken_car_2_top_v1_raw.png --out art-archive/T-399/cars/broken_car_2_top_v1_raw_clean.png`).
- Version 1, the 8 rotations (2 generations, one call): `python game/tools/build_street_cars.py rotations-request --from art-archive/T-399/cars/broken_car_2_top_v1_raw_clean.png --description "a wrecked abandoned small hatchback car, dull dark grey paint, roof caved in and crushed by falling concrete rubble, broken glass, one wheel missing so it sits tilted, rust and black scorch marks, dents, unlit, no fire, no smoke, no stripes, isometric pixel art game sprite, same camera and same size in every direction" --out art-archive/T-399/requests/broken_car_2_rot8_v1.json`, then generate it with `--endpoint generate-8-rotations-v3`, name `broken_car_2_rot8_v1` (8 pictures, `art-archive/T-399/raw/broken_car_2_rot8_v1_0.png` to `_7.png`).
- Pick the facings from the 8 rotations (look at all 8): the **top picture** is the one whose nose points up the screen along a diagonal (top left or top right; its rear lamps red at the lower end), the **bottom picture** the one whose nose points down the screen along a diagonal (bottom left or bottom right: windscreen and bonnet at the lower end). Prefer the pair that look most like the same car at the game's 2:1 angle. Clean each: `python game/tools/build_street_cars.py clean --raw art-archive/T-399/raw/<name>.png --out art-archive/T-399/cars/<name>_clean.png`.
- Repaint the bottom picture's front lamps (free): `python game/tools/build_street_cars.py repaint-front --in art-archive/T-399/cars/<bottom>_clean.png --out art-archive/T-399/cars/<bottom>_front_grey.png --nose bottom-left` (or `--nose bottom-right`, the way its nose points). It turns the red lamps on the nose's side grey and leaves the rest. Check the result: no red left at the front, the rear lamps (on the top picture) still red. The originals stay in `art-archive/T-399/raw/` and `cars/`.
- Make the strip (free): `python game/tools/build_street_cars.py strip --out <the version's PNG> --top-left <top, cleaned> --bottom-left <bottom, repainted>` (use `--top-right` / `--bottom-right` for the way each nose really points). The script mirrors the other two facings (north = top right, east = bottom right, south = bottom left, west = top left) and gives all four one palette of at most 30 colours.
- After each version, write the preview: `python game/tools/build_street_cars.py preview --strip <the version's PNG> --out-dir art-archive/T-399/style-loop/broken_car_2_v<N>_preview` (`preview_side_by_side.png`: the four facings parked on the road and on gravel next to the curbed sidewalk, a building and a boulder, at the game's anchors, dark and light, 3x; `strip_4x.png` the strip enlarged; `footprints.png` the 2 x 3 footprints under the cars). Evaluator: the newest `art-archive/T-399/style-loop/broken_car_2_v*_preview/` folder shows the version in place; open it, and check each facing's nose against its key and that only the rear lamps are red.
