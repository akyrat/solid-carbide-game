T-399, the full sidewalk set for the level creator: the sidewalk tile with every curb piece the creator uses (47 tiles: straight curbs on any of the four edges, outer corners where two edges meet, inner corners where a road is only diagonal). Built from T-397's approved sample tile (`art-archive/T-397/sidewalk_curb_se.png`, 8/10, the board's OK 2026-10-30). The board decided curbs are drawn only: there is no height step in the game, the car drives over them.

What it must be
- The version is the whole sheet: `game/art/ground/sidewalk_tiles.png`'s layout, 1024 x 384, eight 128 x 64 tiles to a row (the road tiles' 2 x 2 unit diamond, 32 art px per unit, drawn at 0.5), transparent outside each diamond. Which tile is which: `game/art/ground/sidewalk_tiles.json` (edges n = the tile's upper-right edge, e = lower right, s = lower left, w = upper left; inner corners ne, se, sw, nw).
- The approved look on every tile: cool blue-grey slabs in the creator buildings' pavement colours (palette B), joints, speckle, light cracks, repeating every 2 units so tiles join with no seam. On each edge that meets a road, the curb: a lighter stone top, a lit lip broken by highlight pixels, and on the front edges (e, s) a darker face and a near-black gutter line against the asphalt; on the back edges (n, w) the face looks away, so only the top and a dark seam show.
- Curbs must join each other and the road tiles with no gap, notch or seam: along a straight, at outer corners (two edges meeting), and at inner corners (two sidewalks meeting round a road corner).
- Rules 1, 2, 3, 4, 7 and 10 of Visual style, Content, "Rules": at most 30 colours in the sheet, alpha 0 or 255, no Greenbull colours, not the boulders' navy, purple or magenta.

How it is made (free, no PixelLab for this piece)
- Version 1: `python game/tools/build_street_pieces.py sheet --out <the version's PNG>` (the sheet exactly as `build` draws it, from T-397's tile code).
- Refines change `draw_tile` (and its constants) in `game/tools/build_street_pieces.py` only; never change `game/tools/build_sidewalk_car_sample.py` (T-397's approved sample must stay reproducible). Then run the same `sheet` command for the new version.
- After each version, write the preview: `python game/tools/build_street_pieces.py preview-sidewalks --sheet <the version's PNG> --out-dir art-archive/T-399/style-loop/sidewalk_v<N>_preview` (the tiles laid round a crossroads of the game's road tiles, with gravel beyond, dark and light, 2x: `preview_side_by_side.png`; and 6x close-ups of the four corners of the crossing: `zoom_corner_n.png`, `zoom_corner_s.png`, `zoom_corner_e.png`, `zoom_corner_w.png`). Evaluator: the newest `art-archive/T-399/style-loop/sidewalk_v*_preview/` folder shows the version in place; open it.
- Do not run `build` (that writes the game's files; the task's agent does it with the kept version).
