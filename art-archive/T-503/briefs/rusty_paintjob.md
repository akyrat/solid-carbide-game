# Brief: Rusty Paintjob icon (T-503)

A 32 x 32 pixel-art icon for the driving power-up "Rusty Paintjob": a rusty-looking spray paint can. It replaces Rusty Overcoat's icon. Shown at 64 x 64 on a power-up card.

Read docs/visual-style.md (Rules 1 to 6, 9) first. Look at game/art/power_ups/ (the other icons) and match their size, outline and palette. The older rusty icon is in art-archive/T-503/old/.

Subject: one spray can, upright, nozzle on top with a small spray puff, body of blue-grey metal with orange-brown rust patches and flaking paint, a bright rim, small cyan and magenta neon glints. One clear central symbol.

Matched-set style: 32 x 32, transparent background, hard pixel edges, a dark almost-black 1 px outline broken by lighter highlight pixels, detailed shading, light from the top-left, neon night palette, at most 30 colours. The icon fills about 28 x 28 pixels, centred. No Greenbull light green (104,220,96) or yellow (247,208,52), no text, no real brands. Rust orange must stay distinct from Greenbull's colours; if it reads as red, use --allow-red.

PixelLab call (planned): create-image-pixflux, 32x32, no_background, single color black outline, detailed shading, highly detailed, text_guidance_scale 8, same settings as T-465.
