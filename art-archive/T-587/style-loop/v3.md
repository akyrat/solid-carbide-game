# T-587 Grinding Wheels icon, version 3

- **A free, script-drawn redraw, 0 PixelLab generations** (still 2 of 4 used). The Style Evaluator's reason for v2 (5/10) asked for geometry: a 3/4 ellipse tyre with tread, a simple lug hub instead of the star, a clear horizontal rail with a top-left highlight, a larger stepped spark spray with cyan or magenta accents, 26 colours or fewer. Free script fixes come before a paid regeneration, and all of these are drawable by script, as the HUD's and the buttons' art was (T-464, T-447).
- **Drawn by** `game/tools/build_grinding_wheels_icon.py` (tests: `test_build_grinding_wheels_icon.py`, 4 tests), deterministic:
  - the tyre: a face ellipse (centre 14,14, radii 9 x 9.6) in three blue-grey steps lit from the top-left (62,74,108 / 36,44,72 / 22,27,46) and a tread band to its right (the same ellipse shifted 4 px, in near-black 16,20,34 with lighter notches every 3 rows), so it reads as a wheel seen 3/4 from the side;
  - the hub: a steel ellipse (5 x 6) in three steps (196,226,232 / 130,160,190 / 84,100,128) with a dark ring, five lug nuts and a lit centre cap;
  - the rail: 30 x 5 px across the bottom (x 1 to 30, rows 24 to 28), a dark outline, a light top row with a pale top-left highlight edge (184,222,223 over the first 8 px and a break further right), a mid and a dark row; the tyre's outline sits on the rail's outline;
  - the sparks: 26 pixels fanning right and up from the tyre's rear contact point in five tiers (white 251,238,219; 255,158,46; 232,112,36; 150,70,26; 84,40,18); the white and bright ones get a stepped halo (two tiers darker) on their free 4-neighbours (rule 5); two cyan (90,220,240) and two magenta (235,80,200) accents;
  - the outline (6,5,10) all round the tyre and rail, broken by highlight pixels (112,133,189) on the tyre's top-left and the rail's top-left.
- **Tries:** `../work/v3_try1.png` had a lone pixel on the tyre's top row (radius 10 landing on a row) and a brown cloud of halo pixels; fixed by the 9.6 radius and by haloing only the two brightest tiers (`v3_try2.png`, identical to v3).
- **Script checks** (`../work/v3_checks.json`): all pass; 22 colours, 536 opaque pixels, alpha 0/255, 8-bit RGBA, no Greenbull colours; bbox 1,5 to 32,29 (31 x 24).
