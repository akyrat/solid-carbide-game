### The gift pack drop

Task T-144 (Game Loop Agent; Game loop architecture, Decisions, "Gift pack drop", with the board's five answers of 2026-10-16). A level-up no longer opens the level-up screen by itself. On every level-up (`RunState.level_up`, unchanged, so other level-up effects such as the burst and the likes bar keep firing at that moment) a helicopter flies across the screen and drops a package, which falls and lands a short way ahead of the car on open road. Driving over the landed package removes it and opens the level-up screen above for that package's level, with T-043's offers, pausing the run as before.

- **Code:** `scripts/run/gift_drops.gd` (`GiftDrops`, a pausable `Node2D`, `run.gift_drops`, node `GiftDrops`, drawn just before `View`). `bind(run)` is called by `run.gd`. It owns `helicopters` (in the air) and `packages` (carried, falling or landed, not yet picked up; `landed_packages()`). Signals: `helicopter_sent(helicopter)`, `package_dropped(package)`, `package_landed(package)`, `package_picked_up(package)`. Every frame `advance(delta)` moves the helicopters and falling packages, then `check_pickups()`. `pick_up(package)` removes it and calls `state.open_choice(package.level)`. A new run (`run_started`) clears everything (`clear()`); nothing moves or is picked up after the run ends, or while the tree is paused (pause screen, level-up screen). With `state.pause_on_level_up` on (tests only) no helicopter comes and the old direct flow runs. `land_all()` and `pick_up_all()` are shortcuts for tests and screenshot fixtures.
- **The flight:** `send_helicopter(level)` picks the landing spot (`landing_spot()`, below), then a path in the view's drawn pixels from `offscreen_margin_px` beyond the screen's left edge to the same beyond its right edge (`visible_rect()`, the camera's view at that moment). While it carries the package the helicopter flies `drop_height_px` above the landing spot (its centre), but never so high that its frame (`helicopter_top()`, read from its sprite) comes closer than `screen_margin_px` to the screen's top edge (`fly_y()`, worked out every frame, since the camera follows the car), so it is always fully on screen. The package hangs `hang_below_px` under the helicopter's centre (`carry_offset()`, its bottom; clear of the belly) until the helicopter is over the landing spot, then floats straight down under its parachute (T-146's falling look) at a steady speed for `fall_seconds` and lands. The fall starts where it hangs and is at least `min_fall_px` (starting a little higher, behind the helicopter, when it hangs too low), but never so high that the parachute's top (`art_height()`) is above the screen's top edge less `screen_margin_px`: zoomed in close, with the landing spot high on the screen, the fall is shorter, down to none, and the parachute still shows for `fall_seconds`. The helicopter flies on and is removed beyond the right edge. In the air (helicopter, carried and falling package) they are drawn over the whole world (`SKY_Z`); a landed package takes the depth order a car would have on that spot (`map.car_z_index`), under the car's drawing.
- **Where it lands (answer 2):** `land_ahead_units` ahead of the car, along the way it is moving (its nose when standing still), if that spot is open road: `map.is_open_spot(spot, clearance_units)` (new in `scripts/level/mvp_map.gd`: inside the map, no building or boulder within the clearance; `can_place_meteor_boulder()` now uses it, unchanged). Otherwise it tries other ways and distances, nearest to straight ahead first (`SEARCH_ANGLES`, `SEARCH_SHARES`, up to 1.5 times the distance), and falls back to the car's own spot.
- **Pickup:** a landed package is picked up when the car's centre comes within `pickup_radius_units` of the package's centre. Several packages (answer 3) each open their own screen; two picked up together show one screen after the other (`RunState.pending_choices`). Packages stay until picked up (answer 4).
- **Off-screen arrow (answer 5):** `scripts/run/gift_arrows.gd` (`GiftArrows`, a CanvasLayer at layer 11, over the HUD and under the level-up screen; `run.gift_drops.arrows`, node `GiftArrows`). For each package on the map (`GiftDrops.mapped_packages()`: falling from the moment it is dropped, and landed; not the one the helicopter still carries) whose centre is off screen it draws one arrow (a pale cyan triangle with a dark outline, placeholder look, no red) on a track 12 px inside the screen's edges, where the line from the screen's centre to the package crosses it, pointing at the package, slid along the track until it is 8 px clear of the HUD's parts (the timer, the health column, the coins row, and the likes bar with its row, fire and combo counter). T-314 (the board's decision of 2026-10-28: an arrow for every gift pack; none for a package on screen, the agents' proposal for the playtest): arrows that would overlap are slid apart along the track: they are placed in the packages' order and each keeps the 8 px gap clear of the ones placed before it, as it does of the HUD's parts. `shown` lists the arrows drawn; `GiftArrows.place(screen, target, avoid)` is the placement on its own.
- **Numbers** (`data/gift_pack.json` through `scripts/data/gift_pack_data.gd`, `GiftPackData`; proposals for the board's playtest, retuned for the final art in the playtest build): `helicopter.draw_scale` 0.65 (T-330: the helicopter 30% bigger, was 0.5; read through `GiftPackData.helicopter_scale()`, applied to `GiftHelicopter/Sprite` in `_ready` or by `send_helicopter`; the scene holds 0.65 too), `helicopter.speed_px_per_second` 360, `helicopter.drop_height_px` 200, `helicopter.screen_margin_px` 12, `helicopter.offscreen_margin_px` 80, `package.hang_below_px` 88 (T-330, was 80: the bigger belly reaches 37 px below the centre; `screen_margin_px` and `offscreen_margin_px` unchanged, as the top and the off-screen start are measured from the drawn size), `package.fall_seconds` 1.4, `package.min_fall_px` 60, `package.land_ahead_units` 8, `package.clearance_units` 1.0, `package.pickup_radius_units` 2.0. Drawn pixels are the isometric view's; units are the car's width.

### The pickup magnet (T-222)

`scripts/run/pickup_magnet.gd` (child "PickupMagnet" of the run, `run.pickup_magnet`): every physics step, coins (group `coins`), landed gift packs and health drops (group `health_pickups`) within `RunState.pickup_magnet_radius_units` of the car's centre fly straight at the car at `RunState.pickup_pull_speed` units per second. Both start from `data/run.json`'s `pickup_magnet` (`radius_units` 3.0, 1 car length: the car is drawn 3 units long; `pull_speed_units_per_second` 30, faster than the car's top speed so a pickup always catches up) through `RunData`, and are set again at every `RunState.start_run()`; the garage upgrade (T-224) sets the radius there: `RunState.start_run()` takes it from the `pickup_magnet` upgrade's bought level (`garage.json`, base 3.0 at level 0; +0.6 units per level to 9.0 at level 10; key `pickup_magnet`, text "Brand Magnet" in `garage_screen.gd`). Test: `tests/unit/run/test_magnet_upgrade.gd`. Picking up is unchanged (the coin's Area2D, the package's pickup radius); a pulled coin or drop is also collected once within 0.5 units of the car's centre. The reach is measured on the flat map (proposal: it ignores heights), and falling gift packs are not pulled, only landed ones. Test: `tests/unit/run/test_pickup_magnet.gd`; GIF fixture `tests/fixtures/pickup_magnet_gif_demo.tscn` (GIF in `art-archive/T-222/screenshots/`).
