# Rail grinding

(Task T-541, a prototype; the index is [../../README.md](../../README.md).) What it does and every proposed number: Drifting, Content, "Rail grinding (task T-541)". The rail format: Level design, Content, "Rails (task T-541)".

## The pieces

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `scripts/level/rails.gd` | Reads a level's `"rails"` list (`from_layout(layout)`), checks each entry (`problem(entry)`, "" when usable) and gives the geometry: `along`, `point_at`, `height_at`, `side_distance`. A rail is `{id, a, b, za, zb, length, dir}` (units; `dir` the unit vector a to b). Node-free. |
| `scripts/driving/rail_grind.gd` | The grind, node-free: `find(pos, vel, height, vz, heading, tilt_step)` (the rail a car in the air catches; since T-566 when the car's drawing crosses the bar's, see below), `start(info, heading)` (adds the landing boost `land_boost` to the speed and sets `boost_left`, T-548), `turn(steer, rate_deg, dt)` (A and D spin `body_heading` at the air turn rate, the board's tweak of 2026-11-06), `step(dt)` ("", "end" or "slow"; the speed loses `decel` a second, and the boost's part `boost_left` another `boost_decline` a second until it is gone), `stop(reason)`, `nose_side()` (+1 nose left of the rail line, -1 right, 0 along it), `launch_speed()` (T-578: the grind's `speed`, or `launch_min_speed` when that is higher), `leave_velocity(reason)` (a jump along the nose, `body_heading`, at `launch_speed()`, whatever the travel, T-548 and T-578; a slow drop pushed toward `nose_side()`), `touched_ground()`. Signals `grind_started(rail_index)` and `grind_ended(reason, seconds, length)`. Its numbers come from `data/rail_grind.json` (`load_numbers()`, with `DEFAULTS`). |
| `scripts/driving/car.gd` | `car.grind` (a RailGrind; `car.grind.rails` empty: nothing changes). `physics_step()`: while grinding, `_grind_step(steer)` does the step (no driving, no collisions, no height step) and returns; when the grind ends that step (`_leave_grind`), the normal step carries on from the rail (the fall, or the Jump's hop). After the normal step's height step, a car in the air is checked with `grind.find()`, and on the ground `grind.touched_ground()`. |
| `scripts/driving/car_height.gd` | `set_on_rail(h)` (on the bar: not airborne, no vertical speed) and `leave_rail(vz)` (into the air, `took_off` sent). The Jump's `hop()` works from the bar as from the ground. |
| `scripts/driving/jump_power_up.gd` | (T-551) No longer part of the grind: `hop_off_rail` is gone with the cooldown; the launch off a rail is `car._leave_grind("jump")` for any car. |
| `scripts/level/rail_drawing.gd` | The placeholder look (Level design, Content, "Rails (task T-541)"): `build(map, rails, height_at, depth_for)` adds the drawing to `map.view_root`, a shadow node right after the ground tiles and one node per piece (`PIECE_UNITS` long at most) with `base_z = depth_for(mid, rail angle, bar height) - 1`; `update_for_car(units, height, car_z)` lifts the pieces just in front of a car under the bar over it. Each node's transform undoes the view's projection, so it draws in screen pixels (`screen_of(units, height)`). |
| `scripts/driving/rail_sparks.gd` | The sparks, in the run's car view (child `RailSparks` of View). `step(car, dt)` each physics step. |
| `scripts/run/run.gd` | `_build_rails()` with every map: the rails to `car.grind`, the drawing (`rail_drawing`, with the elevation layer's `z_for` as its depth), and `car.grind.grind_ended` to `state.add_grind_trick(seconds)`. `_on_car_rails_step()` after each car step: the sparks and `update_for_car`. |
| `scripts/run/run_state.gd` | `add_grind_trick(seconds)`: the combo up by `ComboData.grind_add(seconds)`, `combo_trick("rail_grind", added, multiplier)` (the GRIND! popup, `trick_popups.gd` TEXTS), the subs `ComboData.grind_subs(seconds)` times the multiplier before (`trick_subs_paid`). `jump_at_start()`: the playtest switch. |
| `rail_grind.gd` joints (T-542 round 2) | `joint_at_end()` finds the connected rail past the end the car leaves by (`join_distance`, `join_height`, `join_max_angle_deg`); `step()` crosses to it (`_cross_joint`, counted in `joints`) with the speed untouched and the rest of the step carried along it; `rail`, `rail_index`, `direction` and `s` switch to the new piece. |
| `data/rail_grind.json` | The grind's numbers (proposals; each explained in its `_comment` lines): `min_start_speed`, `square_start_speed` (T-566), `decel`, `end_speed`, `drop_side_speed`, `land_boost`, `boost_decline`, `launch_min_speed` (T-578; it replaced T-548's `jump_speed`; `jump_angle_deg` is gone), `join_distance`, `join_height`, `join_max_angle_deg` (`reach`, `end_margin`, `catch_above`, `catch_below` and `max_angle_deg` are gone since T-566). |
| `data/combo.json` | `rail_grind`: `min_seconds`, `add_per_second`, `max_add`, `subs_per_second`, `max_subs`. |
| `data/playtest.json` | `jump_at_start`: the Jump tier every run starts with (0, off, on master). Tests override it with `RunState.jump_at_start_override`. |

The trick detector (`trick_detector.gd`) is not changed: while grinding the car is not airborne and not drifting, so a landing on a rail pays the air tricks and the airtime as any landing, and the grind's own trick is paid by the run from `grind_ended`.

## The grind overhaul (T-551)

What it does and every proposed number: Drifting, Content, "The grind overhaul (task T-551)". Tests: `tests/unit/test_grind_overhaul.gd` (the tilt, the drawing's hook, the jump effect, the arrow and its setting) and `tests/unit/test_rail_grind.gd` (start only while held, hold, launch on release, backwards, the rail's end while held, the launch's takeoff, no power-up needed).

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `scripts/driving/car.gd` | Reads Space held each step (`Input.is_action_pressed("car_jump")`, or `scripted_jump_held` when `use_keyboard` is false; dead keys read false) into `jump_held`. `grind.find()` only while it is held in the air. `_grind_step(steer, held)`: not held ends the grind with reason "jump", the launch: `_leave_grind` sets the leaving velocity (`RailGrind.leave_velocity("jump")`: the nose at `launch_speed()`, T-578) and `height_model.hop(launch_takeoff_speed())` (`rail_grind.json` `launch_takeoff_speed`, or the owned Jump tier's takeoff if higher). `tilt.update(held and (airborne or grinding), dt)` every step. Signal `jump_started(kind, at_units, at_height)`: "hop" (from `jump.jumped`) and "rail_launch". |
| `scripts/driving/car_tilt.gd` | The tilt, node-free: `update(tilted, dt)`, `step` 0 (normal) to `FRAMES` 3 (held), one frame every `tilt_frame_seconds`, the first at once; `amount()`, `is_tilted()`, `reset()`. |
| `scripts/driving/car_sprite.gd` | The hook `set_tilt_step(step)`; `tilt_frame_index(dir_frame, step, columns)`. With `art/car/car_iso_tilt.png` and `.json` present (`TILT_SHEETS`; T-555: 3 rows, one per step, one column per direction, the car's frame size and centre, shadowless) it draws steps 1 to 3 from it; otherwise the placeholder: `skew` and a vertical squash (`TILT_PLACEHOLDER_SKEW`, `TILT_PLACEHOLDER_SQUASH`) times step / 3. The run calls it in `sync_view()` with `car.tilt.step`. |
| `scripts/driving/jump_effect.gd` | The jump effect, child "JumpEffect" of the run's View, over the car: `bind(car)` listens to `jump_started` and plays `JumpEffectChoice.equipped_id()` at the place and height the car left from, for `jump_effect_seconds`. `static draw_placeholder(ci, id, t, origin, basis, heading, height_px)` draws one id's placeholder at progress t on any CanvasItem (the Cosmetics menu's preview can use it); T-556 swaps its art in there. |
| `scripts/driving/jump_effect_choice.gd` | The seam to the saved cosmetic: `equipped_id()` is `override_id` (tests), else `provider.call()` (T-552 sets `JumpEffectChoice.provider` to a Callable returning its API's `equipped_jump_effect()`), else `DEFAULT_ID` "dash_cloud"; an unknown id falls back to it. `IDS` lists the five. |
| `scripts/driving/facing_arrow.gd` | The jump arrow, child "FacingArrow" of the run's View, over the car: shown while `car.grind.active` with `GameSettings.load_jump_arrow_enabled()` (default on); hears `GameSettings.notifier.jump_arrow_enabled_changed`. `points_units(center, heading)` gives the arrowhead. Numbers `arrow_*` in `rail_grind.json`. |
| `scripts/ui/game_settings.gd` | `load_jump_arrow_enabled()`, `save_jump_arrow_enabled(on)` (section "graphics", key `jump_arrow_enabled`, `DEFAULT_JUMP_ARROW_ENABLED` true); T-553's checkbox calls the save. |

## The rail snap on the drawing (T-566)

What it does and why: Drifting, Content, "The rail snap on the drawing (task T-566)".

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `scripts/driving/rail_grind.gd` | `find(pos, vel, height, _vz, heading = NAN, tilt_step = 3)`: for each rail (not `exclude`; any angle, any flat speed, the board's answer of 2026-11-07), the bar's ends on screen (`CarOutline.screen_of`) and `CarOutline.touches_segment(...)` with `BAR_HALF_PX` (1.5, the bar's 3 rows); the bar nearest the car's drawn centre on screen wins (`screen_gap`); `s` from `snap_t()`, the point of the screen segment nearest that centre (a rail's screen point is linear in `s`); `speed_along` from `start_speed_along(vel, heading, dir)`: the car's own speed along the rail when it is `min_start_speed` or more, otherwise `square_start_speed` (6, `rail_grind.json`) the way the nose points along it (the velocity's way when the nose is exactly square, end b when both are); `start()` adds the landing boost as for any catch. `heading` NAN: the velocity's direction. `vz` is no longer used (kept so callers don't change). |
| `scripts/driving/car_outline.gd` | Node-free, static: `screen_of(u, h)` (the map view's projection, the same as `rail_drawing.gd`'s), `data()` (reads `art/car/car_iso_outline.json` once), `frame_for(rotation)` (as `car_sprite.gd` `frame_for_rotation`), `spans(rotation, tilt_step)`, `rects(at, rotation, tilt_step)` (one screen rectangle per pixel row of the drawing; 0.5 screen px per frame px, the centre pixel on `at`), `bounds(...)`, `touches_segment(at, rotation, tilt_step, a, b, half_width)` (the bounding box first, then the rows) and `segment_hits_rect(a, b, rect)` (Liang-Barsky). Tilt step 0 is the shadowless sheet ("air"), 1 to 3 the tilt sheet's rows ("tilt1" to "tilt3"). |
| `scripts/driving/car.gd` | Passes `model.rotation` and `tilt.step` (the frame drawn this step) to `grind.find()`. |
| `art/car/car_iso_outline.json` | Per sheet key and direction frame, `[row, left, right, ...]`: each pixel row's leftmost and rightmost pixel with alpha 128 or more, in frame pixels; with the frame size, centre pixel and `px_per_unit` of the sheets. |
| `tools/build_car_outline.py` | Builds that file from `car_iso_noshadow.png` and `car_iso_tilt.png`; `--check` exits 1 when it is out of date. Run it again whenever those sheets change. |

Tests: `tests/unit/test_rail_snap_drawing.gd` (catching at heights from 5 under to 8 over the bar, and not 3 units' worth beside; a car falling from 6 over and one hopping from 5 under snap and grind at the bar's height; the drawing moves at most half its height at the snap; no snap without Space or when the drawings never cross; a square-on crossing grinds the way the nose points at `square_start_speed` plus the boost, east or west, and goes on sliding; a car crossing tail first goes where its nose points; `start_speed_along()`'s cases; a standing hop under a bar catches; a rail through the frame's empty corner isn't caught; the nearest bar on screen wins; the projection equals `rail_drawing.gd`'s; the outline file matches the PNGs row by row for four frames; the frame follows the heading as the sprite's), `tools/test_build_car_outline.py` (the spans, the committed file up to date), and the T-541 and T-542 tests updated to the rule (`test_rail_grind.gd`, `test_rail_joints_and_heights.gd`).
