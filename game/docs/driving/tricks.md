## Tricks

Task T-079. The MVP's three tricks are decided in Drifting, Decisions, and the two aerial tricks (task T-219) in Drifting, Decisions, "Aerial tricks". The car finds them in its own driving and never changes how it drives to do it: `scripts/driving/trick_detector.gd` (`TrickDetector`) only reads the driving model after each physics step. Every car (the drift prototype's and the run's) has one, `car.tricks`.

### Trick signal

```
signal trick(trick_id: String)
```

The car node (`scripts/driving/car.gd`) emits it once per trick, at the physics step the trick is completed, for the combo (task T-081) and the trick popups. Several tricks can complete on the same step; each gets its own signal.

| Trick id | Trick | Fires |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `extended_drift` | Extended drift (task T-246) | Once, when one unbroken drift ends (a break, or a takeoff), if it lasted at least `drift_pay.min_seconds`. `car.tricks.last_trick_seconds` is its length while the signal is sent; the run scene passes it to `RunState.add_trick(id, seconds)`, and `ComboData.drift_amount(seconds)` scales the amount |
| `three_sixty` | 360 | Each time the car's heading has turned a full 360 degrees in one direction during one unbroken drift: 720 degrees gives 2 |
| `alternate_drift` | Alternate drift | Each switch between a forward drift and a backward drift with the same steering side, once the new drift has lasted the shortest drift time |
| `air_one_eighty` | Air 180 (task T-219) | On landing, when the heading turned at least 180 degrees in one direction during that airtime and an odd half turn is left after the air 360s: 180 gives 1, 540 an air 360 and an air 180 |
| `air_three_sixty` | Air 360 (task T-219) | On landing, once per full 360 degrees turned in one direction during that airtime (not its 180 too): 720 gives 2 |

Two more tricks raise the combo without the `trick` signal: the airtime trick (`airtime`, the car's `airtime_trick` signal, "Airtime trick (task T-242)" below) and **thread the needle** (`thread_the_needle`, task T-272: driving between two obstacles paired in the level creator; found by `scripts/level/thread_the_needle.gd` from the map's pairs, not by the car; "Level creator", "Thread the needle (T-272)").

The ids are constants on `TrickDetector` (`TRICK_EXTENDED_DRIFT`, `TRICK_THREE_SIXTY`, `TRICK_ALTERNATE_DRIFT`, `TRICK_AIR_ONE_EIGHTY`, `TRICK_AIR_THREE_SIXTY`, the list `TRICK_IDS` and the aerial ones `AIR_TRICK_IDS`); they match the keys of `trick_add` in `data/combo.json` (except `extended_drift`, which `drift_pay` scales by length). `car.tricks.last_trick`, `car.tricks.seconds_since_last` and `car.tricks.counts` (trick id to count) are there for readouts; `car.tricks.reset()` forgets every drift in progress and the counts. `car.detect_tricks = false` turns detection off.

### How each trick is found

- **Drifting** is the driving model's own `is_drifting`, the Unity definition: W, or S without W, held together with A or D. A **break** is any physics step on which the car is not drifting.
- **Extended drift (T-246):** drifting time adds up step by step and nothing is paid while it lasts. The first step that is not drifting ends the drift: if its time is at least `drift_min_seconds` (from `data/combo.json`, `drift_pay.min_seconds`) the detector sets `last_trick_seconds` and emits one trick; the time then starts from zero. Changing steering side or between W and S without a break does not end it. `reset()` forgets a drift in progress without paying. The amount is `ComboData.drift_amount(seconds)`: seconds times `drift_pay.per_second`, at most `drift_pay.max`; the combo's `add_trick(id, seconds)` takes it, and the popup shows it as `LONG DRIFT! +0.12` (the existing text). The XP from drifting seconds (`run_state.gd`, `drift_xp_per_second`) is separate and unchanged. Recording fixture: `tests/fixtures/drift_pay_gif.tscn` (script `drift_pay_gif_demo.gd`, `DRIFT_PAY_POPUP_PNG` saves the popup frame). Tests: `tests/unit/test_tricks.gd`, `tests/unit/test_combo_data.gd`, `tests/unit/run/test_run_combo.gd`.
- **360:** the heading's change adds up step by step while drifting. Turning the other way starts the count again from that step; a break starts it from zero. A full turn (360 degrees, 94 steps with the default turn speed) is one trick, and the count carries on towards the next one. Each spin pays once (task T-219, board): `car.gd` calls `observe()` after its height step with `airborne` (the step ends in the air), and the turn on such steps is not added. That turn goes to the aerial tricks instead. Since "no drifting in the air", the model is not drifting while airborne, so a takeoff is a break: a drift 360 in progress starts again from zero, and so does the extended drift's second.
- **Air 180 and air 360 (task T-219; the proposals are in Drifting, Content, "Aerial tricks"):** `car.gd` calls `tricks.observe_air(airborne, turned)` once per physics step after its height step (`car_height.gd`), with whether the car is in the air and the heading's change on that step. Steps that end in the air add their turn in one direction; turning back starts a new count and the airtime keeps its largest. On the landing step the best turn is paid in half turns (`half_turns()`, `HALF_TURN` = 180 degrees): each pair is an air 360, an odd one left over an air 180. Faster in the air (the board's tweak): `car.gd` sets `model.airborne` from the height model before each step, and the model turns at `tuning.turn_rate_for(airborne)`. That is the ground rate (191.9 deg/s, unchanged), times `air_turn_multiplier + air_turn_bonus` while airborne (`car_tuning.gd`; the base is 1.25 since T-269, halved from 2.5; `air_turn_bonus` is 0 unless the garage's air turn upgrade, T-270, sets it at the run's start with `tuning.apply_air_turn_bonus(added)`, next to `apply_acceleration_upgrade()` in `GameFlow.start_run()`). At 1.25 a 180 takes 38 steps in the air and a 360 about 76: an air 360 off a height-6 rooftop (49 steps) needs a total of about 1.9. Its slider is in the tuning panel's "In the air" group (`CarTuning.AIR_SETTINGS`, kept in `collision_sliders`). So at the base a 180 takes 38 steps in the air; a fall from a height-6 rooftop counts 49. No drifting in the air (the board's tweak): while `model.airborne` is set, `CarModel.update_velocity()` only spins the body at that rate and returns. The velocity is left exactly as it was at takeoff (no speeding up, braking, steering, grip or drift), `is_drifting` is false and the drift amount fades at the exit rate. Wall bounces and knockback still act, as they are applied by `car.gd` outside the model. Driving resumes on the step after the landing, from the kept velocity and the landed heading; see Drifting, Content, "No drifting in the air", for how a landing at an angle behaves. - **Alternate drift:** the driving is cut into drift segments, each with one direction (W: forward, S without W: backward) and one steering side. A switch counts when, in order: a segment lasts at least the **shortest drift** time; it ends (W or S released, or straight from W to S); within the **switch time** a segment starts in the other direction with the same steering side; and that segment also lasts the shortest drift time, at which moment the trick fires. Holding S before releasing W counts as a switch with no gap. A segment too short to count breaks the chain. While **keep steering** is on, letting go of A or D (or pressing the other one) between the two drifts cancels the trick. Forward to backward and backward to forward each count, so W, S, W, S with A held all the way gives 3.

### A crash pays the trick in progress (T-315)

Game loop architecture, Decisions, "A crash pays the trick in progress"; which tricks and why: Drifting, Content, same title. `RunState.collision()` (hits through the car's `hit_obstacle`, and any damage, since `damage_car()` calls it) sends the signal `crashed` while the run is going, with or without a combo to cut. The run scene connects it (`run.gd`, `_on_crashed`) to `car.settle_tricks()`, which calls `tricks.settle()` when `detect_tricks` is on. `TrickDetector.settle()` pays what is in progress as if it ended now: the drift so far (one `extended_drift`, `last_trick_seconds` set, if it reached `drift_min_seconds`; the drift's time then starts from zero) and, in the air, the spins and the airtime so far (through `_land()`, if there is a half turn or at least `airtime_min_seconds`, from `combo.json`'s `airtime.min_seconds`; the car stays in the air and the rest of the airtime counts from zero). Anything too short to pay is left alone, so a crash with no trick in progress behaves as before. The 360 and the alternate drift are never in progress (they pay when complete) and are not touched.

The order is `combo.json`'s `crash_pays_trick_first` (`ComboData.crash_pays_trick_first()`, default `true`): `crashed` fires before the cut, so `combo_trick` (the LONG DRIFT! popup) comes before `combo_broken` (COMBO BROKEN!); `false` fires it after the cut. Because the signals are synchronous, the trick's `add_trick` lands inside `collision()`. Tests: `tests/unit/run/test_crash_pays_trick.gd`. GIF fixture: `tests/fixtures/crash_pays_gif.tscn` (script `crash_pays_gif_demo.gd`; a drift from x2.0 into a boulder south of the building at (12, -96); `CRASH_PAYS_POPUP_PNG` saves the popup frame, `CRASH_START="x,y,rotation"` overrides the start): `"$GODOT_BIN" --path game --fixed-fps 50 --write-movie C:/abs/dir/f.png res://tests/fixtures/crash_pays_gif.tscn`, then `python game/tools/make_turn_gif.py C:/abs/dir out.gif --glob "f*.png" --every 2 --size 640 420 --scale 1 --ms 40`.

### Alternate drift limits

The limits are tuning values in `scripts/driving/car_tuning.gd` (`TRICK_SETTINGS`), read live, with sliders in the tuning panel's "Tricks" group. The panel's reset puts them back to these proposed values, for the board to tune:

| Setting | Key | Proposed | Slider |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Switch time: longest time from the end of one drift to the start of the opposite one | `alternate_max_switch_seconds` | 0.5 s | 0 to 2 s |
| Shortest drift: how long both drifts must last; the trick fires when the new one reaches it | `alternate_min_drift_seconds` | 0.3 s | 0 to 2 s |
| Keep steering: A or D must stay held on the same side during the switch | `alternate_keep_steering` | on | on / off toggle |

The extended drift's second and the 360's full turn are Decisions, so they are constants, not sliders.
