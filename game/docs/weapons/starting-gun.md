# The starting gun

(From the old `game/README.md`; the index is [../../README.md](../../README.md).)

**The starting gun** (`starting_gun.gd`, `weapon_id` `gun`): `stats` is `WeaponData.gun_at(tier)` (plus `bullet_drawn_size` for the placeholder). `fire_clock` counts physics time; when it reaches `fire_interval()` (1.26 s at tier 1) it goes back to 0 and the gun looks for enemies within `stats.range` of the car's centre. None: nothing happens until the next check, one interval later (the gun checks only once per interval, so an enemy that walks in just after a check waits up to 1.26 s). Some: a volley of `stats.bullets` bullets (none when `bullets` is 0, the T-422 guard). **Targeting rule** (T-408; Weapons, Content, "The starting gun"): the enemies in range sorted by distance, closest first, and `stats.targets` (`targets_per_volley()`: 1, then 3 from tier 6 and 5 at tier 11) says how many a volley aims at. **One target:** every bullet is queued for the closest, `delay_between_bullets` (0.05 s) apart; as each leaves, a target that is dead or doomed (`is_doomed`: `damage_in_flight(target)`, the damage of the gun's bullets still flying at it, kept in `_in_flight` as [bullet, target] pairs, is at least its `health`) is swapped for the closest living, not doomed enemy in range (`rack.enemies_within`); when all are doomed it keeps its target, or the last seen place if that died. **Several:** the closest `targets` enemies; bullet i goes to target i mod their count (closest first, round again), all fired in the same step. Each bullet leaves the car's centre aimed at its target's centre at that moment, with no leading. `fired(bullet, target)` fires per bullet; `fire_at(aim_units, target = null)` fires one bullet by hand (tests and fixtures). `checks` and `volleys` count the checks and the volleys that fired.

**The bullet** (`bullet.gd`, a `Node2D` in `rack.draw_root`, node `WeaponShots`, just before the car's `View`): flies in a straight line in the flat world at `bullet_speed` (15 units/s), starting on the physics step after it was fired. After each move it hits every living enemy whose body comes within `bullet_hit_radius` (1 unit) of its centre, closest first, each enemy at most once: `take_damage(damage)` (4, a minion's health) and its `hit(enemy, damage)` signal. It is done after `1 + pierce_hits` hits (1 at every tier since T-408; `pierce_hits` stays in the data code but no tier sets it), or once it has flown `missed_bullet_max_travel` (the gun's range at its tier). No knockback, and it passes through walls and boulders, as in the prototype. It is drawn 0.5 units above the ground at its place in the isometric view, with the minions' depth-order rule; at the same depth the car is drawn over it, so a bullet comes out from under the car.

**Not in the data:** the bullet's drawn height (0.5 units), its placeholder colours and the 0.5-unit fallback target size are in the code (`bullet.gd`, `weapons.gd`): sizes and looks of the placeholder, not balancing numbers.

**Tiers and last tier:** `WeaponData.gun_at(tier)` adds the tiers' effects in order: `add_bullets`, `fire_rate_multiplier` (interval = `base_fire_interval` / rate + `extra_delay_between_volleys`), `set_targets`, and the older `range_multiplier` and `pierce` (unused by the gun's tiers since T-408). The gun's entry in `weapons.json` has its own `max_tier` (11); `WeaponData.max_tier(weapon_id)` returns it, else the shared top-level `max_tier` (8), and `weapon.gd` `max_tier()` asks with its own id, so the level-up offers stop at each weapon's own last tier (this covers T-422 for the gun).

**Tests:** `tests/unit/weapons/test_starting_gun.gd`, part of the GUT suite: the rack in the run with the gun at tier 1 and its draw order; no shots with no enemy or one just out of range; shots at the data's interval (1.26 s) with an enemy in range; one check per interval; the closest enemy targeted and damaged by the data's amount (4), the others untouched; a minion killed through `take_damage`; one bullet killing a minion at tier 1; the bullet's speed and its start at the car's centre; the 1-unit hit circle plus the minion's body; a missed bullet removed past the range; below tier 6 the whole volley at the closest, 0.05 s apart, and passed to the next closest when its target dies mid-volley; tier 6 and 7 spreading over the 3 closest at once (round again with 4 bullets); one enemy taking a tier 6 volley at once; tier 11 over the 5 closest at once; no volley with 0 bullets; every tier's values equal to `WeaponData.gun_at()` and the last tier 11; `upgrade()` through the tiers and not past 11; the level-up offers still offering the gun at tier 10, not at 11; tiers 2, 3, 7 and 8 adding one bullet; tiers 4, 5, 9 and 10 shortening the interval to the data's; the range still 12 at tier 11; a new run (also one started from the main menu) back to the base gun with no projectiles; a non-minion enemy in the group (a stand-in for the kaiju) targeted and damaged; nothing firing while paused or after the run ends; and clear of the heart's red in the placeholder bullet.
