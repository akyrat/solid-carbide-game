## The neon underglow

Task T-168 (Weapon Behavior Agent): the neon underglow as Weapons, Decisions describes it, with T-166's numbers (`data/weapons.json` "underglow" through `WeaponData.underglow_at(tier)`). What it does: `docs/weapons.md`, Content, "The neon underglow (built in task T-168)". The zone is drawn in code (T-274, "The drawing"); the T-175 sprite is kept with a switch back to it.

**See it:** open `tests/fixtures/weapons/underglow_demo.tscn` and press F6: a run from the main menu with the underglow given and four minions just inside the ring; after 2 seconds the keyboard drives the car. Or set `underglow_at_start` to `true` in `data/playtest.json` and start the game (F5). Screenshot, from the repo root: `bash game/tools/screenshot.sh res://tests/fixtures/weapons/underglow_demo.tscn screenshots/underglow.png 40`.

**How it plugs in:** `scripts/weapons/neon_underglow.gd` extends `weapon.gd` (`weapon_id` `underglow`, `starts_owned` false) and is listed in `weapons.gd`'s `WEAPON_SCRIPTS`; `run.weapons.underglow` (or `get_weapon("underglow")`). A new run resets it to tier 0; `run.weapons.upgrade("underglow")` gives it and applies its tiers; `set_tier(t)` sets any tier. `stats` is `underglow_at(tier)` with `damage` times `rack.damage_multiplier()` (re-applied by `set_damage_bonus()`). Offered at a level-up as `get_underglow` / `upgrade_underglow` (T-172, "The level-up offers").

**Playtest switch:** `data/playtest.json`, `"underglow_at_start"` (false, the game's rule). `true` makes the rack call `upgrade("underglow")` at the start of every run (`weapons.gd`, `reset_for_run()`). Tests override it with the script's static `playtest_override` (0 off, 1 on, -1 read the file).

**Each step while owned:** the targets are `rack.living_enemies()`; `is_inside(enemy)` is centre within `radius()` of the car's centre (`contains_point()`), or, for an enemy with `hit_radius_units()` (the kaiju), its body circle overlapping the ring's circle. Each enemy has its own clock (`since_hit`, by instance id): an enemy inside is hit (`take_damage(stats.damage)`, signal `enemy_hit(enemy, amount)`) when it has not been hit in the last `tick_interval` seconds, so it is hit on the step it enters and every interval after; leaving and coming back within its interval gives no extra hit. A clock is dropped once the enemy is outside and its interval is over. `hits` counts the hits this run.

**Damage and push (T-409):** tier 1's `damage` is 2, half a minion's health (4), so a minion dies on its second tick; tiers 3 and 6 give 3 and 4.5. After each hit `_hit()` pushes a surviving minion (`pushes()`: it has `apply_knockback()`, not in group `kaiju`) `knockback_distance` (0.5, `weapons.json`, the same at every tier) straight away from the car; the kaiju gets `apply_nudge()` (`kaiju.gd`, through its `_move()`, so obstacles still stop it) of `kaiju_nudge_distance` (0.1). Both distances come through `underglow_at()`. Recording: `bash game/tools/record_underglow_push_gif.sh <out.gif>` (fixture `tests/fixtures/weapons/underglow_push_gif.tscn`).

**The drawing (T-274):** the zone is drawn in code, not from the sprite. `scripts/weapons/underglow_glow.gd` (node `NeonUnderglow`, made the first time the underglow is owned) is the first child of the run's `View` (under the car sprite, the drift smoke and the flames), placed on the car each step at the car's height (T-225) and hidden while not owned. It marks the damage circle through the isometric transform (an ellipse about twice as wide as tall, flat radius `radius x 16` px) as an image built pixel by pixel, opaque pixels or nothing: a bright pink ring (light core line, hot pink line inside it) just inside the edge, a stepped purple halo outside it (a solid band, then a checker-dithered one) and a purple floor glow inside it (half-dithered, then sparse). The halo and floor glow breathe out and in in steps (the pulse, 2 pulses in the 2 s cycle) while a lighter arc travels once round the ring in the cycle (the shimmer); the animation runs in `_process`, so it stops while paused. The images are built once per radius and frame (24 frames at 12 per second) and reused. **Every colour, width and speed is in `scenes/weapons/neon_underglow_look.tres`** (script `scripts/weapons/underglow_look.gd`): edit it in the Godot inspector to retune; clear of the heart's red. **Switch back to the T-175 sprite:** set `use_sprite` on that resource (the sprite `art/weapons/underglow.png` is kept). Screenshot fixtures: `tests/fixtures/run_underglow_look_{street,roof}_{t1,t8}.tscn`.

**Tests:** `tests/unit/weapons/test_neon_underglow.gd`: not owned in a new run and given by `upgrade()`; the entering hit on that step and one per tick interval with the data's damage times the garage multiplier; nothing outside; leaving and coming back within the interval; separate clocks per enemy; a big enemy hit by body overlap and a minion by centre; a real minion damaged, and dead on its second tick, not its first; damage 2, 3 and 4.5 at tiers 1, 3 and 6; each tick pushing a minion away by the data's distance and only nudging the kaiju; nothing while paused or after the run ends; a new run removes it; the playtest switch; every tier's values; one test per tier 2 to 8 checking the change from weapons.json's tier list and playing it; the damage bonus on every tier; the ring under the car sprite, following the car, the isometric shape, its size with the tiers, and its colours (opaque, not the heart's red).
