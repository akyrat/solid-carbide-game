## The windshield wiper

Task T-170 (Weapon Behavior Agent): the windshield wiper as Weapons, Decisions describes it, with T-166's numbers (`data/weapons.json` "wiper" through `WeaponData.wiper_at(tier)`). What it does: `docs/weapons.md`, Content, "The windshield wiper (built in task T-170)". The blade is a placeholder drawn in code; the final art comes from a separate art task. **It pushes enemies** (so does Bricks since T-324, through the same call).

**See it:** open `tests/fixtures/weapons/wiper_demo.tscn` and press F6: a run from the main menu with the wiper given and four minions across the semicircle in front of the car, pushed away as the blade reaches them; after 2 seconds the keyboard drives the car. Or set `wiper_at_start` to `true` in `data/playtest.json` and start the game (F5). Screenshot, from the repo root: `bash game/tools/screenshot.sh res://tests/fixtures/weapons/wiper_demo.tscn screenshots/wiper.png 70`.

**How it plugs in:** `scripts/weapons/windshield_wiper.gd` extends `weapon.gd` (`weapon_id` `wiper`, `starts_owned` false) and is listed in `weapons.gd`'s `WEAPON_SCRIPTS`; `run.weapons.wiper` (or `get_weapon("wiper")`). A new run resets it to tier 0 (and the arm to its start); `run.weapons.upgrade("wiper")` gives it and applies its tiers; `set_tier(t)` sets any tier. `stats` is `wiper_at(tier)` with `damage` times `rack.damage_multiplier()`. Offered at a level-up as `get_wiper` / `upgrade_wiper` (T-172, "The level-up offers").

**Playtest switch:** `data/playtest.json`, `"wiper_at_start"` (false, the game's rule). `true` makes the rack call `upgrade("wiper")` at the start of every run. Tests override it with the script's static `playtest_override` (0 off, 1 on, -1 read the file).

**The arm:** it turns round `pivot_units()`, `PIVOT_OFFSET_UNITS` (0.5, the nose of the car's 1-unit body) ahead of the car's centre, and reaches `length()` units from it. `sweep_angle` is its angle in degrees from the car's facing (`forward()`, from `CarModel.rotation`), between `-sweep_arc_degrees / 2` and `+sweep_arc_degrees / 2`; it moves `sweep_degrees_per_second` each second in `sweep_dir` (+1 or -1) and turns back at each end, which starts a new pass (`pass_index`). A run gets it at the -90 end. `arm_direction()` and `tip_units()` give the arm in the world, so the semicircle turns with the car.

**Hits:** each step, the arm's swept wedge (from its old angle to its new one, out to `length()`) is tested against `rack.living_enemies()`; an enemy is hit when its body (`rack.radius_of()`) touches the wedge or the arm at either end of the step. One enemy is hit at most once per pass and never twice within `hit_cooldown` seconds (`last_hit`, by instance id: [pass, seconds since]). A hit is `take_damage(stats.damage)`, then, when `pushes(enemy)` is true, `enemy.apply_knockback(away, knockback_distance)` with `away` from the car's centre to the enemy's (the minions' call, T-167); signal `enemy_hit(enemy, amount, pushed)`, counter `hits`. `pushes()` is true only for an enemy with `apply_knockback()` that is not in the group `kaiju` (unless the data's `knockback_kaiju` is true): the real kaiju has no knockback call at all.

**The drawing:** `WiperDrawing` (inner class, node `WindshieldWiper`), made the first time the wiper is owned, in the run's `View`, placed on the pivot each step with `arm_px` the arm through the isometric transform; hidden while not owned. It sits just over the car sprite while the arm's middle is lower on screen than the car's centre (`drawn_over_car()`) and just under it otherwise. It draws a dark outline, a dark steel arm and a pale grey rubber blade on its outer 80%, and a small pivot square (`ARM_COLOUR`, `BLADE_COLOUR`, `OUTLINE_COLOUR`; opaque, hard-edged, clear of the heart's red).

**Tests:** `tests/unit/weapons/test_windshield_wiper.gd` (test minion `tests/fixtures/weapons/fake_minion.gd`, which records every knockback call): not owned in a new run and given by `upgrade()`; the arm sweeping back and forth between -90 and +90 degrees at the data's speed, always in front of the car; the semicircle turning with the car; a minion hit for the data's damage times the garage multiplier and pushed the data's distance straight away from the car; a real minion damaged and knocked back; a big enemy, a kaiju stand-in with a knockback call and the real kaiju damaged but not moved; once per pass and never within the cooldown; nothing out of reach or behind the car; nothing while paused or after the run ends; a new run removes it; the playtest switch; every tier's values; one test per tier 2 to 8 checking the change from weapons.json's tier list and playing it; the damage bonus on every tier; the blade on the pivot out to the tip, following the car, growing with the tiers, over or under the car by its direction; and its colours. `test_starting_gun.gd` and `test_exhaust_flamethrower.gd` each check that their weapon never moves an enemy (a real minion and a recording one).

**Tier 8, the back wiper (T-499):** `weapons.json` "wiper" has `max_tier` 8 and tier 8 `add_back_wiper` (`wiper_at(tier)["back_wiper"]` is true from tier 8). `windshield_wiper.gd` `_sync_back_wiper()` (called first in `apply_tier()`) makes a second instance of the same script, `back_wiper` (a child node named "BackWiper", `is_back` true), and gives it the front's tier, so its `stats` are the same; below tier 8 or at tier 0 it is set to tier 0 (not owned, drawing hidden). The front wiper's `step()` steps it. `forward()` is reversed for it (so its pivot is on the car's tail and its arm points behind the car) and `turn_sign()` is -1 (the arm's angle and the hit wedge's angle are mirrored, so it is a mirror image of the front arm). It has its own `last_hit`, `pass_index` and drawing (a second `WiperDrawing`, same sheet, frame picked by the arm's direction); its hits are re-emitted by the front wiper's `enemy_hit`. Tests: the last four in `tests/unit/weapons/test_windshield_wiper.gd`. Screenshot fixture: `tests/fixtures/weapons/wiper_back_demo.tscn` (screenshots in `art-archive/T-499/screenshots/`).
