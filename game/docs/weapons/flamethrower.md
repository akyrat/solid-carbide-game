## The exhaust flamethrower

Task T-042 (Weapon Behavior Agent): the exhaust flamethrower as Weapons, Decisions describes it, copying the Unity prototype's Flame Exhaust (Unity weapons report, section 3) with the board's fixes, and T-040's numbers (`data/weapons.json` through `WeaponData.flame_at(tier)`). What it does: `docs/weapons.md`, Content, "The exhaust flamethrower (built in task T-042)". The flames are placeholders (orange and yellow squares); the final flame art comes from a separate art task.

**See it:** with the playtest switch on (below), start the game (F5), press Start and drift (W or S held with A or D). Screenshot, from the repo root: `bash game/tools/screenshot.sh res://tests/fixtures/weapons/flamethrower_demo.tscn screenshots/flamethrower.png 90` (a run from the main menu, the car drifting in a circle with the flames behind it). Copies in `art-archive/T-042/screenshots/`.

**See the hit area (T-267):** `bash game/tools/screenshot.sh res://tests/fixtures/weapons/flame_hit_area_shot.tscn out.png 160` (the car standing, the drift held, a minion over the flame and one clear of it; the hit outline in green and the minions' circles in yellow are a debug draw in the fixture only) and `bash game/tools/record_flame_hit_area.sh out.gif 6` (a tier 1 drift through still minions; needs a window and Pillow). Fixture `tests/fixtures/weapons/flame_hit_area_demo.gd` (`mode` shot or gif). Results in `art-archive/T-267/screenshots/`.

**How it plugs in:** `scripts/weapons/flamethrower/exhaust_flamethrower.gd` extends `weapon.gd` (`weapon_id` `flamethrower`, `starts_owned` false) and is listed in `weapons.gd`'s `WEAPON_SCRIPTS`. The rack owns it (`run.weapons.flamethrower`, or `get_weapon("flamethrower")`), steps it once per physics step while it is owned (after the car has moved: the run's `PhysicsWorld` comes before `Weapons`), and resets it at every new run to tier 0, not owned (Weapons, Decisions). `run.weapons.upgrade("flamethrower")` gives it (0 to 1) and then applies its tiers, the call the level-up (T-043) will use; `set_tier(t)` sets any tier. `run.gd` has no flamethrower lines.

**Playtest switch:** `game/data/playtest.json`, key `"flamethrower_at_start"` (the file is a dictionary of independent switches). `true` makes the rack call `upgrade("flamethrower")` at the start of every run (`weapons.gd`, `reset_for_run()`), so the board can play it before the level-up offer exists; `false` is the game's rule (every run starts without it). Not player-facing. Tests override it with the script's static `playtest_override` (0 off, 1 on, -1 read the file).

**Each step while owned:** on while the car's drift state (`CarModel.is_drifting`) is on at `min_speed` or faster, then for `linger` seconds, or always from tier 8. The flame (drawn and hit area, below) starts each drift at `ramp_start_fraction` of full size and grows over `ramp_seconds` (`size_fraction()`, `drawn_scale()`). Damage is the burn (T-497, afterburn; Weapons, Decisions, "The board's own upgrade trees"): an enemy touching the flame that is not burning yet takes one hit at once and starts burning (`_start_burn()`: its `sprite`'s `modulate` = the data's `burn_flash_tint`, `BurnFlames.add_source(e, "flamethrower")`). Each burning enemy has its own entry in `burning` (instance id -> `node`, `left`: burn seconds left, `tick`: seconds since its last hit; the entering step counts on that clock, so the rhythm is the old shared tick's). Every step `_age_burns(dt)` runs first, in `step()` and in `step_wrecked()` alike: each burn's clock runs and, every `tick_interval`, the enemy takes one hit, `take_damage(stats.damage)` (the flame's damage now) and signal `enemy_hit(enemy, amount)`, inside the flame or not; its `left` runs down and at 0 (a hit due on that very step still lands) the tint goes back to white and the burn flames go. Then, while the flame is on, every enemy touching it has its `left` set back to `burn_flash_seconds` (`_refresh_burn()`, no extra hit). So an enemy inside the flame takes one hit per tick, and one that leaves keeps burning, hits, tint and flames, for exactly `burn_flash_seconds` after the last step it touched the flame, also with the flame off and while the car is wrecked. All its hits come from its one clock: never two flame hits in one tick, and touching the flame again only refreshes the burn. There is no shared tick timer any more (`tick_timer` is gone). The code still understands a `turn_speed_multiplier` effect on the car's `tuning.turn_speed`, but no tier of the data uses it since T-496 (the steering tier is gone; a new run's car starts at its own speed). Values: `tier`, `stats`, `active`, `hits`, `is_burning(enemy)`.

**Targets:** `rack.living_enemies()`. **Hit area = the drawn flame (T-267; Weapons, Decisions, "A smaller exhaust flame that burns what it touches"):** `hit_polygon()` is the outline of the flame as drawn now: `ExhaustFlameDrawing.cell_hull(cell)` (the convex hull of the opaque pixels of the sheet cell being drawn, made once per cell from the sheet and cached) at `drawn_scale()` (`size_multiplier` times the ramp, the same number the drawing's `scale` gets), from `exhaust_units()`, taken back through the isometric projection into the flat world. `current_cell()` is the cell the drawing shows (the direction from the car's heading, the flicker frame from the drawing). An enemy is hit (`is_inside()`) when its body circle (`rack.radius_of(e)`: a minion's 0.5 units, the kaiju's `hit_radius_units()`) touches the outline (`overlaps_circle()`; `contains_point()` for a point), so a minion's edge counts, not only its centre. Height is ignored as before: the area is on the flat world at the car's map position (the drawing is lifted). **Why the area and the art differed before:** the area was a rectangle from four numbers in the data (`car_half_size`, `inner_edge_multiplier`, `depth_multiplier`, `half_width_multiplier`, now removed from `weapons.json` and from `flame_at()`), 3.25 units deep and 2.6 wide at full size, in the flat world and starting 0.175 behind the car's centre; the drawing was the 95-pixel plume at the same size factor but unrelated to it: about twice the rectangle's depth on the screen, starting 0.42 behind the centre, and a teardrop in a rectangle; and the minions counted by their centre only. **Size:** `base_size_multiplier` 0.625 (a quarter of 2.5), tiers 2, 4 and 6 times 1.2, 1.2, 1.3. **Start point:** `EXHAUST_OFFSET_UNITS` 1.2 (was 0.42, under the car's body; a quarter-size flame there would be hidden by the car), a proposal for the playtest.

**The drawing (T-071, final art):** `scripts/weapons/flamethrower/exhaust_flame_drawing.gd` (a `Node2D` on `scenes/weapons/flamethrower/exhaust_flame.tscn`, node `ExhaustFlame`; it replaced the particle placeholder), made the first time the flamethrower is owned and kept in the run's `View`, with `z_index` -1 (`DRAW_Z_BEHIND`, T-267: the car and the enemies not covered by a building sit at the same depth, so a flame at the car's depth drew over the enemies that came before the `View` in the tree; one step lower it is behind the car and them, still over the ground, rooftops and buildings, whose depths are at least 2 steps lower), at 1.2 units behind the car's centre (`EXHAUST_OFFSET_UNITS`) and, since T-206, lifted by the car's height. The flamethrower still sets its `position`, `rotation` (the screen angle pointing back), `scale` (size multiplier times the ramp) and `emitting` (on while the flame is on) each step. The node draws the nearest of 32 pre-turned flames from `art/effects/exhaust_flame_sheet.png` (2 flicker frames, 224 x 224 cells, base at the cell centre, drawn at 0.5) and counters its own rotation, so the engine never rotates the pixels; the flicker changes every 0.08 s. The sheet is built by `tools/build_weapon_effects.py` from the one PixelLab generation `art-archive/T-071/exhaust_flame_raw.png` (tailpipe erased by script). Clear of the heart's red. The drift smoke stays on with the flamethrower (board, 2026-10-14).

**Weapon effects art (T-071):** the starting gun's bullet is `art/effects/bullet.png` (a cyan neon ball with a white-hot core, 2 pulse frames of 12 x 12, drawn by script with no PixelLab call; `bullet.gd` draws it at 0.5 and changes frame every 0.4 units flown; the hit circle is unchanged). Burning enemies get `scripts/enemies/burn_flames.gd` (`BurnFlames`): a weapon calls `BurnFlames.add_source(enemy, id)` when it burns the enemy and `remove_source` when it stops (the flamethrower's burn flash, id `"flamethrower"`; each molotov fire the enemy stands in, id the fire's instance id). The flames are a child of the enemy's `sprite`, drawn from `art/effects/burning_flames.png` (one PixelLab generation, `art-archive/T-071/burning_raw.png`, logs cut off by script, 2 mirrored frames of 48 x 54 flickering every 0.12 s), one set per enemy whatever the sources, bigger in whole steps for a bigger body (the kaiju). Drawing only: damage, tint and movement are unchanged. All three sheets and their records are made by `python game/tools/build_weapon_effects.py build` (`check` only runs the rules: alpha 0 or 255, at most 30 colours, clear of the heart's red; tests `tools/test_build_weapon_effects.py` and `tests/unit/enemies/test_burn_flames.gd`). Look at it: `T071_FRAMES=<folder> bash game/tools/screenshot.sh res://tests/fixtures/weapons/weapon_effects_demo.tscn out.png 560` (it also saves every 4th frame to the folder); pictures and a GIF are in `art-archive/T-071/screenshots/`.

**Tests:** `tests/unit/weapons/test_exhaust_flamethrower.gd` (part of the GUT suite; test enemies `tests/fixtures/weapons/fake_enemy.gd` and `fake_big_enemy.gd`): on only while drifting and off after the linger, the minimum speed, the data's damage on entering and per tick inside and none outside, a big enemy burnt when its body overlaps the flames, the ramp, the same size on the screen in every direction, killing a real minion, the hit outline equal to the drawn flame through the ramp, 8 directions and 5 tiers (T-267), every drawn pixel inside it, a minion touching with its edge burnt, tier 1 a quarter of the old size, the depth behind the car and a minion, the burn flash, the afterburn (T-497: an enemy that leaves the flame takes a hit every tick for its burn time, then none, with the flame on or off; the hits are the flame's current damage; a burn 2 s longer keeps hitting 2 s longer; inside and burning, one hit per tick, also going in and out; re-entering refreshes the burn; the tint and flames stay on for exactly the burn time), nothing while paused, every tier's values and effects (size, damage, +1 s burn time, always on), a new run without it, the playtest switch, its place in the rack, the drawing's place and colours. `test_starting_gun.gd` turns the playtest switch off, so it checks the game's rule. `tests/unit/weapons/test_weapons_wreck.gd` checks that a burning enemy keeps taking its hits during the wreck (T-497).
