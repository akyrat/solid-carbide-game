## The molotovs

Task T-169 (Weapon Behavior Agent): the molotovs as Weapons, Decisions describes them, with T-166's numbers (`data/weapons.json` "molotov" through `WeaponData.molotov_at(tier)`). What they do: `docs/weapons.md`, Content, "The molotovs (built in task T-169)". The bottle and the fire are placeholders drawn in code; the final art comes from a separate art task.

**See it:** open `tests/fixtures/weapons/molotov_demo.tscn` and press F6: a run from the main menu with the molotovs given, three bottles thrown at once, two minions dropped into each of the first three fire areas and a ring of minions walking in; after 2 seconds the keyboard drives the car. Or set `molotov_at_start` to `true` in `data/playtest.json` and start the game (F5). Screenshot, from the repo root: `bash game/tools/screenshot.sh res://tests/fixtures/weapons/molotov_demo.tscn screenshots/molotov.png 125`. A copy is in `art-archive/T-169/screenshots/`.

**How it plugs in:** `scripts/weapons/molotovs.gd` extends `weapon.gd` (`weapon_id` `molotov`, `starts_owned` false) and is listed in `weapons.gd`'s `WEAPON_SCRIPTS`; `run.weapons.molotov` (or `get_weapon("molotov")`). A new run resets it to tier 0; `run.weapons.upgrade("molotov")` gives it and applies its tiers; `set_tier(t)` sets any tier. `stats` is `molotov_at(tier)` with `damage` times `rack.damage_multiplier()`. Offered at a level-up as `get_molotov` / `upgrade_molotov` (T-172, "The level-up offers").

**Playtest switch:** `data/playtest.json`, `"molotov_at_start"` (false, the game's rule). `true` makes the rack call `upgrade("molotov")` at the start of every run. Tests override it with the script's static `playtest_override` (0 off, 1 on, -1 read the file).

**Throwing:** `throw_clock` counts physics time while owned; at `throw_interval()` it goes back to 0 and `throw_volley()` throws `molotovs_per_throw` bottles, each to `random_landing()`: a uniform angle and a distance uniform between `throw_distance_min` and `throw_distance_max` from the car's centre, from the weapon's own `rng` (a `RandomNumberGenerator`; tests set `rng.seed`, and a new run does not re-seed it). The run has no shared random generator, so the weapon keeps its own, as the minion spawner and the kaiju do. `throw_to(landing_units)` throws one bottle by hand. Signals `thrown(bottle, landing)` and `fire_started(fire)`; counters `throws`, `bottles`, `fires`.

**The bottle** (`MolotovBottle`, inner class of `molotovs.gd`, a rack projectile in `rack.draw_root`): flies from the car's centre to its landing spot in `FLIGHT_SECONDS` (0.5 s, in the code: a look and feel value), drawn at its place in the isometric view raised by an arc (`height_units()`: from 0.5 units down to the ground plus a 1.5-unit peak), spinning, with the minions' depth rule. When it lands it calls the weapon's `land(centre)`, which makes the fire with the tier's values at that moment.

**The fire area** (`scripts/weapons/molotov_fire.gd`, a rack projectile): `centre`, `radius`, `duration`, `damage`, `tick_interval`, `age`. Each step it ages and, while `age` is within `duration`, hits the living enemies inside, each with its own clock as the underglow does (`since_hit`): hit on the step it enters, then every `tick_interval` while inside; nothing once it has left. `is_inside(enemy)`: centre within `radius` (`contains_point()`), or, with `hit_radius_units()` (the kaiju), its body circle overlapping. Signal `enemy_hit(enemy, amount)`, counter `hits`. Done just after `duration`. It is drawn on the ground: `rack.add_projectile(fire, parent)` (the rack's new optional `parent`) puts it in a node `MolotovFires` in the map's `view_root`, right after `DriftMarks`, so the map's isometric transform makes its flat circle the ground ellipse and the buildings, enemies and car are drawn over it. It is still stepped, freed when done and removed at a new run like any projectile.

**Look (placeholders):** bottle: green glass, pale rag, yellow flame, dark outline (`BOTTLE_*`). Fire: dark rim, orange disc, yellow middle, pale core, six yellow flickers that move every 0.1 s (`FIRE_*`). All opaque, hard-edged, clear of the heart's red.

**Tests:** `tests/unit/weapons/test_molotovs.gd`: not owned in a new run and given by `upgrade()`; a seeded throw at the interval, landing within the data's distances and leaving a fire of the data's radius and duration where it landed, and the next throw one interval later; 200 random spots within the distances and all round the car; the same seed giving the same spots; an enemy inside hit on entering and per tick with the data's damage times the garage multiplier, one outside never; one that leaves taking no more; a big enemy by body overlap; a real minion damaged; a fire going out after its duration; fires, bottles and throws stopping while paused and after the run ends; a new run removing fires, bottles and the molotovs; the playtest switch; every tier's values; one test per tier 2 to 8 checking the change from weapons.json's tier list and playing it; the damage bonus on every tier; the bottle in the draw root and arcing, the fire in `MolotovFires` after the drift marks; and the placeholder colours (opaque, not the heart's red).
