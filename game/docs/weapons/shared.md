## Weapons

Task T-041 (Weapon Behavior Agent): the starting gun, and how weapons plug into the run. What the gun does: `docs/weapons.md`, Content, "The starting gun". Numbers: `data/weapons.json` through `WeaponData` ("Game data" above); nothing in the weapon code changes them. The bullets are placeholders (a small cyan dot with a white core and a dark outline, drawn in code); the final bullet art comes from a separate art task and replaces `bullet.gd`'s `_draw()`.

**See it:** start the game (F5) and press Start, or open `scenes/run/run.tscn` and press F6. Once a minion is within 12 units of the car, the gun fires at it every 1.26 s. Screenshots, from the repo root: `bash game/tools/screenshot.sh res://tests/fixtures/starting_gun_demo.tscn screenshots/starting_gun.png 16` (the base gun's bullet on its way to the closest minion) and `bash game/tools/screenshot.sh res://tests/fixtures/starting_gun_tier2_demo.tscn screenshots/starting_gun_tier2.png 34` (the gun at tier 2, two of its three bullets in the air). Both start the run from the main menu, place four standing minions round the car and set the gun's clock to fire on the first step (fixture script `tests/fixtures/starting_gun_demo.gd`). Copies are in `art-archive/T-041/screenshots/`.

**How weapons plug into the run** (`scripts/weapons/`, for T-042 and later weapons):

- `weapons.gd`, **the weapon rack**: the run scene adds it as `run.weapons` (child `Weapons`) and calls `bind(run)`; that is the only change to `run.gd`. It holds one node of each weapon in `WEAPON_SCRIPTS`, created in `_init()` and kept for the whole session (`run.weapons.weapons`, a Dictionary by id; `run.weapons.gun` is the starting gun). Once per physics step (`_physics_process`, pausable, so the pause screen and the level-up pause stop the weapons) `step(dt)` moves every projectile, then steps every owned weapon; it does nothing once the run is over (`state.is_running()` false) or with `enabled = false` (tests). On `run.world_rebuilt` (every new run) it follows the new car and map, removes every projectile and calls each weapon's `reset_for_run()`: **a new run starts with the starting gun at tier 1 and nothing else.**
- `weapon.gd`, **the base of every weapon** (extend it with `extends "res://scripts/weapons/weapon.gd"`): `weapon_id` (the key in `data/weapons.json`: `gun`, `flamethrower`), `starts_owned` (true only for the gun), `tier` (0 = not owned, 1 = just got it, up to `max_tier()` = 8), `rack` (the rack, set for you), `data` (a `WeaponData`). `set_tier(t)` clamps, calls your `apply_tier()`, then emits `tier_changed(weapon_id, tier)`; `upgrade()` goes up one tier (0 to 1 gets the weapon) and returns false at the last tier; `is_owned()`, `is_max_tier()`; `reset_for_run()` sets tier 1 or 0 (override it to clear your own state, then call `super.reset_for_run()`). Override `step(dt)`: the rack calls it once per physics step while the weapon is owned and the run is going.
- **To add a weapon (T-042):** a new script in `scripts/weapons/` extending `weapon.gd`, setting `weapon_id` and `starts_owned` in `_init()`, reading its tier's values in `apply_tier()` (for example `data.flame_at(maxi(tier, 1))`), and doing its work in `step(dt)`; then add its preloaded script to `WEAPON_SCRIPTS` in `weapons.gd` (one line). It is then reachable as `run.weapons.get_weapon("flamethrower")`, reset every run, paused with the run, and upgradable through `run.weapons.upgrade(id)`.
- **What the rack gives a weapon:** `rack.car` (the run's car; `rack.car_units()` its centre in units, flat world), `rack.state` (the `RunState`), `rack.run` (the run scene), `rack.living_enemies()`, `rack.enemies_within(centre_units, radius_units)` (living enemies whose centre is within `radius_units`, closest first), `rack.radius_of(enemy)` (how far its body reaches, in units), `rack.is_target(node)`, `rack.add_projectile(node, parent = null)` (a `Node2D` with `step(dt)` and `is_done()`, drawn in `rack.draw_root`, or in `parent` when given, and freed once done or at a new run) and `rack.depth` (the minions' depth-order rule, `enemy_depth.gd`, for the `z_index` of anything drawn in the isometric view).
- **Upgrades from code** (the level-up offers them: "The level-up offers" below): `run.weapons.upgrade("gun")` applies the gun's next tier; `run.weapons.gun.set_tier(t)` sets any tier; `run.weapons.owned_ids()` lists the owned weapons; `run.weapons.get_weapon(id).tier` and `.is_max_tier()` say where each stands.

**Targets** (the minions and the kaiju, see "The kaiju"): every node in the group `enemies` inside the run that has `take_damage(amount: float)` and is not dead (`is_dead()`, when it has one; nodes queued for deletion are skipped). Each must have `position_units()` (its centre, flat world, units). Its size comes from `hit_radius_units()` when it has one, otherwise from its first `CollisionShape2D` (a circle's radius; half the diagonal of a rectangle; the larger of a capsule's radius and half height), otherwise 0.5 units. So **the kaiju needs only to join the group `enemies` and have `take_damage()` and `position_units()`**; if its body is not a circle, giving it `hit_radius_units()` makes hits match its size. Range is measured centre to centre (the target's size does not count, as in the prototype); hits use the target's size. Nothing here reads `run.minions` or uses collision layers, so meteors (on layer 3 but without `take_damage`) are never targeted.


### Size upgrades grow

Task T-286 (Weapon Behavior Agent). What it does and why these numbers: `docs/weapons.md`, Content, "Size upgrades grow on screen (built in task T-286)". `scripts/weapons/size_grow.gd` (`SizeGrow`, a RefCounted) holds the whole curve: `DURATION` 0.4 s and `OVERSHOOT` 3.0 (an ease-out-back like `LikesSpring`, T-273), `value_at(from, to, t)`, and one instance per size weapon (`grow`) with `value` (the size now), `target` (the data's), `active`, `grow_to(size)`, `snap(size)` and `advance(dt)`. Each weapon calls `grow.advance(dt)` first in its physics `step`, so the grow stands still while paused, and reads its size from `grow.value`: the wiper's `length()` (and `base_length()`, the art's 2.5, so the sheet is stretched by `length() / base_length()` through `WiperDrawing.set_arm(v, k)`), the underglow's `radius()` (the drawn ring and `contains_point` both use it) and the flame's `drawn_scale()` (`grow.value * size_fraction()`, used by both the drawing's scale and `hit_polygon()`). In `apply_tier()` a weapon calls `grow.grow_to(new size)` when `is_upgrade_step()` and `grow.snap(...)` otherwise. `weapon.gd` sets `_via_upgrade` only inside `upgrade()` (the call the level-up and gift pack make), so only a picked upgrade of an owned weapon grows; `set_tier()` called directly (a new run, tests), getting a weapon (tier 0 to 1) and a tier that keeps the size snap. `stats["length"]`, `["radius"]` and `["size_multiplier"]` stay the data's values. The molotovs have no grow (each fire area is made at the size of its bottle). Tests: `tests/unit/weapons/test_size_grow.gd`. Recording fixture `tests/fixtures/weapons/size_grow_demo.tscn` (the environment variable `SIZE_GROW_WEAPON` picks wiper, underglow or flamethrower): `SIZE_GROW_WEAPON=wiper "$GODOT_BIN" --path game --write-movie C:/abs/m/f.png --fixed-fps 30 --quit-after 150 res://tests/fixtures/weapons/size_grow_demo.tscn`, then `python game/tools/frames_to_gif.py C:/abs/m out.gif --fps 15 --skip 2 --crop 0.7 --shared --colors 64`. GIFs: `art-archive/T-286/screenshots/`.

### The level-up offers

Task T-043 (Weapon Behavior Agent): which weapon choices a level-up gives and applying the one taken. What they do: `docs/weapons.md`, Content, "The level-up offers". Code: `scripts/weapons/level_up_offers.gd`, made by the rack in `bind()` as `run.weapons.offers` (node `LevelUpOffers`). It listens to `RunState.level_up_choice_started`, which fires when the car drives over the gift pack's package (T-144, `RunState.open_choice()`; see "The gift pack drop"), or at once on a level-up with `state.pause_on_level_up` on (tests).

**For the UI Agent (T-052):**

- **Choice ids** (constants on the script, all in `ALL_IDS`): `"upgrade_gun"`, `"get_flamethrower"`, `"upgrade_flamethrower"`, and since T-172 `"get_underglow"`, `"upgrade_underglow"`, `"get_molotov"`, `"upgrade_molotov"`, `"get_wiper"`, `"upgrade_wiper"`, and since T-324 `"get_bricks"`, `"upgrade_bricks"`: `get_<weapon>` while the car does not have the weapon, `upgrade_<weapon>` once it does, below tier 8. `LevelUpOffers.weapon_of(id)` gives the weapon id (`"upgrade_wiper"` → `"wiper"`). Show text by id (the three new weapons' card texts are a separate text and UI task; until then `card_text()` gives empty strings for them).
- **Signal** `run.weapons.offers.offers_ready(level: int, offers: Array)`: the choice has started (the run is already paused). `offers` has 1 or 2 different ids, in `ALL_IDS` order (the gun's first). Also readable at any time: `offers.current_offers` (empty when none waiting) and `offers.has_offers()`.
- **Function** `run.weapons.offers.choose(id: String) -> bool`: call it with the id taken. It applies the choice, emits `offer_taken(id)`, then calls `state.choice_made()` (which resumes the run, or starts the next waiting level-up's choice and a new `offers_ready`). Returns false and changes nothing for an id not on offer.
- **Nothing to offer** (every weapon at the last tier): the choice is skipped on its own, `state.choice_made()` is called at once and `offers_ready` is not emitted, so the screen never opens. The screen must not call `choice_made()` itself.

**The draw (T-172):** `available_offers()` lists every available id (in `ALL_IDS` order, changes nothing). `_draw()` gives the choice (T-321: no fixed first offer any more, `FIRST_OFFERS`, `is_first_offer()` and `choices_taken` are gone, so the first level-up of a run is drawn like every other): the whole list when it has `OFFER_COUNT` (2) ids or fewer, else 2 different ids picked uniformly with the script's own `rng` (a `RandomNumberGenerator`, randomized once; tests set `offers.rng.seed`), returned in `ALL_IDS` order. `offers.compute_offers()` returns what the next choice will show without changing anything (it puts `rng.state` back). `choose(id)` calls `rack.upgrade(weapon_of(id))`.

**Playtest switches:** a run that starts with the flamethrower (`flamethrower_at_start`) draws its first level-up like any other, never offers `get_flamethrower`, and offers `upgrade_flamethrower` among the choices. `data/playtest.json` `"all_weapons_at_start"` (T-172; false, the game's rule; never visible to players) makes `weapons.gd`'s `reset_for_run()` give every weapon the car does not have yet at tier 1 (after the per-weapon switches, so a weapon is never given twice): all six weapons, and the level-ups offer only upgrades. Read by `weapons.gd`'s static `all_weapons_switch_on()`; tests override it with the static `all_weapons_override` (0 off, 1 on, -1 read the file).

**Tests:** `tests/unit/weapons/test_level_up_offers.gd`: the first level-up drawn over 200 seeds (every one of the six first choices appears, always two different ones, the same seed giving the same pair, no `FIRST_OFFERS`/`is_first_offer` left); later offers drawn; 40 seeded runs taken from level 1 until nothing is left (47 choices each since T-324), every offer two different available ids (one when one is left) and every one of the 11 ids offered; the same seed giving the same offers; `compute_offers()` matching the next draw and leaving the generator alone; the shown order; tier-8 weapons never offered; one choice left; nothing left skipping the choice; every id applied and the run resumed (the six new ids each); refused ids; `weapon_of()`; two level-ups at once; a new run clearing the offer; `flamethrower_at_start` leading to its upgrades; `all_weapons_at_start` on (all five at tier 1, with the flamethrower switch too), off, and false in the committed file. `test_level_up_screen.gd`'s two tests that needed a fixed later offer now max the other weapons or press whichever card is first.
