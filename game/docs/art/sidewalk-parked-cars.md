# Sidewalk, parked car and crater art (T-397 sample)

(The board's decision: Level design, Decisions, "Sidewalks and parked cars in the level creator"; the look: Visual style, Decisions, "Sidewalks, parked cars and meteor craters".)

T-397 is the sample for the board's review: one sidewalk tile with its curb, one parked car facing and (added by the board, 2026-10-30) one meteor crater. Nothing is in `game/art/` yet; the full set and the swap into the creator are T-399.

- **Script:** `game/tools/build_sidewalk_car_sample.py` (tests: `game/tools/test_build_sidewalk_car_sample.py`).
  - `sidewalk --out <png>`: draws one 128 x 64 ground tile (2 x 2 units, the road tiles' diamond) for free: 1 x 1 unit slabs as a checker of the buildings' two pavement greys (`build_creator_buildings_art.py` `PAV`, `PAV_DARK`) with thin joints, light speckle and a few light cracks; the slabs repeat every 2 units, so tiles join without a seam. A raised curb runs along the tile's south-east edge (world +x, the screen's lower-right edge, the side that meets the road): the surface is drawn lifted `--curb-px` art px (3, under the 0.1-unit curb rule), so between it and the tile's edge the curb shows: a lighter curb-stone top band, a lit lip broken by highlight pixels, the face in two shades, a near-black gutter line and a strip of road-dark ground against the asphalt. `draw_sidewalk(curb=False)` is the same tile without the curb (an inner tile).
  - `crater --out <png>`: draws the meteor crater for free (`draw_crater`), a 128 x 64 ground decal, transparent round it: the floor is drawn `depth_px` lower than the ground, so where it does not show the far inner wall does (bands of cut asphalt, then earth darkening downwards); round the hole a ring of broken, lifted asphalt slabs (lit on the far side, a near-black lip on the near side), a scorched band dithered out at its edge, glowing magenta cracks with stepped halos, and meteor fragments with glowing seams on a stepped purple glow on the floor. Colours: the road tile's asphalt and the boulder's rock and glow.
  - `car --raw <png> --out <png> [--mirror]`: cleans a PixelLab car picture for free (alpha 0 or 255, a flat border background removed, loose specks and detached blobs dropped, at most 30 colours by `reduce_palette.py`, optionally mirrored, which turns top left into top right and bottom left into bottom right).
  - `preview --sidewalk <png> [--car <png>] [--crater <png>] --out-dir <folder>`: the composed preview: a road along world y of the game's own tiles (`y_line_r_a0`, `y_line_l_a0`), the curb tiles against it, plain sidewalk tiles behind, the creator's small building (`creator_final/small_0.png`) and a boulder (`boulder_a.png`), the car parked at the curb, the crater on the road; dark and light backgrounds, 3x nearest neighbour (`preview_dark.png`, `preview_light.png`, `preview_side_by_side.png`), plus close-ups of the curb (`preview_curb_zoom.png`, `tile_curb_zoom.png`) and of the crater on asphalt, on the sidewalk and alone (`crater_zoom.png`, 6x).
- **Files:** `art-archive/T-397/`: the kept sidewalk `sidewalk_curb_se.png`, the kept car `parked_car_ne.png`, the kept crater `meteor_crater.png`, every style-loop version in `style-loop/` (with each version's preview), the PixelLab raw and its record in `raw/`, the request in `requests/`, the briefs in `briefs/`, the final previews in `screenshots/`.

## The full set (T-399)

- **Sidewalk and curbs:** the 47 tiles of `game/art/ground/sidewalk_tiles.png` (+ `.json`) are drawn by `game/tools/build_street_pieces.py` (`build`, `check`), from T-397's tile code; T-399's style loop (sidewalk, 3 rounds, kept at 8/10) changed `draw_tile` there by script (the hand: the asset-generation agent, in the loop) so the curbs join at outer and inner corners with no notch. `sheet --out <png>` writes the sheet as `build` draws it, `preview-sidewalks --sheet <png> --out-dir <dir>` lays it round a crossroads of the game's road tiles (with 6x close-ups of the four corners). Curbs are drawn only: no height step (the board).
- **Meteor craters:** the three looks (`game/art/craters/crater_<n>.png`, `game/data/level/craters.json`) are drawn by `build_street_pieces.py`'s own crater function (a copy of T-397's `draw_crater`, refined by script in T-399's loop, 4 rounds, kept at 8/10; T-397's script is unchanged). `crater-strip --out <png>` writes the three in one row, `preview-craters --strip <png> --out-dir <dir>` shows each on asphalt, sidewalk and gravel next to a boulder.
- **Cars:** `game/tools/build_street_cars.py` makes a car's four facings (north = top right, east = bottom right, south = bottom left, west = top left) from two PixelLab pictures, one facing up the screen and one facing down it, picked from one `/generate-8-rotations-v3` call (2 generations) on a pixflux picture of the car (1 generation; parked car 1 reuses T-397's). The other two facings are mirrors. Commands: `rotations-request` (the request), `clean` (T-397's clean-up), `repaint-front --nose bottom-left|bottom-right` (the board, 2026-10-30: PixelLab paints the headlights red; this greys the red lamps on the nose's side, unlit, and leaves the rear lamps red), `strip` (one row of four 128 x 128 frames with one shared palette of at most 30 colours), `preview`, `install` (cuts a strip into `game/art/props/<model>_<rotation>_art.png`, anchors 29 px above the lowest row, points `props.json` at them), `check` (the installed files match a fresh install from the kept strips `art-archive/T-399/<model>_facings.png`). Free touch-ups written by the asset-generation agent in the style loop (the hand of the script fixes): `touch_up` (duller paint, a dark outline, flat areas, rear lamps) and, for the broken cars, `wreck` (cracked glass, crushed and dented panels, scorch, rust, a flat tyre or a missing wheel, dusty or dark grey paint, rubble cleared). `rotate-request` is kept but not used: a `/rotate` by direction names turned the car the wrong way.
