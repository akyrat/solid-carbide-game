# Sidewalk and parked car art (T-397 sample)

(The board's decision: Level design, Decisions, "Sidewalks and parked cars in the level creator"; the look: Visual style, Decisions, "Sidewalks and parked cars".)

T-397 is the sample for the board's review: one sidewalk tile with its curb and one parked car facing. Nothing is in `game/art/` yet; the full set and the swap into the creator are T-399.

- **Script:** `game/tools/build_sidewalk_car_sample.py` (tests: `game/tools/test_build_sidewalk_car_sample.py`).
  - `sidewalk --out <png>`: draws one 128 x 64 ground tile (2 x 2 units, the road tiles' diamond) for free: 1 x 1 unit slabs as a checker of the buildings' two pavement greys (`build_creator_buildings_art.py` `PAV`, `PAV_DARK`) with thin joints, light speckle and a few light cracks; the slabs repeat every 2 units, so tiles join without a seam. A raised curb runs along the tile's south-east edge (world +x, the screen's lower-right edge, the side that meets the road): the whole surface is drawn lifted `--curb-px` art px (3, under the 0.1-unit curb rule), so between it and the tile's edge the curb's face shows: a lighter curb-stone top band (0.25 units), a lit lip, the face in two shades and a dark gutter line against the asphalt. `draw_sidewalk(curb=False)` is the same tile without the curb (an inner tile).
  - `car --raw <png> --out <png> [--mirror]`: cleans a PixelLab car picture for free (alpha 0 or 255, a flat border background removed, loose specks and detached blobs dropped, at most 30 colours by `reduce_palette.py`, optionally mirrored, which turns top left into top right and bottom left into bottom right).
  - `preview --sidewalk <png> [--car <png>] --out-dir <folder>`: the composed preview: a road along world y of the game's own tiles (`y_line_r_a0`, `y_line_l_a0`), the curb tiles against it, plain sidewalk tiles behind, the creator's small building (`creator_final/small_0.png`) and a boulder (`boulder_a.png`), the car parked at the curb; dark and light backgrounds, 3x nearest neighbour (`preview_dark.png`, `preview_light.png`, `preview_side_by_side.png`).
- **Files:** `art-archive/T-397/`: the kept sidewalk `sidewalk_curb_se.png`, the kept car `parked_car_ne.png`, every style-loop version in `style-loop/`, the PixelLab raws and records in `raw/`, the requests in `requests/`, the briefs in `briefs/`, the previews in `screenshots/`.
