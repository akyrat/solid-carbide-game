## The board's level is the game's map (T-372)

(Level design, Decisions, "The board's own level becomes the game's map"; Content, "The game's map".)

- **The file the game plays:** `data/level/kaiju_city_map.json` (`res://data/level/kaiju_city_map.json`): a byte-for-byte copy of the board's level **Kaiju City**, made in the level creator and saved in the creator's levels folder as `data/levels/kaiju_city.json` in the main checkout. That original is the board's working file: it is never written, moved or committed; the copy in `data/level/` is the one in the repo. The copy is never edited to fix it: a problem in it goes to the Project Lead (T-372's result notes list what was found).
- **Where it is chosen:** `GameFlow.GAME_MAP_PATH` (`scripts/game/game_flow.gd`). `start_run()` (the main menu's Start, the tutorial slides' Start and the pause screen's Restart) passes it to `GameFlow.add_run(..., layout_path)`, the same path the level creator's Play uses for its levels (T-239), so the run builds its map, rooftops, ramps, props, boulders, craters, painted ground and the enemies' navigation from the file, and starts the car on its `spawn` (T-280).
- **The elevation sample map stays:** `run.gd`'s default map (`ELEVATION_LAYOUT_PATH`, `data/level/elevation_sample_map.json`) is unchanged, so a run made without a map (the tests, `scenes/run/run.tscn` opened with F6) and the creator keep using it.
- **To play another map from Start:** copy its file into `data/level/` under a new name and change `GAME_MAP_PATH`. Check it first, from the repo root: `python game/tools/check_level.py game/data/level/<file>.json` and `python game/tools/check_height_data.py game/data/level/<file>.json` (both print `OK`).
- **Tests:** `tests/unit/game/test_game_map.gd`: Start (and Restart) play the file; the map holds the file's buildings, rooftops, ramps, props and boulders and draws its own ground; the car starts on the spawn point, on a road, at street level; minions spawn and reach the parked car; a scripted chase with the car parked on the spawn and on every rooftop's centre (every one reached by minions except `roof-13`, which has no ramp up: `NO_WAY_UP`); the kaiju arrives at boss time on open street and walks to the car; its meteors find spots; gift packs land on open road. A report test prints where the minions' spawn ring finds no street (the car on some rooftops), and another line lists the kaiju alert signs drawn on the map. `tests/unit/level_creator/test_creator_end_to_end.gd` checks that Start plays `GAME_MAP_PATH` and that the sample map is still there.
- **Screenshots:** `tests/fixtures/game_map_run_demo.gd` with `game_map_run_{start,wide,rooftop,kaiju,gift}.tscn` (a run from Start; the car parked), e.g. `bash game/tools/screenshot.sh res://tests/fixtures/game_map_run_start.tscn art-archive/T-372/screenshots/start.png 90`. Results: `art-archive/T-372/screenshots/`.

## A ramp onto several rooftops (T-549)

(Level design, Decisions, "A ramp may climb onto several rooftops"; Content, "A ramp onto several rooftops (task T-549)".)

- **The rule:** a ramp (not a jump ramp) is valid when its top end lies wholly on the side it climbs of one area at its `top_height` (as before), or, when no one area holds it, when the sides on that line of the areas at its `top_height` that share a stretch of it cover the whole top end together, side by side with no gap. Its `"platform"` then is empty or names one of them. A ramp whose top meets no area at its height, or only part of its top end is covered, is still refused (`ramp '<id>': climbs to unknown area '' (its top meets no area at height <h>)`); naming an area off its top is refused as before (`doesn't touch`). Nothing else about the format changes, so every level saved before stays valid.
- **The checks:** `tools/check_height_data.py` (`top_areas`, used by `check_data`, so also by `check_level.py`). Kaiju City's `ramp-2` (3 to 6, the west sides of `roof-6` and `roof-7`) and `ramp-13` (3 to 6, the east sides of `roof-3` and `roof-6`) pass with `"platform": ""`, as the board saved them.
- **The game:** unchanged. `GroundHeight` reads a ramp's heights and rectangle, not its `"platform"` (it keeps the field, nothing reads it), so the car drives up such a ramp onto whichever rooftop it reaches; the rooftops' seam at the same height is no edge. The elevation layer breaks each rooftop's parapet where the ramp meets it (`edge_gaps`, by rectangle), so both are drawn open there.
- **The level creator:** `LevelDocument.top_areas` is the same rule in GDScript. `ramp_target` (used by `_relink`) still links a ramp to the one area that holds its top end; when several cover it together, it links the first of them in the file's order (`ramp-2` to `roof-6`, `ramp-13` to `roof-3`), and `_relink` keeps a link to any one of them as still right (`_still_climbs`). So a level saved from the creator names a rooftop again, and one saved with an empty link loads and checks as it is. `LevelSnapping.ramp_snap` counts the sides of same-height rooftops on one line, end to end, as one side (`_joined_side`), so a ramp too wide for either rooftop snaps onto both.
- **Tests:** `tools/test_check_height_data.py` (`TestRampOntoSeveralRooftops`: the fixture `tests/fixtures/height/ramp_onto_two_rooftops.json`, ramp-2 and ramp-13 with their rooftops copied from Kaiju City; either rooftop named; a top meeting nothing, partly covered, or at another height refused; the board's `data/levels/kaiju_city.json` passes both checks, skipped when the file is absent), `tests/unit/test_ramp_onto_two_rooftops.gd` (the car drives up each ramp onto each of its two rooftops and ends there at height 6), `tests/unit/level_creator/test_creator_ramp_onto_two_rooftops.gd` (`top_areas`, relink, snapping, save and checks, and the board's Kaiju City loaded, relinked and saved into a temporary folder only).
