## Enemies on the rooftops and ramps (T-207)

Task T-207 (Enemy Behavior Agent): on a map with rooftops and ramps (`map.elevation`, the elevation map the run plays on, T-206) the minions and the kaiju stand on the ground's heights, are stopped by rooftop edges and find their way up and down the ramps to the car (Enemies, Decisions, "Enemies follow the car up the ramps"; what they do: `docs/enemies.md`, Content, "Enemies on the rooftops and ramps"). On the MVP map (`run.use_elevation_map = false`) none of it runs and they walk as before.

**How it is built** (`scripts/enemies/`):

- `enemy_height.gd` (static functions, no state): the rules on the ground's heights (`GroundHeight`, `scripts/level/ground_height.gd`). `MAX_STEP` (0.6 units): a move whose ground height changes more than that is refused, so every rooftop edge (3 or 6 units) and the high part of a ramp's sides is a wall, and a ramp (0.25 a unit) is not. `fits(ground, units, radius)`: the ground under four points of the body's circle is within `MAX_STEP` of its centre's, so a body keeps off an edge. `limit_motion(ground, from, motion, radius)` cuts a move into `SUB_STEP` (0.25 unit) pieces, stops at the first refused one and slides the rest along the free axis (every edge runs north-south or east-west); it returns the moves to make and the edge's normal. `at_car_level(enemy_ground, car_height)`: within `LEVEL_TOLERANCE` (1 unit).
- `minion.gd` and `kaiju.gd`: `ground` (null on a flat map), `ground_height()`, and `_move(motion)`, which every walk and knockback step goes through: `limit_motion` first, then `move_and_collide` as before; an edge that stopped it becomes its wall normal, so the detour round obstacles works on edges too, and a knockback loses the part pushing over an edge. `contact_step()` returns false when the car is not at its level. `follower`: its `enemy_path_follower.gd`.
- `enemy_navigation.gd`: the pathfinding. An `AStar2D` over a grid of 2 x 2 unit cells covering the map (120 x 120, about 12 250 open points): cells in a building (grown by 0.5) or on one of the map's boulders are left out, and two neighbouring cells (sides and corners) are linked only when their ground heights differ by `MAX_STEP` or less (a corner link only when all four cells of its square do). Built once per map in about 27 ms and kept on the map (`EnemyNavigationScript.for_map(map)`, `map.get_meta("enemy_navigation")`), shared by the minions (since T-419 the kaiju has its own, planned for its size: "A way planned for each body's size" below); a new run's map builds a new one. `find_path(from, to)` returns the cell centres, with each climb along a ramp replaced by two points on the ramp's centre line 1.5 units beyond its ends (`ramp_end()`), so every enemy goes up a ramp along its middle. `surface_at(units)` is the ground's area index (`-1` for the street). At most `PATHS_PER_STEP` (4) paths per step (`new_step()`, called by the spawner), so a crowd asks over a few steps. Why `AStar2D`: the heights are rectangles in the map file, so a grid at 2 units is exact (every edge lies on even units) and needs no baking; `AStarGrid2D` cannot leave out the link between two open cells, which is what an edge is here; a navigation mesh would need a mesh per level and links for the ramps.
- `enemy_path_follower.gd`: one per enemy. `target(nav, from, car_units, dt)` returns the car when the enemy is on the car's surface, otherwise the next point of its path (reached within 1 unit), asking for a new path when the car changes surface and every `REPATH_SECONDS` (1 s; each minion's clock starts at a random point). **T-380 (minions):** the spawner sets `follower.by_sight`; the follower then returns the car only while `nav.line_clear(from, car, SIGHT_HALF_WIDTH)` is true (checked every `SIGHT_SECONDS`, 0.1 s), on any surface, and otherwise a path, whose points it takes at `SIGHT_REACH` (0.5 units) instead of 1. `line_clear(a, b, half_width)` samples the line (and two lines `half_width`, 0.5, to either side) every `SIGHT_SAMPLE` (1 unit) on the grid: every cell must be open (the two end cells excepted) and within `MAX_STEP` of the one before, the same rule as the links, so a blocked line always has a path round. The kaiju (no `by_sight`) keeps the surface rule. After T-405 the grid is no longer exact (short-building platforms lie on half units): in `rough` cells `line_clear` also samples the true ground every `SIGHT_FINE` (0.25), and `find_path()` passes its points through `off_edges()`, which keeps them a body's reach off those edges (see `minions.md`, "After T-405 and T-379").
- `minion_spawner.gd`: on `world_rebuilt` it takes `map.elevation.ground_height` and the navigation (`ground`, `navigation`; both null on a flat map or with `heights_enabled = false`, a switch for tests and the soak's comparison, read when a run starts). `step()` = spawn, `move_step(dt)` (walk each minion to its follower's target, contact damage; tests call it alone), remove far ones, draw. Spawn points must be on the street (`is_free_spot`, `elevation.is_street`). Minions push apart only from minions within 1 unit of their height. A minion whose follower has a path is removed only beyond `ON_PATH_KEEP_UNITS` (120) from the car, others a screen beyond the visible one as before. Sprites and coins are placed lifted by their ground height (`ISO * position + (0, -height * 16)`) and get `depth.z_index_for(..., ground_h)`. `last_step_usec` is the last step's time (the soak reads it).
- `kaiju_boss.gd`: the same `ground`, `navigation`, `heights_enabled`, follower (`target(..., budgeted = false)`), street-only arrival, lifted drawing.
- `enemy_depth.gd`: `z_index_for(centre, radius, height, ground_h = 0.0)` lifts the screen shape by `ground_h` and passes it to `elevation.z_under_areas()`, so an enemy is over the rooftop it stands on and under a higher one in front of it.

**Tests:** `tests/unit/enemies/test_enemies_on_heights.gd`: the navigation (one per map, shared, rebuilt per run, none on the MVP map; no link climbing more than a step, every drop link one-way (since T-226); no link up any rooftop edge; buildings and boulders closed; a path from the street to height 6 goes up both ramps in order), the follower (straight on the car's surface, a path otherwise, no new path before a second), the path budget, keeping minions on their way to a ramp; minions from four street spots reach a car parked on each height-6 rooftop by both ramps within 40 s (about 15 to 27 s), never changing height more than a step in one step; with the drop links left out (T-226), minions on the rooftops come down a ramp to a car on the street; minions driven at every rooftop edge (each side, every 3 units, ramps left out) neither climb it from below nor step off it from above; a knockback does not push one over an edge; spawning on the street; drawing lifted and in the depth order (over the roof it stands on, under it from behind, under a higher roof, over a ramp it is on); contact damage only at the car's level (from below an edge, from above, a car in the air); the kaiju climbing to a height-3 car by the ramp, stopped by an edge, hitting only at its level, arriving on the street.

## Enemies drop off ledges (T-226)

Task T-226 (Enemy Behavior Agent): going down, the minions and the kaiju drop off a rooftop's edge to chase the car instead of walking round to a ramp; going up still needs a ramp, and an edge still blocks them from below (Enemies, Decisions, "Enemies jump down off ledges"; what they do: `docs/enemies.md`, Content, "Enemies drop off ledges"). Nothing changes on the MVP map.

**Falling art (T-227):** `game/art/minion/minion_fall_s/se/e/ne/n.png` (8 frames of 48 x 48, 10 a second; the other three directions mirror them) are the minion's falling sheets, one PixelLab `/animate-with-text-v3` call each on the minion's rotation; `minion_sprite.gd` plays them through T-226's hook, so the placeholder fall is used only if a sheet is missing. Build: `python game/tools/build_minion_fall_sheets.py [dir ...]` (snaps colours to the walk sheets' palette, clears the south sheet's chin streak, reads `art-archive/T-227/fall_<dir>/`; test `game/tools/test_build_minion_fall_sheets.py`). Check: `python game/tools/style_rules.py art game/art/minion/minion_fall_e.png --size 48x48 --frames 8 --max-colors 49`. The kaiju still uses the placeholder.

**How it is built** (`scripts/enemies/`):

- `enemy_navigation.gd`: after T-207's links, `_add_drops()` adds a one-way link (`connect_points(k, e, false)`) from every open cell off the ramps to each of its four side neighbours that is open, off the ramps and more than `MAX_STEP` lower (238 on the elevation map; `drop_links`). Nothing links back up. `is_drop(a, b)`: two path points a cell apart with `b` more than a step lower (a ramp's top and foot points are much farther apart, so a ramp is never a drop). `build_for_map(map, drops = true)` builds a navigation that is not kept on the map; `drops = false` is T-207's grid (the tests' "before").
- `enemy_path_follower.gd`: `dropping(nav, from, body_h)` is true while the target is the point below an edge and the enemy is still above it, or the target is the point on an edge, `DROP_NEAR` (3 units) away or less, and the path drops from there (3 units so the kaiju, which keeps its 2-unit radius off an edge, still reaches that point).
- `enemy_height.gd`: `can_step`, `limit_motion` and `_slide` take the body's height (`body_h`, NAN: the ground's, as before) and `drop`. Ground more than `MAX_STEP` above the body is always a wall; with `drop` a step down of any size is allowed and the body need not fit. The body's height is followed piece by piece (`_next_height`), so once it is over lower ground it stays in the air at the height it left.
- `enemy_fall.gd` (new): one per enemy (`fall`). `start(h)`, `step(dt, ground_h)` (the car's `GRAVITY`, 12 units/s², from rest; true on the landing step), `body_height(ground_h)`, `landings`.
- `minion.gd` and `kaiju.gd`: `may_drop` (set each step by the spawner or the boss from `follower.dropping()`), `fall`, `body_height()`, `is_falling()`. A walk moves with `drop = may_drop or falling`; after it `_fall_step(dt, h0)` starts a fall when the body has walked out over ground more than a step under its height before the move, and steps the fall. A knockback moves with `drop` only while already falling, so it never takes an enemy over an edge. `contact_step()` compares the car's height with `body_height()`. `set_position_units()` ends any fall.
- `minion_spawner.gd`, `kaiju_boss.gd`: set `may_drop`, draw at `body_height()` (position lift and `depth.z_index_for(..., body_height())`), call `sprite.set_falling(is_falling())`. Pushing apart uses `body_height()`. `drops_total` counts the minions' drops (the soak prints it).
- `minion_sprite.gd`, `kaiju_sprite.gd`: `set_falling(on)`, `advance(delta)`, `animation()` ("walk", "fall", "land"), `anim_transform()`. The placeholder fall is made by script from the walk frames: the walk cycle at `FLAIL_SPEED` times its speed (the legs flail) and the drawing tipping from side to side (`FALL_TILT`, `FALL_WOBBLE_HZ`; the minion also stretched by `FALL_STRETCH`); after landing a squash (`LAND_SQUASH` easing back over `LAND_SECONDS`). The kaiju draws no ground shadow while it falls. **The art hook (T-227):** falling sheets in `art/minion/minion_fall_<dir>.png` and `art/kaiju/kaiju_fall_<dir>.png` (`FALL_SHEET_DIR`; `<dir>` is e, se, s, ne, n, the other three mirrored as the walk sheets), frames of the walk's `FRAME_SIZE` in one row, as many as the sheet is wide, played at `FALL_FPS` from the moment the enemy leaves the edge. When a direction's sheet exists it is drawn as it is, without the scripted tilt; when it does not, the placeholder. The landing squash applies to both.
- Fix found on the way: a mirrored walk frame (west, south-west, north-west) was drawn one frame width to the right of the enemy (Godot draws a negative-width rect from its x to x + width, flipped), so the kaiju's art stood beside its shadow and health bar. Both sprites now put the rect at `-size / 2` either way.

**Tests:** `tests/unit/enemies/test_enemies_drop_off_ledges.gd`: one-way drop links on every edge (none with `drops = false`); a path from roof-nw-6 to a street car drops once and is much shorter than the way round, a path up still goes by both ramps with no drop; four minions on roof-nw-6 reach a car on the street beside it by dropping, without a ramp, sooner than the same minions on T-207's grid (both times printed), never in a building and never climbing; a minion with `may_drop` still cannot climb an edge from below; without a drop on its path a minion does not step off; minions on the street still go up by the ramps; minions from every rooftop to cars round them never end up in a building; a falling minion's height only goes down, its drawing is lifted by it and in the depth order at it at every step, and the falling animation plays exactly while it falls ("land" right after); the sprites' animation states; the fall's time under the car's gravity; contact damage while falling only at the height in the air; a knockback never takes a minion over an edge; the kaiju drops off roof-nw-6 to a car below. T-207's tests on the grid were changed to the new rule (no link climbs; T-207's ramp-down test runs with the drops left out).

**Recordings** (`tests/fixtures/enemies_drop_demo.gd`: T-207's fixture, then the enemies keep chasing the parked car live, one step per physics frame; `stop_at_seconds` freezes them for a still, `quit_after_seconds` ends the scene): `"$GODOT_BIN" --path game --fixed-fps 50 --write-movie C:/abs/drop/f.png res://tests/fixtures/enemies_drop_gif.tscn` (six minions on roof-nw-6, the car on the street below), `enemies_drop_kaiju.tscn` (the kaiju), `enemies_drop_falling.tscn` (a still, 2.1 s in). ## A way planned for each body's size (T-419)

Enemies, Decisions, "The kaiju only takes paths it fits through". Before, every enemy used the minions' grid (buildings grown by the minion's radius), so the kaiju (radius 2) was sent through gaps it does not fit and to points 1 unit off a wall or a rooftop's edge, which it never reached (REACH 1): it slid on the wall and re-planned every second, mostly with the car on a rooftop.

**How it is built** (`scripts/enemies/`):

- `enemy_navigation.gd`: `for_map(map, radius = -1, fit_edges = false)` keeps one navigation per body on the map (`meta_key()`: the minions' keeps `"enemy_navigation"`, others get their own key). `building_margin` is the body's radius: buildings, props and boulders close every cell whose centre is nearer (`minion_radius()` = `BASE_MARGIN` x `enemies.json` `minion.size_scale`, T-379's size; the kaiju's `RADIUS_UNITS`). The map's edge closes cells strictly nearer than the radius (the minions' cells, exactly 1 unit off it, stay open). With `fit_edges` (`edge_radius`, the kaiju) a cell where the body would hang over a rooftop's or ramp's edge (`EnemyHeight.fits`) is closed too (`unfit`; only the cells within `edge_radius` of an area's boundary are checked, `_close_unfit()`), and a drop links the last cell the body fits on above to the first below, up to `drop_span` cells apart (3 for the kaiju; `is_drop()` allows that distance). `clearance(units)` and `_fits_at(units)`: where a ramp's end point does not fit (Kaiju City's air-conditioning unit at ramp-12's top), `through_ramps()` keeps the path's own cells over that ramp. The minions' grid is unchanged (`edge_radius` 0, `drop_span` 1).
- `kaiju_boss.gd`: `for_map(map, KaijuScript.RADIUS_UNITS, true)`. Build: about 75 ms for the minions' grid and 115 ms for the kaiju's on Kaiju City, both at the run's start.
- `enemy_path_follower.gd`: while the target is a drop's landing the follower keeps its path for up to another `REPATH_SECONDS` (the kaiju's drop starts 3 units back from the edge, over a second's walk; a new path from over the edge led back up and it walked to and fro there).
- T-436: `through_ramps()` keeps a ramp's own cells when the path only skims the ramp (height change under `CLIMB_MIN`) also when the path starts on the ramp. Before, an enemy standing on a ramp's low foot was sent back to the foot's point off the ramp, from where the next path skimmed the ramp again: on the board's Kaiju City (saved 2026-10-30 21:12) the kaiju went to and fro at ramp-12's foot (about (-79, 46)) because its way from there to roof-1 now runs north along x -77, over the ramp's foot cells (ramp-12's own top is closed to it by the AC unit prop at its top). Tests: `test_kaiju_path_size.gd`, the two "ramp's foot" tests on a made-up map.

**Tests:** `tests/unit/enemies/test_kaiju_path_size.gd` (the board's level, `res://data/levels/kaiju_city.json`, skipped with a message when the untracked copy is missing): one navigation per body size; over 50 seeded start/target pairs every waypoint of the kaiju's path is at least 2 units off every building, prop and boulder, and none hangs over an edge; a 3 and a 3.5-unit gap are used by the minions and never by the kaiju, a 6-unit gap by the kaiju through its middle; the kaiju walks round two buildings to a car on a rooftop within 30 s without staying within 1 unit of one spot for 2 s, and reaches a car on roof-1 from ten street spots the same way. `test_enemies_on_heights.gd`: the kaiju has its own navigation. `test_enemies_drop_off_ledges.gd`'s kaiju drop covers the drop span and the follower's commitment.

**Picture:** `bash game/tools/draw_kaiju_nav.sh <out.png> [level] [car x] [car y]` draws a level with the cells the kaiju's grid closes and the minions' keeps (orange), and the kaiju's paths from five street spots to the car before (red, the minions' grid) and now (cyan).
