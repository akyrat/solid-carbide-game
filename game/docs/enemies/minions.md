## Minions

**Health drops (T-220).** `data/enemies.json` `health_drops` (`drop_chance` 0.225, `restore_hearts` 1, read through `EnemyData.health_drops()`). When a minion dies, `MinionSpawner._on_minion_died()` rolls `roll_health_drop()` (its own roll, apart from the coins') and calls `spawn_health_pickup_at(units)`, which adds `scripts/enemies/health_pickup.gd` (an `Area2D` on the pickups layer, mask 2, group `health_pickups`, built like the coin; `spawner.health_pickups` lists them, as `spawner.coins` lists coins) with its drawing `health_pickup_sprite.gd` (the final canister art `art/effects/health_canister.png`, 14 x 10, T-221: the board-approved PixelLab sample `art-archive/T-221/canister_raw.png` with three free script fixes by `python game/tools/build_health_canister.py`, no PixelLab: the white mark redrawn as a clean plus, the darkest red moved away from the old logo's red so no colour is within 45 of it, and scaled down to about a coin's size; 6 colours; test `game/tools/test_build_health_canister.py` and `test_canister_art_is_not_old_logo_red` in `test_health_drops.gd`; screenshots `art-archive/T-221/screenshots/`), at the ground height and in the depth order like a coin. Driving over it calls `RunState.heal_car(restore_hearts)` (capped at the maximum, no combo effect) and frees it. The kaiju drops none. Cleared by `clear()` on a new run. The chance is 0.225 (T-371: 1.5 times the 0.15 of T-333, which halved from 0.3, T-277 halved it from 0.6; the coins' stays 0.6) and one pickup heals 1 heart (was 0.3 of the maximum). Tests: `tests/unit/enemies/test_health_drops.gd`. Screenshot: `tests/fixtures/minions_demo.tscn` (two canisters beside the coins).

Task T-044 (Enemy Behavior Agent): the MVP minion as Enemies, Decisions describes it, with T-039's numbers (`data/enemies.json` through `EnemyData`, "Game data" above). What it does: `docs/enemies.md`, Content, "The minion". Placeholders for now (a light green four-legged lizard drawn in code, gold coins); the final art comes from separate art tasks.

**See it:** start the game (F5) and press Start, or open `scenes/run/run.tscn` and press F6. Minions start coming in from the screen's edges at once and walk towards the car. Screenshots, from the repo root: `bash game/tools/screenshot.sh res://tests/fixtures/minions_demo.tscn screenshots/minions.png 20` (minions and two coins around the car in the gravel square) and `bash game/tools/screenshot.sh res://tests/fixtures/minions_draw_order_demo.tscn screenshots/minions_draw_order.png 20` (the car on the road beside a block of skyscrapers and two boulders, minions in front of and behind them). Both start the run from the main menu (fixture script `tests/fixtures/minions_demo.gd`). Copies are in `art-archive/T-044/screenshots/`.

**How it is built** (`scripts/enemies/`):

- `minion_spawner.gd`: the run's minions. The run scene adds it as `run.minions` (child `Minions`) and calls `bind(run)`. It moves everything in `step(dt)`, once per physics step from `_physics_process` (pausable, so the pause screen and the level-up pause stop the minions and their damage), and does nothing once the run is over. On `run.world_rebuilt` (a new run) it removes every minion and coin and follows the new car and map. `enabled = false` stops it (tests).
- `minion.gd`: one minion, a `CharacterBody2D` in the run's `PhysicsWorld` (flat world pixels, 16 per unit, like the car) on layer 3 (`enemies`) with mask 1 (obstacles): a circle of `radius_units()` (`BASE_RADIUS_UNITS` 0.5 times `size_scale`, 1.0 since T-379). It slides along walls, buildings and boulders and walks round one it meets head-on (`detour_side`, picked at random per minion); it never pushes or blocks the car. In the groups `enemies` and `minions`.
- `minion_sprite.gd`: the minion's final art (T-068): 8-frame walk sheets in `art/minion/` (`minion_s/se/e/ne/n.png`, 8 frames of 48 x 48 side by side; the other three directions mirror se, e and ne), drawn at scale 0.5 (16 screen pixels per unit), the direction chosen from the way it walks, 10 frames a second, each minion at a random phase. It stops with the game's pause. No shadow under it (board tweak, 2026-10-15). Falls back to the old placeholder shape if a sheet is missing. Sheets are built by `tools/build_minion_sheets.py` from `art-archive/T-068/`; `tools/fix_minion_frames.py` holds the free pixel fixes.
- `coin_sprite.gd`: the placeholder coin drawing, in the isometric view.
- `coin.gd`: a coin, an `Area2D` on layer 4 (`pickups`) with mask 2 (the car).
- `enemy_depth.gd`: the minions' and coins' place in the buildings' depth order.
- `enemy_ground_index.gd`: the enemies' quick lookup of the ground's heights and surfaces (T-444, "Performance" below).

**Spawning (T-443, on a timer):** one minion every `spawn_interval()` seconds, however many are already around (no wanted count any more: that rewarded not killing them). `spawn_interval()` is `EnemyData.spawn_interval(state.time_elapsed(), spawn_multiplier)`: the rate (1 / interval) grows in a straight line from 1 / `spawn_interval_at_start_seconds` (1.2 s) at second 0 to 1 / `spawn_interval_at_boss_time_seconds` (0.3 s) at `growth_until_seconds` (180, 3:00, T-522; was 420, 7:00), then is held (0.48 s at 1:30; about 375 minions by 3:00, was 875 over the 7 minutes with no combo). The first comes at once (`_spawn_clock` starts full, also after `clear()`). Each goes at a random point on the ring `spawn_margin_units` (3) beyond the edge of the visible screen (the camera's view, so it follows the zoom), inside the map, on the street and clear of buildings and boulders (`is_free_spot()`; 12 tries per step, else the spawn waits for the next step, so a blocked edge delays a spawn by a step, not an interval). The only thing that holds the timer is the safety ceiling `max_minions_alive` (100, `max_minions_alive()`, for the frame rate: the informal measurement in T-443's result notes put `last_step_usec` at about 0.2 ms per minion before T-444; "Performance" below has the numbers since). Intervals missing or not above 0 in the data are reported and replaced by `DEFAULT_SPAWN_INTERVAL` (0.5 s). `spawned_total` counts the timer's spawns since the last `clear()` (tests, the soak). A minion a whole screen beyond the visible screen (the car drove away from it) is removed without coins; the timer keeps bringing new ones in at the edge. All the numbers are proposals for the playtest, in `data/enemies.json` `spawning` with their reasons in its `_comment`.

**Performance (T-444):** how fast the game runs with a crowd of minions is measured by `game/tools/minion_crowd_bench.sh` (`minion_crowd_bench.gd`, headless). From the repo root:

```
bash game/tools/minion_crowd_bench.sh [--counts=50,100,200,300] [--seconds=6] [--warmup=1] [--seed=444] [--breakdown]
```

It starts the game's own run on Kaiju City (`GameFlow.GAME_MAP_PATH`), parks the car where the run puts it, and for each count holds that many minions alive round the car (placed 4 to 30 units from it, topped up every frame, too tough for the weapons to kill; the timer's ceiling is set to the count for the measure only, nothing is saved). After `--warmup` seconds it measures `--seconds` and prints, per count, the average, 99th percentile and worst of: the whole frame (Godot runs with `--fixed-fps 50`, so each frame is exactly one of the game's 50 physics steps a second and one idle frame; 20 ms is the whole budget), the scripts' physics step (every node's `_physics_process`, between two bracket nodes that run first and last), and the minion spawner's own step (`last_step_usec`). `--breakdown` also times parts of the work apart (separation, sight checks, heights, depth order, the edge check). Headless, so drawing on screen is not in it. Exit codes: 0 measured, 1 the run did not start, 2 bad arguments or no Godot. The machine's other work moves the numbers: run it more than once and compare the spread.

What T-444 changed, with the same behaviour (the result notes have the before and after numbers): the minions' step was about 0.35 ms per minion on Kaiju City and is about 0.07 ms now.
- `enemy_ground_index.gd`: `GroundHeight.height_at` and `area_index_at` tested every rooftop, short building and ramp of the map, and a minion asks them many times a step (its height, the edges round each move, its surface). The index puts each area in the 4-unit buckets it touches, once per ground (kept in the ground's meta, rebuilt when its platform or ramp list is replaced or changes size), and answers from one bucket through `GroundHeight.height_among` / `area_among`, which give the same numbers. `enemy_height.gd` and `enemy_navigation.gd` read every height through it; `minion.ground` and `navigation.ground` stay the map's `GroundHeight`. The biggest saving.
- `enemy_depth.gd`: the depth order tested every building, boulder and prop sprite and raised area for each minion each step. It now files them in a grid of 64-pixel screen cells once per map (rebuilt when a list changes: a meteor boulder) and tests only those in the cells the minion's shape touches, with the same rules, so the z_index is the same.
- `minion_spawner.gd` `_separation()`: pushing apart compared every pair (300 minions, 90 000 pairs). Now the minions go into cells as wide as the push's reach and each is compared with its own and the eight cells round it, in the same order, so the pushes are the same sums (`_separation_all_pairs()` keeps the old rule for the tests).
- `enemy_height.gd` `limit_motion()`: a move whose whole reach is over flat street (no area in the index's buckets there) is allowed at once, as the piece-by-piece check would allow it.
- `enemy_navigation.gd` `surface_at()` keeps its last answer (every minion asks for the car's surface each step), and the spawner asks `follower.dropping()` only when the follower has a path (it is false without one).

Tests: `tests/unit/enemies/test_minion_crowd_performance.gd` (each shortcut against the work it replaced, on Kaiju City, and a crowd still closing in on the car).

**The combo raises spawning (T-085, T-443):** every call of `spawn_interval()` sets `run.minions.spawn_multiplier` from `RunState.combo_spawn_multiplier()` (`ComboData.spawn_multiplier(combo)`: 1.0 with no combo, up to the data's `max_spawn_multiplier` 1.75 in `data/combo.json`), which multiplies the spawn rate (divides the interval), so the timer follows the combo live. **The switch:** `run.minions.combo_raises_spawning` (a variable of `minion_spawner.gd`, default `true`). Set it to `false` and the combo changes nothing: the multiplier stays 1.0. To flip it for a playtest, change the default on that line, or set it from a test or playtest scene. It is a setting, not a key and not player-facing text. Tests: `tests/unit/enemies/test_minions.gd`.

**Movement:** straight towards the car's centre at `move_speed` (4.2 units/s, 30% slower than the old 6, T-443), plus a small push away from other minions closer than 1.2 units (`Minion.SEPARATION_UNITS`), so a crowd spreads round the car.

**Contact damage:** a minion touches the car when its circle overlaps the car's 1 x 1 unit body (`touches_car()`). While touching, it hits as soon as it touches: `RunState.damage_car(contact_damage)` (1 heart; this also cuts the combo) and `car.apply_knockback(away from the minion, knockback)` (8 units/s). Since T-334 the car's invulnerability spaces the hits: while `RunState.is_invulnerable()` (1 s after any hit) `contact_step()` does nothing, no hit and no knockback, for every minion (and the kaiju), so a swarm takes one heart a second at most and the car can drive out of it. `contact_damage_interval` stays as an optional per-minion repeat on top, 0 (none) by default; the data's 0.5 s is gone since T-333.

**Damaging a minion (for the weapon tasks, T-041 and T-042):**

```
func take_damage(amount: float) -> void
```

Call it on a minion node. `amount` is in the same points as `health` (4, `data/enemies.json`). Zero or negative amounts, and calls on a dead minion, are ignored. At 0 health the minion dies: its `died(minion, position_units)` signal fires once, it leaves the groups `enemies` and `minions` at once, its drawing goes, and the node is freed at the end of the frame (so calling it from an `Area2D` callback is safe). To find targets: `get_tree().get_nodes_in_group("enemies")` (the minions and, once it has appeared, the kaiju: see "The kaiju") or `run.minions.minions` (the live minions). Each has `position_units()` (flat world, units; `position` is in flat world pixels), `health`, `max_health` and `is_dead()`. Bodies are on layer 3, so a projectile's `Area2D` finds them with a mask of 4. Check `is_dead()` before damaging one found earlier in the same frame.

**Pushing a minion (T-167, for the windshield wiper):**

```
func apply_knockback(direction: Vector2, strength: float) -> void
func is_knocked_back() -> bool
```

Call it on a minion node. `direction` is in the flat world (any length; it is normalized), `strength` is the distance to push in units (flat world units, 16 pixels each; the wiper's 6 in `docs/weapons.md` is one). The minion travels that distance over `Minion.KNOCKBACK_SECONDS` (0.15 s) at an even speed, inside its own `walk()` steps, so the spawner's `step()` moves it and the pause stops it; while pushed it does not walk or separate, then walks on as before. It moves with the same `move_and_collide` and mask 1 as its walking: walls, buildings, the map's edge and boulders stop it, it slides along them, and the part of the push into an obstacle is dropped, so it is never pushed into one. A second call before the push ends adds its distance to what is left and restarts the 0.15 s. Ignored when `strength` is zero or negative, `direction` is zero, or the minion is dead. `is_knocked_back()` is true while a push is moving it. Without a call, minion movement is unchanged. **Only minions can be pushed:** the kaiju (`kaiju.gd`) has no `apply_knockback`, so a weapon checks `has_method("apply_knockback")` (or the `minions` group) before pushing. (The car has its own, different `apply_knockback`, in units/s: "Knockback" above.)

**Coins:** when a minion dies, `drop_chance` (0.6) decides whether it drops coins; if so, `coins_per_drop` (2) coins of 1 each land around where it died (`roll_coin_drop()`, using the spawner's `rng`; seed it for tests). Driving over a coin (the car's body entering its circle of 0.5 units) collects it: `RunState.add_coins(1)`, and the coin and its drawing are gone. Coins stay until collected or until a new run. `run.minions.coins` lists them; `spawn_coin_at(units, value)` adds one. The spawner's `minion_killed(minion, coins)` signal fires per death with the number of coins dropped.

**Draw order:** each minion's and coin's drawing is in `run.minions.draw_root` (the node `Enemies`, just before the car's `View` in the run, Y-sorted). Every physics step each drawing is placed on its body in the isometric view and given a `z_index` by the map's rule for the car (`map.car_z_index()`, "See-through buildings" above): one under the lowest building slot or boulder sprite that covers it (it stands behind it and their outlines overlap on screen), otherwise `map.top_z_index()`, over every building. Nothing fades for a minion: the fade stays car-only, so a minion behind an opaque skyscraper is hidden by it, and one behind a building faded for the car shows through it. Where the car and a minion get the same z, the car is drawn over the minion.

**Size (T-379):** `minion.size_scale` in `data/enemies.json` (2, a whole number so the pixel edges stay hard; the kaiju is unchanged). `Minion.setup()` reads it: `radius_units()` (0.5 x scale = 1.0) is its collision circle, its reach for touching the car (`touches_car`), the radius weapons see (`hit_radius_units()`, so the gun's bullets, the molotov and the underglow hit the bigger body), the spawner's free-spot clearance and the depth order; `minion_sprite.gd` draws the 48 x 48 frames at `ART_SCALE` 0.5 x scale (one screen pixel per art pixel); the separation distance and `enemy_navigation.gd`'s margin round buildings and boulders (`building_margin`, 0.5 x scale) grow with it, so the paths keep a bigger minion off them. The body keeps the old 0.5 off a rooftop's or ramp's edge (`BASE_RADIUS_UNITS` in `_move()`), because those edges are not physics walls and the navigation's 2-unit cells put path corners 1 unit from an edge, where a 1-unit body jammed at a rooftop corner. Tests: `test_minions.gd` (size tests at the end), `test_minion_sprite.gd`, the elevation tests in `test_enemies_on_heights.gd`.

**Round ramps and corners (T-380):** the board saw minions stuck on walls going round a corner, mostly at a ramp's bottom end. Cause (reproduced in `tests/unit/enemies/test_minions_round_ramps.gd` with the old rule, `follower.by_sight = false`): on the car's surface the follower sent a minion straight at the car whatever stood between. On the street a ramp's high side stopped it head-on (`walk()`'s `detour_side` sent half of them north into the pocket where the side meets the rooftop's edge, where they stood), a building's side stopped it just short of the corner (the slide along it came out under `walk()`'s 0.5 threshold, so it detoured the other way and back, on the spot), and at the ramp's foot it stepped onto the ramp, which is another surface, got a path back to the foot's point, stepped off and walked straight again: back and forth at the bottom end. Fix: the spawner's minions walk by sight (`enemy_path_follower.gd`, `by_sight`; `enemy_navigation.gd`, `line_clear`; "Enemies on the rooftops and ramps" in `ledges-and-rooftops.md`): straight only while the grid line to the car is clear, else by a path, on any surface and any map with heights (a level creator's too). With the 1-unit body, taking a path's point at 1 unit turned it into a building's corner too early, so by sight it takes them at 0.5 (`SIGHT_REACH`). The 0.5 off ground edges (T-379) stays. A scripted chase over 14 car stops round both ramp pairs (110 minions): 8 stood still more than 2 s with the old rule, none now. Recording: `tests/fixtures/minions_round_ramps_gif.tscn` (`--fixed-fps 50 --write-movie`, then `tools/frames_to_gif.py`), the GIF in the ticket's `art-archive` screenshots folder.

**After T-405 and T-379 (merge, 2026-10-30):** on master two minions stood at the south-east short platform's corner (block@66,12: x 82.5..94.5, y 28.5..40.5, height 3). Cause: the navigation grid assumed every edge on even units; the T-405 platforms lie on half units, so the cells beside them have street heights at their centres while the platform reaches 0.5 into them. `line_clear` saw a clear line past the corner that the body (0.5 off an edge) could not walk, and the path put points 0.5 off the edge, where the body did not fit and `walk()`'s detour side pointed back along the wall. Fix (`enemy_navigation.gd`): `rough` cells (an area's edge within `BASE_MARGIN` of the cell) where `line_clear` also samples the true ground every `SIGHT_FINE` (0.25 units), and `off_edges()` on every found path, which moves a point in a rough cell 0.5 units away from each direction where higher ground lies within `EDGE_PROBE` (0.9), never a drop's landing point; grid-aligned edges are 1 unit off every centre, so their paths are unchanged. Also `minion_spawner.gd` `_despawn_far()`: walking by sight a minion has a path just to go round something on the car's own surface, so far-off minions are kept only with a path and on another surface than the car (before T-380 a path meant exactly that); `test_minions_far_behind_are_removed` failed on this.

**Not in the data:** the coin's size (0.5 unit radius), the separation distance (1.2 units) and the rule that removes minions a whole screen behind are in the code (`minion.gd`, `coin.gd`, `minion_spawner.gd`): sizes and rules of the placeholder, not balancing numbers.

## Rusty Overcoat (T-463)

With the driving power-up owned (`RunState.power_up_tier("rusty_overcoat")` above 0), `Minion.contact_step()` and `Kaiju.contact_step()` call `OvercoatContact.hit()` (`scripts/enemies/overcoat_contact.gd`) once the enemy touches the car at its level. It reads the owned tier from `PowerUpData` (`power_ups.json`) and calls `take_damage(contact_damage, "rusty_overcoat")`, so the red flash, death, drops and the recap work as for any damage. The per-enemy repeat is the `overcoat_cooldown` field (seconds, counted down in `contact_step`), set to the tier's `repeat_cooldown_seconds` after a hit. The kaiju is skipped unless the tier's `hits_kaiju` is true. The check runs before the car's invulnerability test, so the car being invulnerable does not stop it; the enemy's hit on the car is unchanged. Rules and proposals: Enemies, Content, "Rusty Overcoat". Tests: `tests/unit/enemies/test_rusty_overcoat.gd`.
