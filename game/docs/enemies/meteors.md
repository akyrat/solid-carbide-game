### The meteor attack (T-046)

Task T-046 (Enemy Behavior Agent): the kaiju's one special move, with T-039's numbers (`meteor_attack` in `data/enemies.json`, through `EnemyData.meteor_attack()`; the schedule matches `EnemyData.meteor_attack_times()`). What it does: `docs/enemies.md`, Content, "The kaiju's meteor attack". The warning circle, the meteor, the dust and the windup are placeholders drawn in code; the final look comes from separate art tasks.

**See it:** turn the playtest switch `kaiju_early_arrival` on in `data/playtest.json` (it is off, the game's rule), start the game and wait: the kaiju walks in 30 seconds into the run and its first attack comes 5 seconds later. Screenshot, from the repo root: `bash game/tools/screenshot.sh res://tests/fixtures/kaiju_meteors_demo.tscn screenshots/T-046_meteors.png 20` (the kaiju beside the car, three warning circles, one meteor falling, and two boulders just left by meteors with their dust, all held still; fixture script `tests/fixtures/kaiju_meteors_demo.gd`). A copy is in `art-archive/T-046/screenshots/`.

**How it is built:** `scripts/enemies/kaiju_meteors.gd`, a child `Meteors` of the boss (`run.kaiju_boss.meteors`), made and bound in `kaiju_boss.gd`'s `bind()` (the only change to the boss; `kaiju.gd` is unchanged). It moves in `step(dt)`, once per physics step from `_physics_process`, and its drawings animate in `_process`, so pausing the tree stops it; it does nothing once the run is over, and `run.world_rebuilt` (a new run) clears it. `enabled = false` stops new attacks (tests, fixtures); `rng` picks the landing spots (seed it for tests).

- **Clock:** `fight_time` counts the seconds the live kaiju has been on the map. The first attack starts at `first_attack_after_seconds`, then every `seconds_between_attacks`. `start_attack()` starts one now: `windup_seconds` of windup (`is_winding_up()`), then `drop_meteors()`.
- **Warnings:** `drop_meteors()` adds `meteors_per_attack` warnings (`add_warning(units)`), each at `pick_spot()`: uniform within `land_within_units_of_car` of the car, and `is_good_spot()`: the map's `can_place_meteor_boulder()` (T-097), no overlap with a warning still showing or a boulder still waiting, and off the kaiju's body. T-147: `is_good_spot()` also keeps `boulder_clearance_units` (`meteor_attack`, 1.5) of free ground between the circle and every boulder in `map.meteor_boulders` (standing or fading ones are there until `remove_meteor_boulder`), and between it and warnings and `pending_boulders`; the map's own boulders are covered by the placement check. Before, touching was allowed and the tall boulder sprite made a circle behind it look as if it fell on it. Test: `test_kaiju_meteors.gd` (many seeded attacks around a standing, a fading and a waiting boulder, a freed spot used again); screenshot fixture `tests/fixtures/meteors_avoid_boulders_demo.tscn`. `SPOT_TRIES` (40) tries per meteor; a meteor with no spot is left out. `warnings` lists `{units, time_left}`; each lands when `time_left` (from `warning_seconds`) reaches 0.
- **Landing:** `land(units)`: if the car's centre is within `impact_radius_units` and the car is not invulnerable after a hit (T-334; then the meteor does nothing to it), `RunState.damage_car(impact_damage)` (1 heart) and `car.apply_knockback(away from the spot, knockback)`. Then the dust, and the boulder through the map's `add_meteor_boulder(units)` if `can_place_meteor_boulder(units)` still accepts it. While the car's 1 x 1 body or the kaiju's body overlaps the boulder's circle, the boulder waits in `pending_boulders` and is placed on the first step the spot is clear.
- **Signals:** `attack_started()`, `warnings_shown(spots)`, `meteor_landed(units, hit_car)`, `boulder_placed(units, body)`.
- **Drawing:** the warning circles are the node `MeteorWarnings` in the map's `View`, right after `DriftMarks` (on the ground: under buildings, boulders, enemies and the car), drawn in screen pixels like the ground tiles. The windup rings, the falling meteor and the dust are the node `MeteorSky`, a child of the run with a z_index over everything. Colours: `WARNING_COLOR` hazard amber `Color(1, 0.62, 0.12)` (not the heart's red), a dark purple meteor with a magenta glow, warm grey dust, pale yellow windup.

**Not in the data:** the spot tries, the falling time (0.5 s) and height, the dust time (0.5 s), the rule that a spot avoids the kaiju and other meteors, and the rule that a boulder waits for the car or the kaiju to move off: rules of the code and the placeholder, not balancing numbers.

**Boulders fade (T-142).** `kaiju_meteors.gd` keeps each boulder it placed in `standing_boulders` and ages it in `step()` (so it stops while paused). Up to `boulder_lifetime_seconds` (10) it is untouched; then `map.set_meteor_boulder_alpha(body, a)` lowers its sprite's transparency in a straight line over `boulder_fade_seconds`, and at the end `map.remove_meteor_boulder(body)` takes it off the map: the body leaves the physics world (the car no longer bounces off it), the sprite is freed, and its entries in `meteor_boulders`, `meteor_boulder_bodies` and `all_boulders()` go, so the spot is free for a new boulder. Both functions only accept bodies from `add_meteor_boulder`, never the map's own boulders. Both numbers are in `data/enemies.json`, `meteor_attack`. The flat view's placeholder circles are not faded, they vanish at the end.

**Paired meteors (T-539).** What it does: `docs/enemies.md`, Content, "Paired meteors hurt the kaiju". In `kaiju_meteors.gd`; the placeholder drawings in `meteor_pair_fx.gd` (static functions, preloaded as `PairFx`, no `class_name`).

- **Picking:** `drop_meteors()` adds `meteors_per_attack / 2` pairs through `pick_pair()` (an odd one left over lands alone, as before). `pick_pair()` picks the first spot as `pick_spot()` does, then up to `PARTNER_TRIES` (16) partners `pair_distance_units` away in a random direction, also within `land_within_units_of_car` of the car; both pass `is_good_spot()`, and `gap_is_clear(a, b)` samples the line between the two boulders' edges every 0.5 units with the map's `is_open_spot(p, 0.5)` (the car's half width). `add_pair_warnings(a, b)` adds the two warnings and returns the pair.
- **The pair:** `pairs` holds `{a, b, landed, bodies, paid, dead}`; each warning carries its `pair` (empty for a lone meteor; `add_warning(units)` and `land(units)` still work without one). `land(units, pair)` counts the landing; `_place_waiting_boulders()` records the boulder body in `pair.bodies` and its `standing_boulders` entry carries the pair; a refused placement or a faded boulder sets `dead`. `is_pair_armed(pair)`: both landed, not paid, not dead.
- **Crossing:** `step()` calls `_check_pair_crossings()` after landing and ageing: if `car_is_drifting()` (`car.model.is_drifting` and not `airborne`, as the drift marks read it) and the car's move since the last step (`_last_car_units` to now) crosses an armed pair's segment `a`-`b` (`Geometry2D.segment_intersects_segment`), `strike_pair(pair)`: `kaiju.take_damage(pair_damage_to_kaiju)` (no source, so the recap's weapon kills are untouched; the red flash comes from `take_damage`), both boulders removed through `map.remove_meteor_boulder()` (and dropped from `standing_boulders` and `pending_boulders`), a break-up per boulder in `breakups`, a beam in `links` (`{from, to, kaiju, age}`), and `pair_struck(a, b, damage)`. Spent and dead pairs are dropped from `pairs`. `clear()` forgets pairs and effects.
- **Drawing:** the pair line is drawn by `MeteorWarnings` (on the ground, under the circles); the break-ups and the beam with the kaiju's burst by `MeteorSky`. Lengths in `meteor_pair_fx.gd`: `LINK_SECONDS` 0.7, `LINK_TRAVEL_SECONDS` 0.15, `KAIJU_BURST_SECONDS` 0.45, `BREAKUP_SECONDS` 0.55 (rules of the placeholder, like the dust's 0.5 s).
- **See it:** `bash game/tools/record_meteor_pair_gif.sh <out.gif> [seconds] [skip]` records `tests/fixtures/meteor_pair_strike_demo.tscn` (a pair lands, the car really drives and drifts through it with scripted input, the beam hits the kaiju standing to one side; `KEEP_FRAMES=<folder>` keeps the frames, `PAIR_SEED` changes the pair, `PAIR_DEBUG=1` prints the car's path). The GIF and a frame strip: `art-archive/T-539/screenshots/`.
- **Tests:** `tests/unit/enemies/test_meteor_pairs.gd`: whole pairs at the data's distance, near the car, with an open gap; a drift through a landed pair takes the data's damage and removes both boulders, spawning the beam, two break-ups and the kaiju's flash; no drift, no effect; a pair pays once; crossing the line beyond a boulder, before both landed, or after they faded does nothing; a new run clears it; the drawings run without errors.

**Tests:** `tests/unit/enemies/test_kaiju_meteors.gd`, part of the GUT suite: the attack hooked into the boss and the map's drawing; no attack without the kaiju; attacks on the data's schedule; the windup, then the data's number of warning circles near the car where the placement check accepts, shown for 2 seconds, and the meteors landing where they showed; a meteor on the car damaging it and knocking it away, one elsewhere doing nothing; one boulder per meteor through `add_meteor_boulder()`, none on a spot the check refuses; the boulder waiting until the car is off the spot; the car bouncing off a meteor boulder; everything stopping while paused; nothing landing after the run ends; a new run clearing it; the placeholders not the heart's red; (T-142) a boulder fully visible and solid just before 10 seconds, fading and then removed from the scene and the physics world, the map's own boulders staying, and both numbers read from the data. The tests set the playtest switch off and do not depend on it.

## Meteor effects: the comet tail and the impact (task T-070)

The kaiju's meteors (scripts/enemies/kaiju_meteors.gd) now stream a **comet tail** behind them while they fall (the last `FALL_SECONDS`, 0.5 s, of the warning) and play an **impact animation** where they land (it replaces the plain dust burst; the boulder still appears at landing, as in T-046/T-147, under the first flash frames). The meteor, the warning circle and the boulder are unchanged. Drawing is in `scripts/enemies/meteor_fx.gd` (`MeteorFx`), called by the attack's sky drawing; tail and impact are pure functions of flight time and impact age.

**The setting:** `data/meteor_fx.json`, key `version`, one of:
- `pixel`: sprite sheets drawn by script, `art/effects/meteor_tail_pixel.png` (6 frames, loops at 14 fps) and `meteor_impact_pixel.png` (8 frames over 0.5 s). No PixelLab. `python game/tools/build_meteor_fx.py build` redraws them (byte-identical).
- `pixellab`: sheets animated by PixelLab (`/animate-with-text-v3`, 2 calls) from first frames the script drew, `meteor_tail_pixellab.png` (8 frames) and `meteor_impact_pixellab.png` (8 frames). Built from the raw frames in `art-archive/T-070/pixellab/` by `build_meteor_fx.py from-pixellab` (background and half-transparent pixels cut, at most 30 colours).
- `particles` (default, the board's pick, 2026-10-21): no sprites; square pixel embers, ring, sparks, shards and dust computed each frame in `meteor_fx.gd` (impact 0.6 s).
- `off`: the earlier placeholder (no tail, the plain dust burst).

The environment variable `METEOR_FX` overrides the file (used for recordings). Sheet cells are 128 x 128 art pixels drawn at 0.75; palette is the boulders' violet and magenta, white-hot core, grey-violet dust, never the heart's red. `python game/tools/build_meteor_fx.py check` runs the sprite rules.

**Recording:** `bash game/tools/record_meteor_fx.sh <version> <out.gif> [seconds]` plays `tests/fixtures/meteor_fx_demo.tscn` (a run with meteors falling at once and every 1.5 s, three at a time, the car standing still; needs a window) and writes a GIF with `tools/frames_to_gif.py`. Recordings: `art-archive/T-070/screenshots/meteor_fx_<version>.gif`.

**Tests:** `tests/unit/enemies/test_meteor_fx.gd` (tail only while falling, the impact once and gone, each version draws, the setting, sheet sizes and no red) and `tools/test_build_meteor_fx.py`.
