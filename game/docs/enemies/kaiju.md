## The kaiju

Task T-045 (Enemy Behavior Agent): Crash City's final boss as Enemies, Decisions describes it, with T-039's numbers (`kaiju` and, T-119, `kaiju_coins` in `data/enemies.json` through `EnemyData`). What it does: `docs/enemies.md`, Content, "The kaiju". A placeholder for now (a large dark slate green Godzilla-style shape drawn in code); the final art comes from a separate art task. Its meteor attack (T-046): "The meteor attack" below.

**See it:** start the game (F5) and press Start, or open `scenes/run/run.tscn` and press F6. With the playtest switch on (below) the kaiju walks in from a screen edge 30 seconds into the run; the starting gun (and the flamethrower, with T-042's `flamethrower_at_start` on) can kill it, which wins the run. Screenshot, from the repo root: `bash game/tools/screenshot.sh res://tests/fixtures/kaiju_demo.tscn screenshots/kaiju.png 20` (the kaiju beside the car in the gravel square, with a few minions, in a run started from the main menu; fixture script `tests/fixtures/kaiju_demo.gd`). A copy is in `art-archive/T-045/screenshots/`.

**Playtest switch: early arrival.** The boss time is the run's 1:00 left (3:00 in the 4-minute run), so for playtests `data/playtest.json` (its other keys are other tasks' switches) can bring the kaiju's arrival forward:

```
"kaiju_early_arrival": { "enabled": true, "after_seconds": 30 }
```

With `enabled` true, the kaiju arrives `after_seconds` into the run instead of at the boss time. **To switch it back, set `"enabled": false`**: the kaiju then arrives at the boss time (7 minutes in, 1:00 left) as Enemies and Game loop architecture decide. A missing file or key is off too. Only the kaiju's arrival moves: the clock, the minions' growth and the `boss_time` signal stay as they are, and a later `boss_time` brings no second kaiju. It is on (30 s) for the board's playtest of T-045 and is set back to `false` right before T-045 merges (board, 2026-10-14). `KaijuBoss.read_early_arrival_seconds()` (in `kaiju_boss.gd`) reads it into `run.kaiju_boss.early_arrival_seconds` (-1 = off) when the run is made. Tests set the static `playtest_override` in `kaiju_boss.gd` (-1 = off, seconds = on, -2 = read the file), as T-042's tests do for the flamethrower.

**How it is built** (`scripts/enemies/`):

- `kaiju_boss.gd`: the run's kaiju. The run scene adds it as `run.kaiju_boss` (child `Kaiju`) and calls `bind(run)`. On the state's `boss_time` it makes the kaiju appear (`try_appear()`; once per run). It moves everything in `step(dt)`, once per physics step from `_physics_process` (pausable, so the pause screen and the level-up pause stop the kaiju and its damage), and does nothing once the run is over. On `run.world_rebuilt` (a new run) it removes the kaiju; it arrives again at that run's boss time. `enabled = false` stops it (tests).
- `kaiju.gd`: the kaiju, a `CharacterBody2D` in the run's `PhysicsWorld` (flat world pixels, 16 per unit) on layer 3 (`enemies`) with mask 1 (obstacles): a circle of `RADIUS_UNITS` (2.0, so 4 units across). It slides along walls, buildings and boulders and walks round one it meets head-on, as a minion does; it never pushes or blocks the car. In the groups `enemies` and `kaiju` (not `minions`).
- `kaiju_sprite.gd`: the kaiju's drawing in the isometric view, `HEIGHT_UNITS` (7) tall. **Final art (T-069):** 5 walk sheets in `art/kaiju/` (`kaiju_s/se/e/ne/n.png`, 6 frames of 224 x 224 side by side, 30 colours at most per sheet; south-west, west and north-west mirror south-east, east and north-east), drawn at scale 0.5 (so the 224-pixel canvas is the 7 units), the direction chosen from the way it walks (`compass_index`, as the minion's), the frame from the walk cycle (`frame_for`), feet on the ground point (`ART_FEET_Y`), the ground shadow kept. `drawn_height_px()` is the art's visible height (head top `ART_TOP_Y` to feet) so the health bar sits on its head; `drawn_width_px()` is unchanged. Without the sheets it draws the T-045 placeholder (left or right facing). T-612: the walk, windup and death sheets are named in `data/enemies.json`, `"kaiju_animations"` ("The kaiju's animations" below). The sheets: `python game/tools/build_kaiju_sheets.py` (free; from the 6 first frames of each `art-archive/T-069/walk_<dir>/`, one shared palette per sheet by `reduce_palette.py`), tests `tools/test_build_kaiju_sheets.py` and `tests/unit/enemies/test_kaiju_sprite.gd`. PixelLab record, review pictures and the one-round notes: `art-archive/T-069/`; the raw 8 rotations are there too. Screenshots and a GIF of it walking in a run (`tests/fixtures/kaiju_walk_demo.tscn`, `KAIJU_REC_DIR=<folder>` saves a picture every 4 frames): `art-archive/T-069/screenshots/`.

**Arrival:** at a random point on `arrival_ring()`, the visible screen (the camera's view) grown so that the whole drawing, its ground circle and its height, is `OFFSCREEN_MARGIN_UNITS` (1) beyond the screen's edge, inside the map and clear of buildings and boulders (`is_free_spot()`; 24 tries a step, else it tries again on the next step). `appear_at(units)` puts it at a given place (tests, fixtures). T-612: `spawn_now()` (God mode's "Call the kaiju", through `GodMode.spawn_boss()`) asks for the same arrival at once, with the clock untouched; once per run, false when the kaiju has arrived or is due (`can_spawn_now()`), so the boss time brings no second kaiju.

**Movement:** straight towards the car's centre at `move_speed` (3 units/s).

**Contact damage:** as a minion's: it touches the car when its circle overlaps the car's 1 x 1 unit body (`touches_car()`); while touching, it hits at once, then again only once the car's invulnerability after a hit (1 s, T-334) is over; during it no hit and no knockback: `RunState.damage_car(contact_damage)` (2 hearts; this also cuts the combo) and `car.apply_knockback(away from the kaiju, knockback)` (16 units/s).

**Damaging the kaiju (for the weapon tasks, T-041 and T-042):** exactly as a minion, so a weapon that finds minions finds the kaiju too: `take_damage(amount: float)` on the kaiju node, the same points as `health` (500). It can be damaged at any time, as soon as it has appeared (no shield, no vulnerability windows). Zero or negative amounts, and calls on a dead kaiju, are ignored. It is in the `enemies` group and its body on layer 3, so `get_tree().get_nodes_in_group("enemies")` and a projectile's `Area2D` with a mask of 4 find it; it has `position_units()`, `health`, `max_health` and `is_dead()` like a minion. It is much bigger than a minion, so it also has `hit_radius_units()`, its body on the ground as a radius in units (`RADIUS_UNITS`, 2.0, its collision circle; the minion's body is 0.5). The weapons read it ("Weapons", Targets): the gun's bullets hit when they come within their hit radius of its body, and the flamethrower counts any overlap of the flames with its body as a hit (board, 2026-10-14; minions keep the centre rule). Check `is_dead()` before damaging one found earlier in the same frame.

**Health, for the HUD:** `run.kaiju_boss.kaiju` is the live kaiju (null before it appears and after it dies). The boss relays everything a HUD needs, so it can connect once:

| Signal or function | Is |
| --- | --- |
| `kaiju_appeared(kaiju: Node)` | The kaiju appeared (just off-screen) |
| `kaiju_health_changed(health: float, max_health: float)` | Once when it appears (full health), then on every hit |
| `kaiju_defeated(coins: int)` | It died, with the coins it added |
| `kaiju_health()`, `kaiju_max_health()`, `has_appeared()` | Its health now (0 when there is none), its full health, and whether it has appeared this run |

The kaiju node itself sends `health_changed(health, max_health)` on every hit and `died(kaiju, position_units)` once.

**Death (T-612):** at 0 health the kaiju dies once: `died` fires, it leaves the groups `enemies` and `kaiju` at once and its body leaves the enemies' layer (deferred), so no weapon finds it; `is_dead()` is true and `run.kaiju_boss.kaiju` is null from that moment, so it no longer walks, hurts the car or draws meteor attacks, and the health bar goes on the next step (a proposal). The boss keeps it as `dying_kaiju` (`is_kaiju_dying()`) while its sprite plays the death for `EnemyData.kaiju_death_seconds()` (the death's frames / fps plus its end: hold, cracks, explosion; 4.4 s), drawn where it died in its depth order (a fall in progress still comes down); it also calls `RunState.kaiju_dying()`, which makes the win stand: the clock holds at 0:00 instead of running out, a wreck waits at its end instead of losing, and a kill on the very step the clock ran out calls the time's up off (all proposals; Game loop architecture, Content). The kill counts in the recap at once. When the death has played the boss frees the kaiju, adds its coins, `EnemyData.kaiju_coins_on_defeat()` (10), through `RunState.add_coins()`, sends `kaiju_defeated`, and only then calls `RunState.kaiju_killed()`, which ends the run as a win; so `run_ended("win", coins)` carries the kaiju's coins and they are saved with the run. A new run during the death removes it.

**Draw order:** the kaiju's drawing is in the minions' draw node (`run.minions.draw_root`, the Y-sorted `Enemies` node just before the car's `View`), placed every physics step on its body in the isometric view with a `z_index` from `enemy_depth.gd` (the map's rule for the car, as for the minions, with the kaiju's size).

**Not in the data:** the kaiju's size (4 units across, 7 tall) and the off-screen margin are in the code: sizes and rules of the placeholder, not balancing numbers.

**Tests:** `tests/unit/enemies/test_kaiju.gd`, part of the GUT suite: no kaiju before boss time; boss time bringing it just off-screen, clear of obstacles, walking towards the car at the data's speed, once per run; walking round a building; weapon damage through `take_damage()` lowering its health as soon as it appears, with both health signals; a projectile-style physics query on the enemies mask finding it; damage to 0 adding its coins before the run ends as a win, `kaiju_killed()` called once; contact damage at the data's rate and knockback away from it; no damage without touching; standing still while paused and after the run ends; a new run removing it and boss time bringing it back; the collision layers; the draw order; the placeholder's size and clear of the heart's red; `hit_radius_units()` matching its body; the starting gun hitting it, the flamethrower burning it when only its body overlaps the flames, and the gun killing it winning the run with its coins; the playtest switch (arrival at its time, once, again in a new run), reading its key beside other keys, and the committed file keeping both switches.

**The kaiju's health bar (task T-050):** `scripts/hud/kaiju_health_bar.gd` (`KaijuHealthBar`), `run.kaiju_health_bar`, a child of the world view like the trick popups. `bind(boss)` follows `kaiju_appeared` (shows), `kaiju_health_changed` (`fraction`, 0 to 1) and `kaiju_defeated` (hides); each physics step (priority after the boss's) it sits `GAP` above the kaiju sprite's head and takes its width from the sprite's `drawn_width_px()` and `drawn_height_px()`, so the final kaiju art only has to provide those two functions (the placeholder's width is its ground circle, 90 px). It hides when there is no kaiju (a new run). Neon purple `FILL_COLOR` on white, black outline (`OUTLINE` 2 px, stepped corners), a black 1 px divider at each of `DIVISIONS` (10) tenths, `BAR_HEIGHT` 8 px, whole pixels, no text. Absolute depth `CANVAS_ITEM_Z_MAX`, so buildings never cover it. Change to the kaiju's files: only the static `drawn_width_px()` in `kaiju_sprite.gd`. Test: `tests/unit/hud/test_kaiju_health_bar.gd`. Screenshot (copy in `art-archive/T-050/screenshots/`): `bash game/tools/screenshot.sh res://tests/fixtures/kaiju_bar_demo.tscn screenshots/kaiju_bar.png 20`.

## The kaiju's animations (task T-612)

What it does: `docs/enemies.md`, Content, "The kaiju" (Look, Death) and "The kaiju's meteor attack" (Windup); the decision: Enemies, Decisions, "The kaiju is animated" (board, 2026-11-09). Three animations, each one sheet per drawn direction, all named in `data/enemies.json`, `"kaiju_animations"` (read by `EnemyData.kaiju_animations()`), so new frames go in by replacing files or changing that section, with no code change:

| Animation | Plays | Sheet (now) | frames | fps | loop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| walk | always, while it walks | `res://art/kaiju/kaiju_%s.png` (T-069's art) | 6 | 5.4 (its old 0.9 cycles a second) | yes |
| windup | the meteor attack's 1 s windup (`meteors.is_winding_up()`), from its first frame; holds its last frame | `res://art/placeholders/kaiju/kaiju_windup_%s.png` (placeholder) | 6 | 6 (1 s) | no |
| death | once, from the kill; it buckles, kneels and falls flat, then holds its last frame for the death's end (below) | `res://art/placeholders/kaiju/kaiju_death_%s.png` (placeholder) | 16 | 8 (2 s) | no |

All counts and speeds are proposals for the playtest.

**The death's end (quick tuning 1, the board 2026-11-09; Enemies, Decisions, "The kaiju's death is longer and more dramatic"):** `"death_end"` in the same section, on the death's last frame, drawn in code by `scripts/enemies/kaiju_death_fx.gd` (no frames needed from the art task): `hold_seconds` (0.4) lying still; `crack_seconds` (1.0) of neon green cracks spreading over the body's own pixels from scattered points while a green glow fills its silhouette until it is all green; `explosion_seconds` (1.0) of an explosion `explosion_radius_px` (150 screen pixels) across at its widest, from the lying body's middle (its silhouette flashes white first). The phases: `KaijuSprite.death_phase_at(seconds)` gives "fall", "hold", "crack", "explode", then "gone", with the progress in each; the whole death is `death_length()` = frames / fps + hold + crack + explosion (4.4 s), which `EnemyData.kaiju_death_seconds()` gives the boss, so the coins and the win wait for the explosion's end. Colours: the cracks are the minions' spill greens (140, 224, 26) and (204, 250, 89) with a near-white green core (232, 255, 176); the explosion is a white flash (235, 255, 255), the kaiju's plate cyan (140, 235, 235), the HUD's kaiju purple (184, 46, 255), the meteors' magenta (217, 77, 230) and navy (51, 36, 82), and its hide (51, 77, 71); every one is over 40 (RGB, 0 to 255) from Greenbull's three colours (rule 6; tested). No camera shake (the game has none) and no damage. The cracks follow whatever the last death frame looks like, so T-613's frames get them unchanged.

**The frame format (for the final art, T-613):**

- **Files:** one PNG per animation and drawn direction, the sheet path with `%s` replaced by `e`, `se`, `s`, `ne` or `n` (east, south-east, south, north-east, north on screen). South-west, west and north-west are the game's mirror images of south-east, east and north-east, so they need no files. Final frames may go anywhere under `res://art/` (for example `res://art/kaiju/kaiju_windup_%s.png`); the section's `sheet` then names the new path.
- **Sheet:** the frames in one row, left to right, each `frame_size` x `frame_size` pixels (224), no gaps, transparent background; the sheet is as tall as one frame. A sheet may hold more frames than `frames` (the extra ones are not played) but not fewer (the game then plays only as many as it holds).
- **Size and anchor:** frames are drawn at half size (a 224 frame is the kaiju's 7 units, 112 screen pixels). The feet stand at `feet` (x 112, y 203 in the frame) in every frame and every direction, so the kaiju does not slide; `head_top_y` (17) is where the top of the head is when it stands, for the health bar above it. Mirrored directions are flipped about the feet.
- **Counts and speed:** `frames` (how many play) and `fps` (frames a second) per animation; `loop` true only for the walk. The death's last frame is the kaiju lying on the ground (it is held for the cracks; the proposal for the final art: a heavy topple onto its side with the tail settling), and the coins and the win wait for `frames / fps` plus the death's end.
- **Shadow:** the ground shadow is drawn by the game, not in the frames; it stays while the kaiju lies there and fades out with the explosion.
- **Missing files:** a missing windup or death sheet falls back to the walk frames; a missing walk sheet to the code-drawn T-045 placeholder (which simply goes at the death).

**How it is built:** `kaiju_sprite.gd` reads the section once (`config()`; tests set `config_override`). `current_frame()` says what is drawn now: the animation (death, else the fall when it has T-227 frames, else the windup, else the walk), its texture for the facing direction, the frame and whether it is mirrored. The frame clocks (`walk_seconds`, `windup_seconds`, `death_seconds`) move on in `_process`, so they stop while the game is paused. The boss sets the windup from the meteor attack each step (`set_winding_up()`, in `_place_sprite()`), and `kaiju.gd` starts the death (`set_dying()`) at the kill. The red flash (`modulate`), the depth order (`z_index`), the height drawing and the ground shadow are the sprite's as before, so they work on every animation. The meteor attack's placeholder yellow rings above the head are gone (`kaiju_meteors.gd`).

**Placeholder frames:** `python game/tools/build_kaiju_anim_placeholders.py` (free, no PixelLab) makes them from the first frame of each walk sheet, nearest neighbour only, no new colours: the windup stretches the kaiju taller and narrower about its feet (rearing up, peak by the fifth frame, held); the death squashes it for four frames (buckling), sinks it to its knees for four, then, quick tuning 1, leans it forward while it flattens onto the ground for eight (a heavy slump that overshoots a little and settles; a side view turned a full 90 degrees read as a nose-dive), lying on the ground line in the last frame. Test: `tools/test_build_kaiju_anim_placeholders.py`.

**Final death art for east and the explosion (T-613, a sample):** `"death"` has an optional `"sheets"` (a drawn direction's own file; the other directions use `"sheet"`), set for east to `res://art/kaiju/kaiju_death_e.png` (west mirrors it); the sheet holds the data's 16 frames, its last four repeating the lying frame. `"explosion"` (`sheet`, `frames` 6, `scale` 1.0) is one PixelLab sheet drawn centred on the lying body in the explosion phase for every direction, frame by the phase's progress (`explosion_frame_at`), replacing the code-drawn explosion, which is still drawn when the sheet is missing; `explosion_radius_px` is 112 (the art at scale 1 is 224 across). Facing se, s, ne or n (and their mirrors sw, nw) the death is still the placeholder fall, then the same cracks and the new explosion.

**Tests:** `tests/unit/enemies/test_kaiju_animations.gd`: the data's three animations, sheets and counts, and this format written here; the walk advancing through the facing direction's frames and looping, standing still while paused; the windup during the meteor windup from its first frame, held, the walk again after it, and the rings gone; the death for the data's length, once, with the kaiju still, no contact damage, out of the group and the enemies' layer, then gone; the shadow staying while it lies there and fading with the explosion; (quick tuning 1) the phases fall, hold, crack, explode, gone in that order with the data's lengths, the kaiju lying on the ground in the last death frame, the cracks on its body and the green growing until it has consumed it, the greens and the explosion's colours far from Greenbull's, every phase drawing; the coins and the win only after the explosion; the timer reaching 0:00 during the death, a kill on the step the clock ran out and a wreck during the death all keeping the win; another file and other counts in the data changing what is drawn. The older kaiju tests (`test_kaiju.gd`, `test_kaiju_health_bar.gd`) play the death through before checking the win. Recordings: `art-archive/T-612/screenshots/` (fixture `tests/fixtures/kaiju_anim_demo.tscn`).
