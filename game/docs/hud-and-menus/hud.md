**Likes bar spring (task T-273):** `scripts/hud/likes_spring.gd` (`LikesSpring`, `hud.likes_spring`, a pausable child of the Hud) moves `shown`, the bar's fill as a share (0 to 1), to a new level in `DURATION` (0.25 s) on an ease-out-back (`ease_back()`, `OVERSHOOT` 1.2, about a 5 percent overshoot); these two constants are the only timing. The Hud's `_on_xp_changed()` decides: likes going up call `spring_to()`, a level-up calls `fill_then()` (the old bar springs to full, the level label and `level_up_flash` change at once, then `restarted` fires, the bar's `max_value` becomes the new level's and the fill springs from empty to the leftover), a drop (the likes draining) or a new run `snap()`s. `_show_likes()` writes `likes_bar.value = clamp(shown, 0, 1) * max_value`, so the bar never draws past full; `likes_bar.value` is therefore the shown fill, and tests call `hud.settle_likes()` to jump to the end before reading the real one. Tests: `tests/unit/hud/test_likes_spring.gd`. Screenshots and the GIF: `bash game/tools/record_likes_spring.sh` (fixture `tests/fixtures/likes_spring_demo.tscn`, `LIKES_SPRING_MODE` plain, burning or gif; `tools/likes_spring_gif.py`), saved in `art-archive/T-273/screenshots/`.

**Level-up animation (task T-139):** `scripts/hud/level_up_flash.gd` (`LevelUpFlash`, `hud.level_up_flash`), a Control drawn in code that the Hud places over the likes bar and the 24 px above it (`MAX_UP`), under the texts. The Hud connects the run state's `level_up` signal (in `bind()`) to `_on_level_up()`, which calls `start()`; nothing else starts it, and it never touches the bar's value or the label's text. It is `PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS`, so it plays the same whether the level-up paused the run or not (it only processes while `playing`). `advance(delta)` (called by `_process`, and by tests) moves it on in `ComboHeat.FLICKER_FRAME` frames; it lasts `FRAME_COUNT` (9) frames, `length()` 0.75 s, then `stop()` hides it and restores the label. Per frame it draws the bar overlay (`OVERLAY`, from `overlay_rects()`: a sweep, a full cover, stripes) and the sparks (`spark_rects()`, 16 square pixels of `LikesFire.PALETTE`, deterministic from a hash), and sets "Hype Lv" scale (`LABEL_SCALE`, pivot at its bottom right, set in `_layout_bottom()`) and colour (white-hot, yellow, normal). A new level-up restarts it; `bind()` stops it. Tests: `tests/unit/hud/test_hud_level_up_flash.gd` (start and end, bar and texts as before, paused and running, other bar changes ignored, restart, clear of the heart's red, clear of the combo counter and inside the bar at the four window sizes); `test_hud_layout.gd` stops the animation before measuring, since the label's rectangle grows during the pop. ## Airtime counter (HUD)

Task T-243 (HUD and menus, Decisions, "Airtime counter"). `scripts/hud/airtime_counter.gd` (`AirtimeCounter`, a child "AirtimeCounter" of the `Hud`): a wing icon (placeholder drawn in code, white with a black outline, never the heart's red) and the airtime as a two-decimal number in Bangers (`0.00`, seconds, no unit). `Hud.bind_car(car)` (called by `run.gd` for each new car) follows the car's `stepped` signal and feeds `AirtimeCounter.step(car.airborne, car.model.dt)`: it shows from the first airborne step, adds one physics step each step in the air (the same airtime the airtime trick pays out for) and hides and resets on landing. The HUD places it at `Hud.AIRTIME_OFFSET` (70, -10) from the screen's centre: right of the car and a little below where the combo popups pop out. Size, place and icon are proposals for the playtest.

- **Tests:** `tests/unit/hud/test_hud_airtime_counter.gd`.

## Drift counter (HUD)

Task T-247 (HUD and menus, Decisions, "Drift counter"). `scripts/hud/drift_counter.gd` (`DriftCounter`, a child "DriftCounter" of the `Hud`) extends `AirtimeCounter` and reuses its look and code (white Bangers number with a black outline, two decimals, seconds, no unit); only the icon differs: two curving skid marks drawn in code (`_make_icon()`, white with a black outline, never the heart's red). `Hud._on_car_stepped` feeds `step(car.model.is_drifting, car.model.dt)` once per physics step: it shows from the first drifting step, adds one step per step drifting and hides and resets when the drift ends (the moment it pays out, T-246). `Hud.bind_car` resets it with the airtime counter. Place: `Hud.DRIFT_OFFSET` (70, 30) from the screen's centre, directly under the airtime counter at the same x, so both fit at once. Place and icon are proposals for the playtest. Note: there is no drifting in the air (T-219), so in play the drift counter ends as the car leaves an edge and the airtime counter starts; the two can overlap only on the step boundary. The screenshot with both is staged.

- **Tests:** `tests/unit/hud/test_hud_drift_counter.gd`.
- **Screenshot and GIF:** `tests/fixtures/drift_counter_gif.tscn` (`DRIFT_PNG` and `DRIFT_BOTH_PNG` save screenshots), recorded with `--write-movie` and `tools/frames_to_gif.py`; results in `art-archive/T-247/screenshots/`.

## Live viewers counter (HUD)

Task T-336 (what it shows: `docs/hud-and-menus.md`, Content, "Live viewers counter"). `scripts/hud/viewers_counter.gd` (`ViewersCounter`, `hud.viewers_counter`, a pausable Control) is built by `Hud._build_viewers()` and anchored to the bottom-right corner from `data/hud.json` (`viewers_counter.layout`: x896 y560 w240 h72 at 1152 x 648, so 16 px from the right and bottom edges at any window size). Children, in drawing order: `panel` (`art/hud/viewers_panel.png` at 2x), `flash_rect` (the bump's and the crash's flash over the panel's screen), `fire` (`art/hud/viewers_fire_00.png` ... `_11.png` at 2x, placed so the frame's panel at (8, 24) sits on the counter; shown at step 4, a frame per `ComboHeat.FLICKER_FRAME`), `badge` (`art/hud/live_badge.png`, `tooltip_text` = `Hud.TEXT_LIVE_BADGE`), `eye` (`art/hud/eye_icon.png`), `number_label` and `unit_label` (`Hud.TEXT_VIEWERS_UNIT`), Bangers with smoothing off, right-aligned in fixed boxes right of the badge (`_layout_text()`). The Hud feeds it: `_on_combo_changed()` calls `set_multiplier()` (it keeps `previous_multiplier`), `bind()` calls `reset(state.combo)` (the number goes straight to its value, nothing in flight), and the run state's `combo_broken` calls `crash(last_loss())` (`ViewersData.crash_loss` from the last multiplier change: the cut comes right before the signal). Each frame `advance(dt)` moves `shown` toward `count_target()` with `ViewersData.count_toward`, draws `displayed` = `with_wobble(shown, time)` in `ViewersData.format`, and runs the bump, the flash and the fire. `count_target()` is `target()` (`viewers_for(multiplier)`) less `pending`, the viewers of trick popups whose amount has not landed yet. `step` is `ViewersData.step_for_viewers(count_target())` (0 base, 1 to 3 heat, 4 fire); `_apply_step()` takes the font size from `viewers_counter.number_sizes` and the colour, glow and pulse from `ComboHeat.STEPS` (the base step's colour is the viewers colour, `viewers_counter.color`); `number_scale()` is the heat pulse plus the bump. **Flights:** `hud.bind_popups(run.trick_popups)` (called by the run after `bind()`) sets `TrickPopups.fly_amounts` and connects two signals: `viewers_popup(popup)` (sent for each run trick popup with a viewers amount: the counter `hold()`s its `viewers_gain`, and `release()`s it if the popup leaves before its amount did) and `amount_detached(popup, screen_at)` (sent `TrickPopups.DETACH_AT` 0.3 s after the popup shows, when the popup sets `amount_gone` and redraws without the amount; `screen_at` is the amount's centre in canvas pixels, `PopupText.amount_screen_center()`). The counter's `fly_in(text, gain, from)` adds a `ViewersFlight` (`scripts/hud/viewers_flight.gd`, a top-level Control: the amount in Bangers 30 in the viewers colour and the eye at 2x, on a quadratic arc `flight_arc` 90 px high over `flight_seconds` 0.55, eased in, shrinking to 0.85); on `arrived` the gain is released, `bump()` runs (`bump_seconds` 0.25, `bump_scale` 0.18, a cyan flash) and `landed(gain)` is sent. **Crashes:** `crash(lost)` drops all holds, flashes `crash_color` (#ff5010, the combo broken popup's) for `crash_flash_seconds` 0.45 (the number in that colour too, `is_flashing_red()`), and adds a `ViewersDrop` (`scripts/hud/viewers_drop.gd`, a Label: `TrickPopups.LOST_TEXT` with `format(lost)`, Bangers 24, falling `drop_px` 70 with gravity and fading over `drop_seconds` 0.9), drawn under the number so the number stays readable. **The popups' amount** (`scripts/hud/trick_popups.gd`, `trick_popup_art.gd`): see `popups.md`, "Viewers on the trick popups". The gift arrows keep clear of the counter (`GiftArrows.hud_rects()`). Tests: `tests/unit/hud/test_hud_viewers_counter.gd` (the combo counter gone; place and size at 1152 x 648 and the bottom-right anchor at three other sizes; badge, eye, panel and unit; the viewers colour nowhere else; counting toward the target, never still, standing still while paused; the steps and the fire at 1,000,000; no heart red; the popup's "+{viewers}" pixels and eye; the detach, the flight's end on the number, the bump; popups pushed out early; the drift prototype; the crash's red flash and drop; no drop when the combo drains; a new run; no literal text), plus the T-336 updates in `test_hud_combo_heat.gd`, `test_hud_layout.gd`, `test_hud_level_up_flash.gd`, `test_likes_spring.gd`, `test_hud_positions_t086.gd`, `tests/unit/run/test_gift_drops.gd` and the trick text tests. Screenshots and GIFs: `bash game/tools/record_viewers_counter.sh [out_dir]` (fixture `tests/fixtures/viewers_counter_demo.tscn`, modes `stills`, `trick`, `crash`; `tools/viewers_counter_gif.py` crops the frames from the car to the counter); copies in `art-archive/T-336/screenshots/`.
