## Airtime counter (HUD)

Task T-243 (HUD and menus, Decisions, "Airtime counter"). `scripts/hud/airtime_counter.gd` (`AirtimeCounter`, a child "AirtimeCounter" of the `Hud`): a wing icon (placeholder drawn in code, white with a black outline, never the heart's red) and the airtime as a two-decimal number in Bangers (`0.00`, seconds, no unit). `Hud.bind_car(car)` (called by `run.gd` for each new car) follows the car's `stepped` signal and feeds `AirtimeCounter.step(car.airborne, car.model.dt)`: it shows from the first airborne step, adds one physics step each step in the air (the same airtime the airtime trick pays out for) and hides and resets on landing. ## Reaction icons (HUD)

Task T-351 (what they do: `docs/hud-and-menus.md`, Content, "Reaction icons"). `scripts/hud/reactions.gd` (`Reactions`, `hud.reactions`, a full-screen pausable Control that ignores the mouse) is the Hud's first child (`_build_reactions()`), so every other HUD piece draws over it. It holds `items` (a dictionary per icon: kind, colour, centre `pos`, `vel`, `age`, `life`, sway `phase`, `floater`) and draws them in `_draw()` from two pixel grids (`HEART`, `THUMB`, `icon_scale` 3 pixels per cell) in `COLORS` (pink, orange, violet, white: none the viewers cyan, the heart red or Greenbull's) at `alpha_of()` (fade in over `fade_in`, hold, fade out over the last half, times `alpha` 0.7). The art is a placeholder; T-352 swaps in the final icons (only `_draw()` and the grids change). `advance(dt)` (the `_process`, and for tests) asks `step_source` (the Hud gives it `viewers_counter.step`) for the look step, takes the rate from `rate_for_step()` (`rates_per_second`, 0 below the first heat step) and, when `next_in` runs out, `spawn_group()`s 1 to 3 floaters below the bottom edge in the left or right `lane_edge` to `lane_inner` share of the width, then sets the next gap to 1 / rate times a random `gap_min_share` to `gap_max_share`. A floater climbs at `rise_min` to `rise_max` with a `wobble_px` sway. `burst(origin)` (the Hud calls it from `_on_level_up()` with the sub goal square's centre, so once per goal) makes `burst_count` icons with random upward velocities that slow by `burst_drag`; older icons make room so the total never passes `cap` (12). `clear()` is called by `Hud.bind()` (a new run). `rng` is a `RandomNumberGenerator` (tests seed it); `area_override` sets the screen size in tests.
