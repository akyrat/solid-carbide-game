# Livestream HUD art (T-339)


The pieces of the livestream HUD (HUD and menus, Decisions, "The HUD looks like a livestream"; what they look like: Visual style, Decisions, "The livestream HUD art") are in `art/hud/`, each PNG with a json record beside it (how it was made, its style loop version and score, the PixelLab candidates made and not kept). They are drawn at 2x: show them at scale 2 with nearest filtering, like the likes heart. None is in a scene yet; the HUD tickets (T-335 to T-338) use them.

- `clock_panel.png` 100 x 36, `viewers_panel.png` 120 x 36, `subgoal_frame.png` 64 x 64 (its black inside is (4, 4) to (59, 59), with rounded inner corners: draw the green fill and the gift pack there), `live_badge.png` 34 x 13, `eye_icon.png` 16 x 11, `subs_icon.png` 22 x 22, `heart_full.png` and `heart_empty.png` 18 x 16.
- `clock_digits.png`: one row of 12 frames of 16 x 26 (0 to 9, the colon, an unlit 8), greyscale so the clock tints them with `modulate`; draw `clock_digit_off` (dim) behind each digit for an LCD's unlit segments. Each frame is also its own PNG, `clock_digit_0.png` ... `clock_digit_9.png`, `clock_digit_colon.png`, `clock_digit_off.png`.
- `viewers_fire.png`: one row of 12 frames of 136 x 64, a one second loop at 12 frames a second; the viewers panel sits at (8, 24) in each frame and is left clear (two rows lick its top edge), so the fire can be drawn behind or in front of the panel. Each frame is also its own PNG, `viewers_fire_00.png` ... `viewers_fire_11.png`.

**Built by** `game/tools/build_hud_livestream_art.py` (free, no PixelLab): one command per piece (`clock-panel`, `viewers-panel`, `subgoal-frame`, `digits`, `live-badge`, `fire`, `heart`, `heart-empty --from`, `eye`, `subs`), each writing one PNG and never overwriting one; `post` makes a PixelLab raw game-ready with no new pixels (alpha to 0/255, a flat background removed, cropped and centred, never scaled, an optional HUD outline, at most 30 colours); `request` writes a pixflux request body (with an optional init image); `split` cuts a sheet into frames; `contact` makes the enlarged dark and light contact sheet. The style loop's later fire versions are kept in the script as `fire_frame_v4`. `game/tools/write_hud_livestream_records.py` writes every record from the task's Style review and the generation log. Tests: `game/tools/test_build_hud_livestream_art.py` (each piece passes the script rules at its size, the fire loops and leaves the panel clear, the digits are greyscale, the empty heart keeps the full heart's outline, `post` never scales, and the script redraws every kept file in `art/hud/` pixel for pixel) and `test_write_hud_livestream_records.py`. Contact sheet: `art-archive/T-339/contact_sheet.png`; a mock-up and a fire GIF: `art-archive/T-339/screenshots/`.

## The HUD style sheet (T-430)

The sheet is in Visual style, Decisions, "The HUD style sheet". `game/tools/hud_sheet_check.py` checks every piece in `art/hud/` against it (outline colour, corner radius 2, no Greenbull colours; tests in `tools/test_hud_sheet_check.py`). The one piece that broke it, the sub goal frame (corner radius 3), was redrawn by script with radius 2 (`subgoal_frame(size, radius=PANEL_RADIUS)` in `build_hud_livestream_art.py`; the T-339 version is in `art-archive/T-430/before/`). `likes_heart.png` (T-086, 11 x 10) is no longer loaded by the game (only by its tests); `heart_full.png` and `heart_empty.png` are the hearts.

## Reaction icons (T-352)

Six 16 x 16 icons in `art/hud/` (`reaction_heart`, `reaction_thumbs_up`, `reaction_fire`, `reaction_clapping`, `reaction_star`, `reaction_smile`, png plus json), hand-placed grids drawn by `tools/build_hud_reaction_icons.py` (`build --out-dir DIR [--force]`, `contact --out SHEET`), tests in `tools/test_build_hud_reaction_icons.py`. `scripts/hud/reactions.gd` preloads them (`ICONS`) and draws them at `icon_scale` 2.
