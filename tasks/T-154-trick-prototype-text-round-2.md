## Description

After playing the trick prototype, the board asked for more (Drifting, Decisions, 2026-10-17). Each new piece of text shows on the trick confirmation card (T-152's comic card, Bangers font, capitals only), like SIDE FLIP! (T-150). Write the final text for:

1. **The left side flip** (Up, Left, Down, Right): the mirror of SIDE FLIP!, rolling to the car's left. It should read as a pair with SIDE FLIP! (you may also propose renaming the right one, for example to name both sides, but then give both). At most 14 characters each.
2. **The nose launch** (Up, Down, Up, Up): the car dips onto its nose, then launches forward into the air, its nose flipping up as it flies, and lands, like pressing down on the back of a toy car and letting it spring forward. At most 14 characters.
3. **The jump-over bonus:** shown when the car clears an obstacle or wall during any trick's jump. It counts as a trick of its own. A short shout, at most 16 characters.

Write the final text in the result notes, one line per item, with a one-line reason each.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The result notes give final text for items 1, 2 and 3, in capitals, within the character limits. (QA: pass)

## Result notes
1. Left side flip: **LEFT FLIP!** (10 characters). SIDE FLIP! beside LEFT FLIP! would not read as a pair, so rename the right one too: **RIGHT FLIP!** (11 characters) and **LEFT FLIP!** (10). Both plain, same shout style, and the pair is obvious at a glance. (If the board would rather not rename, the fallback is SIDE FLIP! for the right and LEFT FLIP! for the left.)
2. Nose launch: **NOSE POP!** (9 characters). Short and toy-car playful: the nose dips, springs, and pops up.
3. Jump-over bonus: **CLEARED IT!** (11 characters). A cocky shout that fits a stunt driver hopping a wall for the cameras, with no reference to what was jumped (it can be a wall or an obstacle).
